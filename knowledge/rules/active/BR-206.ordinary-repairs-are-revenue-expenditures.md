# Ordinary Repairs Are Revenue Expenditures

## Statement

Costs related to ordinary maintenance and repairs of a fixed asset are recorded as Repairs and Maintenance Expense of the current period.

## Rationale

Ordinary repairs maintain the asset's existing service capacity and benefit only the current period.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A repair maintains existing service capacity without extending useful life
- **THEN** Debit Repairs and Maintenance Expense
- **OTHERWISE** If the repair extends useful life, treat as extraordinary repair (capital expenditure)
