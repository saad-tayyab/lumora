# Flexible Budget Adjustment

## Statement

A flexible budget adjusts variable costs for the actual level of activity while keeping fixed costs constant.

## Rationale

Provides a more accurate benchmark for evaluating performance at the actual activity level.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Actual activity differs from budgeted activity
- **THEN** Adjust the budget by recalculating variable costs at actual activity level
- **OTHERWISE** Comparison to static budget may be misleading
