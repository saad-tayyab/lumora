# Direct Method Cash Flow Components

## Statement

Under the direct method, cash flows from operating activities must report at minimum: cash received from customers, cash paid for merchandise, cash paid for operating expenses, cash paid for interest, and cash paid for income taxes.

## Rationale

These categories provide users with a clearer picture of the sources and uses of operating cash than the indirect method.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the statement of cash flows using the direct method
- **THEN** Report each major class of gross cash receipts and payments
- **OTHERWISE** The statement is not compliant with GAAP requirements for the direct method
