# Adjustments for Depreciation at Period End

## Statement

Depreciation must be recorded as an adjusting entry at the end of each accounting period for all depreciable fixed assets.

## Rationale

Allocates the cost of the asset over its useful life in accordance with the matching principle.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** End of accounting period with depreciable assets
- **THEN** Record depreciation expense and accumulated depreciation
- **OTHERWISE** Expenses will be understated and assets overstated
