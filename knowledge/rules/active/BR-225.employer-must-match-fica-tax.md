# Employer Must Match FICA Tax

## Statement

Employers must match the employee's FICA tax contribution (Social Security 6.2% + Medicare 1.45%). This matching amount is recorded as Payroll Tax Expense.

## Rationale

FICA requires equal contributions from both employee and employer to fund Social Security and Medicare programs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording payroll for any employee
- **THEN** Employer must record matching FICA tax as Payroll Tax Expense
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
