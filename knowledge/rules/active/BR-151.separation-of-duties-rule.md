# Separation of Duties Rule

## Statement

The responsibilities for operations, custody of assets, and accounting should be separated. Employees who handle cash receipts should not record cash receipts in the accounting records.

## Rationale

Prevents employees from stealing assets and hiding theft in the accounting records. Also prevents operating managers from distorting reports.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Designing internal control procedures
- **THEN** Separate operations, custody of assets, and accounting responsibilities among different employees
- **OTHERWISE** Risk of fraud and errors increases significantly
