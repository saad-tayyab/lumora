# Internal Control Disclosure Requirement

## Statement

Material internal control failures must be disclosed to external auditors and included in the Management's Report on Internal Control.

## Rationale

Concealing internal control failures from auditors is unethical and potentially illegal, misleading investors about the reliability of financial statements.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An internal control failure is identified that is material
- **THEN** Report to external auditors and disclose in annual report
- **OTHERWISE** Violates ethical standards and securities regulations
