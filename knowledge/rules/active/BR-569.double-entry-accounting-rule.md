# Double-Entry Accounting Rule

## Statement

Every transaction must be recorded so that debits equal credits, maintaining the accounting equation (Assets = Liabilities + Equity).

## Rationale

Double-entry system ensures mathematical accuracy and the fundamental accounting equation remains in balance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording any business transaction
- **THEN** Debit accounts increased and credit accounts decreased (or vice versa) with equal totals
- **OTHERWISE** The trial balance will not balance, indicating an accounting error
