# Bond Discount Amortization Increases Interest Expense

## Statement

Amortization of bond discount increases interest expense above the cash interest payment, reflecting the additional cost of borrowing at a discount.

## Rationale

The discount represents additional interest cost that must be recognized over the bond's life, so amortization increases period interest expense.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Bonds were issued at a discount
- **THEN** Interest expense equals cash payment plus discount amortization
- **OTHERWISE** Interest expense equals cash payment minus premium amortization
