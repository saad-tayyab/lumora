# Gross Pay Less Deductions Equals Net Pay

## Statement

Net pay (take-home pay) equals gross pay minus all deductions including federal/state income taxes, FICA taxes, retirement contributions, and other authorized withholdings.

## Rationale

Employees receive net pay after all legally required and voluntary deductions are withheld.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing employee payroll
- **THEN** Net Pay = Gross Pay - Total Deductions
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
