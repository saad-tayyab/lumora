# Unequal Lives Equalization Rule

## Statement

When comparing proposals with unequal useful lives, equalize the analysis period by estimating the residual (selling) value of the longer-lived asset at the end of the shorter life.

## Rationale

NPV is only comparable when proposals are evaluated over the same time horizon; equalizing lives makes the comparison valid.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing two or more capital investment proposals with different useful lives
- **THEN** Assume the longer-lived asset is sold at the end of the shorter life; include estimated selling price as a cash inflow at that point
- **OTHERWISE** Direct NPV comparison is valid when lives are equal
