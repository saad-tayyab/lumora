# Staffing Budget Variability Rule

## Statement

Staffing budgets for nonmanufacturing businesses should flex staffing levels based on demand drivers (e.g., number of guests, patients, vehicles).

## Rationale

Service businesses have variable demand; staffing must match demand to control labor costs while meeting service levels.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Demand varies significantly across periods (e.g., weekdays vs. weekends, seasons)
- **THEN** Calculate staff requirements based on activity drivers and adjust accordingly
- **OTHERWISE** Overstaffing wastes money; understaffing degrades service quality
