# Allowances May Exceed Net Income

## Statement

When salary and interest allowances exceed net income, each partner receives their allowance and the remaining deficiency is shared in the income-sharing ratio, potentially resulting in charges to capital accounts.

## Rationale

Allowances are contractual allocations; they must be honored even when they exceed income, with the deficiency reducing capital accounts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Total salary and interest allowances exceed net income
- **THEN** Partners still receive their allowances; the excess deficiency is allocated in the income-sharing ratio
- **OTHERWISE** Standard allocation applies
