# Three Methods Yield Different Results Rule

## Statement

The three inventory costing methods (FIFO, LIFO, weighted average) will normally yield different amounts for ending inventory, cost of goods sold, and gross profit for the period.

## Rationale

Different assumptions about which costs are matched against revenue produce different financial results, especially when prices are changing.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Inventory costs change over the period
- **THEN** FIFO, LIFO, and weighted average will produce different ending inventory, COGS, and gross profit amounts
- **OTHERWISE** If costs are stable, all three methods produce the same results
