# Interest Expense Reported Separately

## Statement

Interest expense from notes payable should be reported in the 'Other expense' section of the income statement, separate from operating expenses.

## Rationale

Interest is a financing cost, not an operating expense, and should be separately disclosed.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Reporting interest expense on the income statement
- **THEN** Report in the 'Other revenue and expense' section
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
