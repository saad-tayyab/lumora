# FOB Shipping Point - Buyer Pays Freight

## Statement

When merchandise is purchased FOB shipping point, the buyer pays freight costs and debits Inventory (perpetual) or Freight In (periodic).

## Rationale

Ownership passes at the shipping point, so the buyer bears the freight cost as part of inventory cost.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Merchandise is purchased with FOB shipping point terms
- **THEN** Buyer records freight costs in Inventory (or Freight In)
- **OTHERWISE** Inventory cost is understated
