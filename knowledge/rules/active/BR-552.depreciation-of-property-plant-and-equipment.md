# Depreciation of Property, Plant and Equipment

## Statement

All depreciable property, plant and equipment assets must be systematically depreciated over their estimated useful lives using an appropriate method.

## Rationale

Matching principle requires allocating the cost of long-lived assets to the periods they benefit.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** PP&E assets are placed in service
- **THEN** Begin depreciating cost less salvage value over estimated useful life
- **OTHERWISE** Construction in process and land are not depreciated
