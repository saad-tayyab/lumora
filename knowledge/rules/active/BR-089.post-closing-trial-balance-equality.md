# Post-Closing Trial Balance Equality

## Statement

The total debits must equal total credits on the post-closing trial balance.

## Rationale

Verifies that the ledger is in balance after closing entries have been posted.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Post-closing trial balance is prepared
- **THEN** Total debits must equal total credits
- **OTHERWISE** An error exists in the closing process or ledger that must be corrected
