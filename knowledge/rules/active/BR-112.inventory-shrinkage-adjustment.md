# Inventory Shrinkage Adjustment

## Statement

At the end of the period, inventory shrinkage is recorded by debiting Cost of Goods Sold and crediting Inventory for the difference between book balance and physical count.

## Rationale

Ensures the Inventory account agrees with the physical inventory on hand. Shrinkage is a normal cost of operations.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Physical inventory count is less than the Inventory account balance
- **THEN** Debit COGS, credit Inventory for the shrinkage amount
- **OTHERWISE** Inventory is overstated on the balance sheet
