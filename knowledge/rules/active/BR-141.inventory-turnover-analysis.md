# Inventory Turnover Analysis

## Statement

Inventory turnover ratio = Cost of Goods Sold / Average Inventory. Days' sales in inventory = Average Inventory / Average Daily COGS. Generally, lower days' sales in inventory indicates more efficient inventory management.

## Rationale

These ratios measure how efficiently a company manages its inventory. Higher turnover and lower days' sales indicate faster selling and better inventory control.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing inventory management efficiency
- **THEN** Compute inventory turnover and days' sales in inventory
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
