# Prior Period Adjustment Reported Net of Tax

## Statement

Prior period adjustments for error corrections are reported as adjustments to the beginning balance of retained earnings, net of applicable income taxes.

## Rationale

Error corrections should not flow through the current period income statement.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An error is discovered in the financial statements of a prior period
- **THEN** Adjust the beginning balance of retained earnings net of tax; disclose the nature of the error
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
