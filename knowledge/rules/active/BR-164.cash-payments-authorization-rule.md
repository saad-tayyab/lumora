# Cash Payments Authorization Rule

## Statement

Cash payments should be made only for authorized transactions. Controls should ensure that all available purchase discounts are taken.

## Rationale

Prevents unauthorized disbursements and ensures the company benefits from available discounts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Processing cash payments
- **THEN** Verify authorization and take available purchase discounts
- **OTHERWISE** Unauthorized payments may be made and discounts lost
