# Bank Deposit Verification Rule

## Statement

When cash is deposited in the bank, the bank stamps a duplicate copy of the deposit ticket with the amount received. This bank receipt is returned to the Accounting Department and compared to the total amount that should have been deposited.

## Rationale

Helps ensure all cash is deposited and no cash is lost or stolen on the way to the bank.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Depositing cash in the bank
- **THEN** Compare bank-stamped deposit ticket to expected deposit amount
- **OTHERWISE** Cash may be lost or stolen during transport to bank
