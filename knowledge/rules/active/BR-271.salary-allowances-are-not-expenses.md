# Salary Allowances Are Not Expenses

## Statement

Partner salary allowances are not operating expenses of the partnership; they are divisions of net income credited to the partners' capital accounts.

## Rationale

Partners are not employees of the partnership, so salary allowances represent a way to allocate income rather than an expense.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A partnership agreement provides for salary allowances
- **THEN** Treat salary allowances as allocations of net income, not expenses
- **OTHERWISE** Salary allowances would incorrectly reduce net income
