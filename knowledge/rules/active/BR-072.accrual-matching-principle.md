# Accrual Matching Principle

## Statement

Revenues must be recorded in the period they are earned, and expenses must be recorded in the period they are incurred to generate those revenues.

## Rationale

Ensures financial statements accurately reflect the economic activity of the period, not just cash movements.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An accounting period ends
- **THEN** All earned revenues and incurred expenses must be recorded via adjusting entries
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements will misstate income and financial position
