# P/E Ratio Interpretation Rule

## Statement

A higher price-earnings ratio indicates that investors expect higher future earnings growth relative to the current stock price.

## Rationale

The P/E ratio reflects market expectations about future earnings potential.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing market valuation of a company's stock
- **THEN** Compare P/E ratio to industry averages and historical trends
- **OTHERWISE** Investment decisions may be based on incomplete valuation analysis
