# Overtime Hours Capped At Maximum

## Statement

Daily overtime hours must not exceed the configured maximum daily overtime limit.

## Rationale

This prevents excessive overtime that could lead to employee burnout, safety issues, or labor law violations. The cap enforces reasonable working hours while allowing flexibility for occasional peak periods.

## Scope

- Applies to: All attendance records (CON-HR-004) subject to OvertimePolicy (CON-HR-018)
- Bounded Context: BC-HR (Human Resources)
- Entities: Attendance, OvertimePolicy

## Conditions

- WHEN daily hours worked exceed the standard workday
- THEN overtime hours MUST NOT exceed max_daily_overtime
- OTHERWISE the overtime is capped at max_daily_overtime

## Pseudocode

```
function calculateOvertime(attendance, policy):
    dailyHours = attendance.hours_worked
    standardHours = policy.standard_workday_hours
    maxOvertime = policy.max_daily_overtime
    
    if dailyHours > standardHours:
        overtime = min(dailyHours - standardHours, maxOvertime)
    else:
        overtime = 0
    
    return overtime
```
