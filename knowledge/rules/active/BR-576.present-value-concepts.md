# Present Value Concepts

## Statement

Cash to be received (or paid) in the future is not the equivalent of the same amount of money received at an earlier date.

## Rationale

Money has time value due to earning potential; future cash flows must be discounted.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing cash flows at different points in time
- **THEN** Discount future amounts to present value using an appropriate rate
- **OTHERWISE** Comparisons will be misleading
