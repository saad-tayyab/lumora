# Depreciation Methods Consistency

## Statement

Once a depreciation method is selected for an asset, it should be applied consistently throughout the asset's useful life.

## Rationale

Consistency principle ensures comparability of financial statements across periods.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A depreciation method is chosen for a fixed asset
- **THEN** Apply the same method for all periods of the asset's life
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements will not be comparable across periods
