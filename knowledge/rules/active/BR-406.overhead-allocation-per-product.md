# Overhead Allocation per Product

## Statement

Overhead Allocated = Activity-Base Usage × Activity Rate, for each activity. Total Manufacturing Overhead Cost = Sum of all activity allocations.

## Rationale

Assigns overhead costs to products based on their actual consumption of activities.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Allocating overhead costs to individual products using ABC
- **THEN** Multiply each product's activity-base usage by the corresponding activity rate and sum across all activities
- **OTHERWISE** Products will not receive their fair share of overhead costs
