# Days' Sales Comparison to Credit Terms

## Statement

Days' sales in receivables should be compared with a company's credit terms to evaluate collection efficiency.

## Rationale

If days' sales exceeds credit terms, collections are inefficient. If within terms, collections are efficient.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating accounts receivable collection efficiency
- **THEN** Compare days' sales in receivables against credit terms
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot assess whether collections are timely
