# Depreciation Allocation

## Statement

The cost of a tangible fixed asset must be allocated as depreciation expense over its estimated useful life.

## Rationale

Matches the cost of using the asset with the revenues it helps generate over time.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A fixed asset is placed in service
- **THEN** Depreciation expense is recorded each period until the asset is fully depreciated or disposed
- **OTHERWISE** Asset costs would be incorrectly expensed in the purchase period or not expensed at all
