# Cost Distortion Causes Pricing Errors

## Statement

When product costs are distorted by inaccurate overhead allocation, companies may underprice high-consumption products and overprice low-consumption products, leading to incorrect strategic decisions.

## Rationale

Inaccurate costs lead to poor pricing, which can cause loss of sales on overpriced products and losses on underpriced products.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using inaccurate overhead allocation methods for pricing decisions
- **THEN** Products consuming more overhead are underpriced and products consuming less are overpriced
- **OTHERWISE** The company may lose market share or profitability
