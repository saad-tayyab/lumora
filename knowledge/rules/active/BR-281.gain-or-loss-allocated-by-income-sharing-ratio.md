# Gain or Loss Allocated by Income-Sharing Ratio

## Statement

Gains and losses on the sale of partnership assets during liquidation are allocated to partners' capital accounts based on the income-sharing ratio specified in the partnership agreement.

## Rationale

The income-sharing ratio determines how all gains and losses are divided among partners, including those realized during liquidation.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Noncash assets are sold during liquidation
- **THEN** Allocate gain or loss to each partner based on income-sharing ratio
- **OTHERWISE** Losses or gains would not be properly attributed
