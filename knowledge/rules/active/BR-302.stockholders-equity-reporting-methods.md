# Stockholders' Equity Reporting Methods

## Statement

Stockholders' equity can be reported using Method 1 (each class of stock with its related paid-in capital accounts) or Method 2 (stock accounts followed by additional paid-in capital as a single item).

## Rationale

Both methods are acceptable; Method 1 provides more detail while Method 2 is more concise.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the stockholders' equity section of the balance sheet
- **THEN** Use either Method 1 or Method 2 consistently; disclose all relevant rights and privileges of each class of stock
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
