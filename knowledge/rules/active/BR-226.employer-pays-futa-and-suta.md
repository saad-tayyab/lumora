# Employer Pays FUTA and SUTA

## Statement

Employers are solely responsible for paying Federal Unemployment Tax (FUTA) and State Unemployment Tax (SUTA). These are not withheld from employee earnings.

## Rationale

Unemployment taxes are employer-only taxes that fund unemployment compensation programs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording employer payroll taxes
- **THEN** Record FUTA and SUTA as Payroll Tax Expense (employer-only taxes)
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
