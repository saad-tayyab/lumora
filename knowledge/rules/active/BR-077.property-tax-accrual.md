# Property Tax Accrual

## Statement

Property taxes covering a period that spans multiple accounting periods must be allocated proportionally across those periods.

## Rationale

Ensures each period bears its fair share of the property tax expense.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A property tax payment covers a period beyond the current accounting period
- **THEN** The portion attributable to the current period is recorded as an expense
- **OTHERWISE** Expenses are misstated across periods
