# Budget Goal Setting Behavioral Rule

## Statement

Budget goals should be challenging but attainable; setting goals too tightly can lead to unethical behavior, while setting them too loosely reduces motivation.

## Rationale

Unattainable budgets cause frustration and potential fraud; overly easy budgets waste organizational potential.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Establishing budget targets
- **THEN** Set goals that are achievable with reasonable effort and provide motivation
- **OTHERWISE** Budgetary slack or dysfunctional behavior may result
