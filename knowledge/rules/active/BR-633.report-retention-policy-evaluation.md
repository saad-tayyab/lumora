# Report Retention Policy Evaluation

## Statement

Report retention policy evaluation runs daily as a background job to enforce retention requirements.

## Rationale

Automated daily evaluation ensures consistent enforcement of retention policies without manual intervention. This prevents storage bloat and ensures compliance with data retention requirements.

## Scope

- Applies to: All reports (CON-REPORT-001) subject to ReportRetentionPolicy (CON-REPORT-015)
- Bounded Context: BC-REPORT (Reporting & Analytics)
- Entities: Report, ReportRetentionPolicy

## Conditions

- WHEN the daily retention evaluation job runs
- THEN all reports exceeding their retention period MUST be processed according to policy
- OTHERWISE retention policies are not enforced

## Pseudocode

```
function evaluateRetentionPolicies():
    policies = findActiveRetentionPolicies()
    for policy in policies:
        reports = findReportsByType(policy.report_type)
        for report in reports:
            if isExpired(report, policy):
                if policy.archive_before_delete:
                    archiveReport(report, policy.archive_retention_days)
                else:
                    softDeleteReport(report)
```

## Exceptions

- None — this rule has no exceptions.
