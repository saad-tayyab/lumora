# Balance Sheet Account Classification

## Statement

Balance sheet accounts must be properly classified as assets, liabilities, or owner's equity. Current and non-current classifications must be correct.

## Rationale

Proper classification enables stakeholders to assess liquidity, solvency, and financial position.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A balance sheet is prepared
- **THEN** Accounts are classified as current/non-current assets, current/non-current liabilities, and owner's equity
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements are misleading and violate GAAP
