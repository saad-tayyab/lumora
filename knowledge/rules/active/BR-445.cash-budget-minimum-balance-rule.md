# Cash Budget Minimum Balance Rule

## Statement

Maintain a minimum cash balance at all times; if the projected ending cash balance falls below the minimum, arrange financing.

## Rationale

Companies need a cash cushion for unexpected expenses and to maintain operational continuity.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Projected ending cash balance is less than minimum required balance
- **THEN** Borrow funds to bring cash balance up to the minimum
- **OTHERWISE** Company may face liquidity problems
