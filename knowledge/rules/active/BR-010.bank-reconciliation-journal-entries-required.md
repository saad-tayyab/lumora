# Bank Reconciliation Journal Entries Required

## Statement

Any additions or deductions made to the company's book balance during bank reconciliation require journal entries.

## Rationale

The company must update its records to reflect items discovered on the bank statement that were not previously recorded.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Bank reconciliation identifies items affecting the book balance
- **THEN** Record journal entries for bank collections, NSF checks, service charges, and errors
- **OTHERWISE** The company's cash records will be inaccurate
