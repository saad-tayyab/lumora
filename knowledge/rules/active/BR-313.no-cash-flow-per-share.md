# No Cash Flow per Share

## Statement

Cash flow per share should not be reported in the financial statements because it may be misinterpreted as the per-share amount available for dividends or as equivalent to earnings per share.

## Rationale

Cash flow per share can be misleading to users who may confuse it with dividends per share or EPS.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing financial statements including the statement of cash flows
- **THEN** Do not report cash flow per share
- **OTHERWISE** Users may be misled about the company's ability to pay dividends or its earnings performance
