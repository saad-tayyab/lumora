# Break-Even for Service Companies

## Statement

Service companies apply the same CVP formulas as product companies, but unit contribution margin is defined as revenue per account minus variable cost per account over the customer lifetime.

## Rationale

Service businesses have different revenue and cost structures but the same fundamental cost-volume-profit relationships.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing a service company's break-even point
- **THEN** Define the unit of analysis (account, subscription, seat) and compute contribution margin per unit over the customer lifecycle
- **OTHERWISE** Break-even analysis will be inaccurate for service businesses
