# Sunk Cost Irrelevance Rule

## Statement

Sunk costs are not affected by later decisions and must be ignored when evaluating alternative courses of action.

## Rationale

Sunk costs have already been incurred and cannot be recovered regardless of the decision made, so including them would distort the differential analysis.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An asset has been purchased or a cost committed prior to the decision date
- **THEN** Exclude the book value or original cost from differential analysis — only consider future costs and revenues
- **OTHERWISE** All costs are relevant to the decision
