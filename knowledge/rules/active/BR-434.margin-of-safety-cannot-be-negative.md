# Margin of Safety Cannot Be Negative

## Statement

If margin of safety is negative (actual sales below break-even), the company is operating at a loss. The margin of safety expressed as a percentage cannot exceed 100%.

## Rationale

Negative margin of safety signals the company is below break-even and needs to increase sales or reduce costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing margin of safety
- **THEN** If actual sales < break-even sales, margin of safety is negative, indicating a loss position
- **OTHERWISE** A positive margin of safety indicates a profit cushion
