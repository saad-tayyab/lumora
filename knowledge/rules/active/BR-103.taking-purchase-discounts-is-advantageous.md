# Taking Purchase Discounts is Advantageous

## Statement

A buyer should normally take purchase discounts even if borrowing is required, because the implied annual interest rate of not taking the discount is typically very high (e.g., 2/10, n/30 ≈ 36.5%).

## Rationale

The cost of foregone discounts far exceeds typical borrowing costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A purchase discount is available
- **THEN** Evaluate whether to take the discount; it is almost always advantageous
- **OTHERWISE** The buyer pays an excessively high implied interest rate
