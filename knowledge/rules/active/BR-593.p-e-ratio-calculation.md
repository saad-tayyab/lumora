# P/E Ratio Calculation

## Statement

Price-Earnings ratio equals market price per share divided by earnings per share.

## Rationale

Provides a measure of how much investors are willing to pay per dollar of earnings, indicating market expectations for growth.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating stock valuation relative to earnings
- **THEN** Calculate P/E ratio as market price per share / earnings per share
- **OTHERWISE** P/E ratio is not meaningful if EPS is zero or negative
