# Closing Process

## Statement

At the end of each accounting period, all temporary accounts (revenues, expenses, and drawing) must be closed to the owner's capital account.

## Rationale

Resets temporary accounts to zero for the next period and updates the capital account with the period's results.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** The accounting period ends and financial statements are prepared
- **THEN** Revenue accounts are debited, expense accounts are credited, and drawing account is credited, all to the capital account
- **OTHERWISE** Temporary account balances would carry into the next period, misstating results
