# Transfer Amount Must Be Positive

## Statement

A bank transfer amount must be greater than zero.

## Rationale

This ensures data integrity by preventing invalid transfer transactions. Zero or negative amounts would represent meaningless or reversed transfers that could corrupt financial records.

## Scope

- Applies to: All bank transfers (CON-CASH-002)
- Bounded Context: BC-CASH (Cash & Treasury)
- Entities: BankTransfer

## Conditions

- WHEN a bank transfer is created or updated
- THEN amount MUST be greater than zero
- OTHERWISE the transfer is rejected with error `ERR_TRANSFER_AMOUNT_INVALID`

## Pseudocode

```
function validateTransferAmount(transfer):
    if transfer.amount <= 0:
        throw TransferAmountInvalidError(transfer.id, transfer.amount)
```
