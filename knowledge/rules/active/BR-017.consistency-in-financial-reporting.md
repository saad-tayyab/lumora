# Consistency in Financial Reporting

## Statement

Accounting methods and practices should be consistent from one period to the next.

## Rationale

Consistency enables meaningful comparison of financial statements across different periods.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Selecting accounting methods
- **THEN** Apply the same methods consistently across periods
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements will not be comparable
