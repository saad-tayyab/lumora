# Overapplied and Underapplied Overhead

## Statement

A debit balance in Manufacturing Overhead represents underapplied overhead (unfavorable variance). A credit balance represents overapplied overhead (favorable variance).

## Rationale

The Manufacturing Overhead account tracks actual costs debited against applied standard costs credited, with the balance being the total variance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Actual overhead exceeds applied overhead
- **THEN** Manufacturing Overhead has debit balance (underapplied)
- **OTHERWISE** Manufacturing Overhead has credit balance (overapplied)
