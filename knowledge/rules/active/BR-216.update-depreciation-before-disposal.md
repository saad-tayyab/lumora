# Update Depreciation Before Disposal

## Statement

When a fixed asset is sold or discarded, any depreciation for the current period must be recorded up to the date of disposal before removing the asset from the accounts.

## Rationale

The book value must be current as of the disposal date to correctly compute gain or loss.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Disposing of a fixed asset before year-end
- **THEN** Record depreciation expense for the partial year up to disposal date
- **OTHERWISE** Proceed directly to disposal entry if already current
