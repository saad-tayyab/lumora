# Industry-Specific Profitability Measures

## Statement

Different industries use different profitability measures. For example, airlines use revenue per passenger mile, hotels use occupancy rates.

## Rationale

Generic ratios may not capture industry-specific performance drivers.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing a company's profitability
- **THEN** Consider industry-specific measures alongside standard ratios
- **OTHERWISE** Analysis may miss key performance indicators
