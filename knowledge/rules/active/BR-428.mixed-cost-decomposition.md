# Mixed Cost Decomposition

## Statement

A mixed cost must be decomposed into its fixed and variable components before performing CVP analysis.

## Rationale

CVP analysis requires separation of costs into fixed and variable categories to determine contribution margin and break-even points.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A cost is identified as mixed (containing both fixed and variable elements)
- **THEN** Separate the cost into fixed and variable components using methods such as high-low, scattergraph, or regression
- **OTHERWISE** CVP calculations will be inaccurate
