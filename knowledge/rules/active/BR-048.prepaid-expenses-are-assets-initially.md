# Prepaid Expenses Are Assets Initially

## Statement

Prepaid expenses are recorded as assets when paid, then expensed as they are consumed or expire over time.

## Rationale

The payment creates a future benefit (asset) that is gradually used up.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An expense is paid in advance of receiving the benefit
- **THEN** Record as an asset (prepaid expense), then adjust to expense as the benefit is consumed
- **OTHERWISE** Expenses will be overstated in the payment period and understated in future periods
