# Trade Discounts Not Recorded

## Statement

Neither sellers nor buyers record the list prices of merchandise or trade discounts in their accounts. Transactions are recorded at the net price after trade discounts.

## Rationale

Trade discounts are used for price differentiation and are not part of the actual transaction amount.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A transaction involves a trade discount
- **THEN** Record the sale/purchase at the net price (list price minus trade discount)
- **OTHERWISE** Assets and revenues/payables are overstated
