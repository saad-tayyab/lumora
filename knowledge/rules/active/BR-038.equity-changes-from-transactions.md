# Equity Changes from Transactions

## Statement

Owner's equity changes through four types of events: owner investments (increase), net income (increase), net loss (decrease), and owner withdrawals (decrease).

## Rationale

Understanding what causes equity to change is fundamental to preparing the statement of owner's equity and interpreting financial performance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing changes in owner's equity over a period
- **THEN** Beginning Capital + Investments + Net Income - Withdrawals = Ending Capital
- **OTHERWISE** Owner's equity will not reconcile correctly
