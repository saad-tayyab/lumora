# Depreciation Exclusion from Cash Flow

## Statement

Depreciation is not included when determining cash flow from an investment because it is a noncash expense.

## Rationale

Cash payback and other cash-based methods measure actual cash recovery; depreciation does not represent a cash outflow.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing net cash inflow for capital investment analysis
- **THEN** Subtract depreciation from total expenses to get cash expenses; net cash inflow = cash revenues - cash expenses
- **OTHERWISE** Include all expenses for income-based methods like average rate of return
