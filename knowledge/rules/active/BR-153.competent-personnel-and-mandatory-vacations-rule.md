# Competent Personnel and Mandatory Vacations Rule

## Statement

Procedures should be established for properly training and supervising employees. Duties of accounting personnel should be rotated and vacations mandated for all employees.

## Rationale

Employee fraud is often discovered when a long-term employee who never took vacations misses work. Rotation encourages adherence to procedures.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Managing accounting personnel
- **THEN** Provide training, rotate duties, and mandate vacations
- **OTHERWISE** Fraud may go undetected for extended periods
