# Quick Ratio Interpretation

## Statement

A quick ratio below 1.0 indicates a company may have difficulty meeting its short-term obligations without selling inventory.

## Rationale

Measures immediate liquidity without relying on inventory liquidation.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating short-term liquidity
- **THEN** Compute quick ratio = quick assets / current liabilities
- **OTHERWISE** Liquidity risk may be underestimated
