# Sales Discounts GAAP Requirement

## Statement

GAAP requires sellers to record revenue in the amount most likely to be received (entitled). When customers take or are likely to take a discount, sales must be reported net of the discount.

## Rationale

Revenue should reflect the amount the seller expects to collect, not the gross invoice amount.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Significant accounts receivable subject to sales discounts exist at year-end
- **THEN** Adjust Accounts Receivable and Sales to the amount expected to be received
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue is overstated
