# Perpetual Inventory Sale Recording

## Statement

When merchandise is sold under the perpetual inventory system, two entries are required: (1) record the sale at selling price, and (2) record the cost of goods sold and reduce Inventory.

## Rationale

The perpetual system requires simultaneous recording of revenue and cost of goods sold at each sale transaction to maintain accurate inventory records.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording sales under perpetual inventory system
- **THEN** Record sale at selling price AND record COGS and reduce Inventory
- **OTHERWISE** Under periodic system, only the sale is recorded; COGS is computed at period end
