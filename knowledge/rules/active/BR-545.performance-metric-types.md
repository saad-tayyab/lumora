# Performance Metric Types

## Statement

Performance metrics should include both leading indicators (nonfinancial predictors of future performance) and lagging indicators (financial measures of past performance).

## Rationale

Balancing leading and lagging indicators allows organizations to both predict future performance and measure historical results, enabling proactive management.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Selecting performance metrics for the balanced scorecard
- **THEN** Include both leading indicators (e.g., customer satisfaction, training hours) and lagging indicators (e.g., sales, profits)
- **OTHERWISE** The scorecard may be overly focused on either past results or future predictions
