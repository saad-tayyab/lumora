# Adjusting Entries Never Involve Cash

## Statement

No adjusting entry debits or credits the Cash account. Adjusting entries only affect non-cash asset, liability, equity, revenue, or expense accounts.

## Rationale

Cash transactions are recorded when they occur. Adjusting entries handle accruals and deferrals that do not involve immediate cash flows.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An adjusting entry is prepared
- **THEN** Neither the debit nor the credit involves the Cash account
- **OTHERWISE** Cash transactions would be duplicated or incorrectly recorded
