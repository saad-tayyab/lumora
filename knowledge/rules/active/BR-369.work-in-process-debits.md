# Work in Process Debits

## Statement

Work in Process is increased (debited) for direct materials cost, direct labor cost, and applied manufacturing overhead cost.

## Rationale

Accumulates all product costs for goods currently in production.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Manufacturing costs are assigned to jobs
- **THEN** Debit Work in Process
- **OTHERWISE** WIP does not reflect current production costs
