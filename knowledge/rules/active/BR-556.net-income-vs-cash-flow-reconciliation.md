# Net Income vs. Cash Flow Reconciliation

## Statement

Net income and cash flows from operations will differ due to non-cash items (depreciation, deferred taxes, stock-based compensation) and changes in working capital components.

## Rationale

Accrual accounting recognizes transactions when incurred, not when cash changes hands.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing net income to cash flows from operations
- **THEN** Start with net income and adjust for non-cash items and working capital changes
- **OTHERWISE** Direct comparison without reconciliation would be misleading
