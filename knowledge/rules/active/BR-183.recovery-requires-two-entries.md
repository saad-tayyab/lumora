# Recovery Requires Two Entries

## Statement

When a previously written-off account is collected, two journal entries are required: (1) reinstate the account receivable, and (2) record the cash collection.

## Rationale

Properly documents both the reversal of the write-off and the cash receipt for audit trail purposes.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Cash is received for an account previously written off
- **THEN** First entry reinstates the receivable; second entry records the cash receipt
- **OTHERWISE** The audit trail is incomplete and the customer's credit history is not properly maintained
