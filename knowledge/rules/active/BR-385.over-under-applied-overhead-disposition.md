# Over/Under Applied Overhead Disposition

## Statement

At period end, over-applied or under-applied manufacturing overhead must be disposed of by either closing the entire amount to Cost of Goods Sold or prorating it among Work in Process, Finished Goods, and Cost of Goods Sold.

## Rationale

Ensures that actual overhead costs are properly reflected in the financial statements.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Period ends and overhead account has a balance
- **THEN** Close over/under applied overhead to COGS or prorate among WIP, FG, and COGS
- **OTHERWISE** No disposition needed
