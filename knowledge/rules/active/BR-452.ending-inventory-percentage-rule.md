# Ending Inventory Percentage Rule

## Statement

Desired ending finished goods inventory should be maintained at a specific percentage (e.g., 5%, 10%, 20%) of the next period's estimated sales.

## Rationale

Maintaining a proportional ending inventory ensures adequate stock while avoiding excessive inventory carrying costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Determining desired ending inventory for the production budget
- **THEN** Calculate as: next period's estimated sales × ending inventory percentage
- **OTHERWISE** Inventory levels will not align with future demand
