# IRR Factor Computation Rule

## Statement

When equal annual net cash flows are expected, the IRR can be found by computing the present value factor for an annuity of $1 (Amount to Invest / Annual Cash Flow) and locating it in the present value annuity table.

## Rationale

The factor that equates the investment cost to the present value of cash flows identifies the actual rate of return.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing IRR for a proposal with equal annual net cash flows
- **THEN** PV factor = Amount to Invest / Annual Net Cash Flow; locate factor in annuity table for the given number of periods; the column heading is the IRR
- **OTHERWISE** Use spreadsheet software for unequal cash flows
