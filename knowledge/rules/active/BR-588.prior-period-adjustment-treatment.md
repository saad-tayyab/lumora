# Prior Period Adjustment Treatment

## Statement

Material errors from prior periods are corrected as adjustments to the beginning balance of retained earnings, not on the current period income statement.

## Rationale

Prevents distortion of current period performance by excluding corrections of past errors.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A material error from a prior period is discovered
- **THEN** Adjust beginning retained earnings and restate prior period financial statements
- **OTHERWISE** Correct immaterial errors in the current period
