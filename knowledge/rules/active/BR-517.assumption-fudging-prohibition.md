# Assumption Fudging Prohibition

## Statement

Capital budgeting analysis should not be used to justify an assumed net present value by working backwards from a desired result; estimates must be independent and unbiased.

## Rationale

Reverse-engineering assumptions undermines the credibility of the entire analysis process and can lead to poor investment decisions.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a capital investment analysis
- **THEN** Estimate inputs independently based on available data; do not adjust inputs to produce a predetermined NPV outcome
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
