# Continuous Budget Update

## Statement

A continuous budget should be updated monthly by adding a new month at the end and removing the expired month, maintaining a constant twelve-month planning horizon.

## Rationale

Ensures management always has a full year of forward-looking budget data for planning and decision-making.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using a continuous (rolling) budget approach
- **THEN** At the end of each month, add the next month's budget and drop the expired month
- **OTHERWISE** The budget horizon shrinks over time, reducing planning effectiveness
