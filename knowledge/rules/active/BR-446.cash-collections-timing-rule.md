# Cash Collections Timing Rule

## Statement

Cash receipts from credit sales should be estimated based on historical collection patterns (e.g., 70% collected in month following sale, 30% in second month).

## Rationale

Not all sales are collected immediately; accurate cash flow forecasting requires realistic collection timing assumptions.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Estimating cash receipts from accounts receivable
- **THEN** Apply historical collection percentages to current and prior period credit sales
- **OTHERWISE** Cash budget will overstate available cash
