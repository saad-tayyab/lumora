# Yield Pricing Demand-Based Rule

## Statement

Service businesses with high fixed costs should charge higher prices during periods of high demand and lower prices during periods of low demand to maximize revenue from fixed capacity.

## Rationale

Fixed costs do not vary with the number of customers served, so filling excess capacity with lower-priced customers generates additional contribution margin.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Demand for fixed capacity varies by time period or customer segment
- **THEN** Set higher prices during peak demand and offer discounts (standby, early booking) during off-peak periods
- **OTHERWISE** Charge a uniform price
