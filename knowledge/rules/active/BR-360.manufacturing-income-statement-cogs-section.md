# Manufacturing Income Statement COGS Section

## Statement

For manufacturing companies, COGS = Beginning Finished Goods + Cost of Goods Manufactured - Ending Finished Goods. This differs from merchandising where COGS = Beginning Merchandise + Purchases - Ending Merchandise.

## Rationale

Reflects the unique cost flows in manufacturing where goods are produced rather than purchased.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a manufacturing income statement
- **THEN** Use finished goods inventory and cost of goods manufactured in the COGS section
- **OTHERWISE** Income statement does not reflect manufacturing cost flows
