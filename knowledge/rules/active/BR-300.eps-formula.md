# EPS Formula

## Statement

Earnings per share = (Net Income - Preferred Dividends) / Weighted Average Common Shares Outstanding.

## Rationale

EPS measures the amount of income available to each share of common stock, the most widely used measure of corporate profitability.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing earnings per share for common stock
- **THEN** Subtract preferred dividends from net income; divide by weighted average common shares outstanding
- **OTHERWISE** If no preferred stock is outstanding, EPS = Net Income / Average Common Shares
