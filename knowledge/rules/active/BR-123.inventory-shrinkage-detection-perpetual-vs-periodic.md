# Inventory Shrinkage Detection - Perpetual vs Periodic

## Statement

Under the perpetual system, inventory shrinkage is directly determined by comparing the physical count to the Inventory account balance. Under the periodic system, shrinkage is indirectly included in the cost of goods sold computation and cannot be separately identified.

## Rationale

The periodic inventory system does not keep the inventory account up to date for purchases and sales, making direct shrinkage measurement impossible.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Performing year-end physical inventory count
- **THEN** Perpetual system records shrinkage separately; periodic system includes it in COGS
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
