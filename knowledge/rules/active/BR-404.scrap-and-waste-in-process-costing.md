# Scrap and Waste in Process Costing

## Statement

Scrap produced during manufacturing reduces the yield ratio and increases the cost per unit of good output. Machine shutdowns and startups often produce waste, reducing efficiency.

## Rationale

Understanding waste sources helps identify opportunities to improve yield and reduce costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing production efficiency
- **THEN** Compute yield ratio = Output units / Input units
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot measure production efficiency
