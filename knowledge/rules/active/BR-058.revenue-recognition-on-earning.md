# Revenue Recognition on Earning

## Statement

Revenue (Fees Earned) is recorded when services are performed or goods are delivered, regardless of when cash is received.

## Rationale

Under accrual accounting, revenue is recognized when earned, not when cash changes hands. This matches revenue to the period in which it was generated.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Services are performed or goods are delivered to a customer
- **THEN** Record revenue (credit Fees Earned) for the amount earned
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue will be understated in the period the work was performed
