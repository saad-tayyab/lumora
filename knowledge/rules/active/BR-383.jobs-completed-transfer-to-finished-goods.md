# Jobs Completed Transfer to Finished Goods

## Statement

When a job is completed, its total cost is transferred from Work in Process to Finished Goods inventory.

## Rationale

Reflects the physical completion of the job and its readiness for sale.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A job is completed
- **THEN** Debit Finished Goods, Credit Work in Process for the job's total cost
- **OTHERWISE** Job remains in Work in Process
