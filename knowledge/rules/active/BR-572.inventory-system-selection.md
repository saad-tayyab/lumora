# Inventory System Selection

## Statement

A business must choose either a periodic or perpetual inventory system and apply it consistently.

## Rationale

Consistency principle requires uniform application of accounting methods across periods.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Inventory tracking is needed
- **THEN** Apply the chosen system (periodic or perpetual) consistently
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements may be misleading due to inconsistent methods
