# Credit Card Fees are Period Expenses

## Statement

Processing fees charged by credit card clearinghouses or issuing banks are recorded as Credit Card Expense periodically.

## Rationale

The fees are a cost of accepting credit card payments for merchandise.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Credit card processing fees are incurred
- **THEN** Record as Credit Card Expense (debit) and Cash (credit)
- **OTHERWISE** Expenses are understated
