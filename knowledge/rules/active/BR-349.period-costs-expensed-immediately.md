# Period Costs Expensed Immediately

## Statement

Period costs (selling and administrative expenses) are reported as expenses on the income statement in the period they are incurred and never appear on the balance sheet.

## Rationale

Period costs are not tied to the production of inventory and should be matched to the period in which they are incurred.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Selling or administrative expenses are incurred
- **THEN** Expense immediately on the income statement
- **OTHERWISE** Period costs would be improperly capitalized as assets
