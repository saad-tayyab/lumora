# Accrual Basis Requirement

## Statement

The accrual basis of accounting must be used in preparing financial statements in accordance with GAAP.

## Rationale

Accrual accounting better matches revenues and expenses, providing a more accurate picture of operating performance than cash basis accounting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing financial statements under GAAP
- **THEN** Use accrual basis: record revenues when earned and expenses when incurred
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements do not comply with GAAP
