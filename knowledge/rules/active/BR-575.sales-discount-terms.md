# Sales Discount Terms

## Statement

Credit terms such as 2/10, n/30 mean the buyer gets a 2% discount if paying within 10 days, otherwise the full amount is due in 30 days.

## Rationale

Encourages early payment and defines the credit period clearly.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A credit sale is made with discount terms
- **THEN** Offer the discount if payment is received within the discount period
- **OTHERWISE** Full invoice amount is due within the credit period
