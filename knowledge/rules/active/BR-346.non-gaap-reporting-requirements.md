# Non-GAAP Reporting Requirements

## Statement

Companies reporting adjusted (non-GAAP) amounts must label them as non-GAAP and provide a reconciliation to GAAP amounts.

## Rationale

Ensures transparency and prevents misleading investors by obscuring GAAP results.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Company reports non-GAAP financial measures
- **THEN** Label as non-GAAP and reconcile to GAAP
- **OTHERWISE** Violates SEC reporting requirements
