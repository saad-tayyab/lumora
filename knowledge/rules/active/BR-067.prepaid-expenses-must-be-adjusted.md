# Prepaid Expenses Must Be Adjusted

## Statement

At period end, prepaid expenses must be adjusted to reflect the portion consumed during the period, moving the used amount from the asset account to the appropriate expense account.

## Rationale

Under accrual accounting, expenses must be recognized in the period they are consumed, not when cash is paid. Failing to adjust overstates assets and understates expenses.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A prepaid asset has been partially consumed during the period
- **THEN** Debit the appropriate expense account and credit the prepaid asset account for the consumed portion
- **OTHERWISE** Assets are overstated and expenses are understated on the financial statements
