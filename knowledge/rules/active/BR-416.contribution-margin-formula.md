# Contribution Margin Formula

## Statement

Contribution Margin = Sales Revenue - Variable Expenses. Unit Contribution Margin = Selling Price per Unit - Variable Cost per Unit.

## Rationale

The contribution margin is what remains to cover fixed costs and generate profit.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Calculating contribution margin for CVP analysis
- **THEN** Subtract variable expenses from sales revenue
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot perform break-even or target profit analysis
