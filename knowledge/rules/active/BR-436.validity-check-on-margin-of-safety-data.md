# Validity Check on Margin of Safety Data

## Statement

If reported break-even units exceed actual sales units while margin of safety is reported as positive, the data is inconsistent and invalid.

## Rationale

A positive margin of safety requires actual sales to exceed break-even sales; contradictory data indicates an error.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Reviewing CVP data for consistency
- **THEN** Verify that break-even units < actual sales units when margin of safety is positive
- **OTHERWISE** Data contains an error and should not be used for decision-making
