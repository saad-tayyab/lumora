# Bond Indenture Ratio Compliance

## Statement

Companies must maintain required financial ratios (e.g., working capital, current ratio, quick ratio) as specified in bond indenture agreements throughout the bond term.

## Rationale

Failure to maintain required ratios can result in bond default and acceleration of debt repayment.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Company has outstanding bonds with financial ratio covenants
- **THEN** Monitor and maintain required ratios at all times
- **OTHERWISE** Bondholders may demand immediate repayment
