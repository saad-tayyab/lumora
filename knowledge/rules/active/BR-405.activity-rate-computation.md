# Activity Rate Computation

## Statement

Activity Rate = Budgeted Activity Cost / Total Activity-Base Usage

## Rationale

Establishes the cost per unit of activity for allocating overhead to products.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing activity-based overhead allocation rates
- **THEN** Divide each activity's budgeted cost by its total activity-base usage
- **OTHERWISE** Activity costs cannot be accurately assigned to products
