# Gain/Loss on Sale Adjustment

## Statement

Gains on the sale of long-term assets must be deducted from net income, and losses must be added back, because the full cash proceeds from the sale are reported as investing activities.

## Rationale

The gain or loss is included in net income but is not an operating cash flow; the entire sale proceeds are an investing activity.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using the indirect method and a gain or loss on sale of assets exists
- **THEN** Deduct gains from net income; add back losses to net income
- **OTHERWISE** Operating and investing cash flows will be misstated
