# Sales Discount - Gross Method

## Statement

Under the gross method, record the sale at the full invoice amount. If payment is received within the discount period, record the discount as a debit to Sales Discounts.

## Rationale

The gross method assumes the customer will take the discount, and the discount is recorded only if actually taken.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording sales under the gross method
- **THEN** Record sale at full invoice amount
- **OTHERWISE** Under net method, record sale net of discount
