# Fixed Costs May Persist

## Statement

When discontinuing a product or segment, fixed costs may continue even after the segment is eliminated, potentially decreasing total company income.

## Rationale

Many fixed costs like depreciation, insurance, and property taxes cannot be eliminated by discontinuing a product.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Discontinuing a product or segment
- **THEN** Determine which fixed costs will persist and which will be eliminated
- **OTHERWISE** May incorrectly assume all costs will be eliminated
