# Separate Cashier and Accounting Rule

## Statement

The duties of the Cashier's Department (which handles cash) and the Accounting Department (which records cash) must be separated.

## Rationale

If Accounting Department employees both handle and record cash, they could steal cash and change accounting records to hide the theft.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Processing cash receipts or payments
- **THEN** Separate cash handling from cash recording duties
- **OTHERWISE** Employees could steal cash and manipulate records
