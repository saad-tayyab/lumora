# Variance Sign Convention

## Statement

A positive variance amount represents an unfavorable variance (increases costs, recorded as a debit). A negative variance amount represents a favorable variance (decreases costs, recorded as a credit).

## Rationale

Standardized sign convention ensures consistent recording and interpretation of variances across the accounting system.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Actual cost exceeds standard cost
- **THEN** Variance is unfavorable (debit balance)
- **OTHERWISE** Variance is favorable (credit balance)
