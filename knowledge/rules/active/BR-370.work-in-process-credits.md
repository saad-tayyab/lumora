# Work in Process Credits

## Statement

Work in Process is decreased (credited) when jobs are completed, transferring the cost to Finished Goods.

## Rationale

Moves the cost of completed goods from production to finished inventory.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A job is completed
- **THEN** Credit Work in Process and debit Finished Goods
- **OTHERWISE** Completed goods remain in WIP and finished goods is understated
