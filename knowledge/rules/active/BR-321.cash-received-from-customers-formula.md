# Cash Received from Customers Formula

## Statement

Cash received from customers equals sales revenue minus an increase in accounts receivable (or plus a decrease in accounts receivable).

## Rationale

An increase in accounts receivable means some sales were on credit and not yet collected in cash; a decrease means more cash was collected than sales recorded.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing cash received from customers under the direct method
- **THEN** Cash received = Sales - Increase in A/R (or + Decrease in A/R)
- **OTHERWISE** Cash received from customers will be incorrect
