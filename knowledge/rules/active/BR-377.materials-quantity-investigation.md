# Materials Quantity Investigation

## Statement

If materials quantity for a job exceeds expectations while price remains constant, investigate causes such as untrained employees, poor quality materials, defective equipment, or incorrect instructions.

## Rationale

Materials waste increases costs without adding value; root causes must be addressed.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Materials quantity exceeds expectations
- **THEN** Investigate training, supplier quality, equipment condition, and instructions
- **OTHERWISE** Excessive waste continues
