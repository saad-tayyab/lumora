# Bond Face Amount Repayment at Maturity

## Statement

At maturity, the full face amount of the bonds must be repaid to bondholders, regardless of any unamortized discount or premium (which should be zero at maturity).

## Rationale

The face amount is the contractual obligation to be repaid at the end of the bond term.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** The bond reaches its maturity date
- **THEN** Debit Bonds Payable and credit Cash for the face amount
- **OTHERWISE** The company defaults on its obligation
