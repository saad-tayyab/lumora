# Direct Labor Time Variance Computation Rule

## Statement

Direct Labor Time Variance = (Actual Hours - Standard Hours Allowed) × Standard Rate.

## Rationale

Separating the time variance allows the Production Department to be held accountable for labor efficiency.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating direct labor cost variance
- **THEN** Compute time variance using standard rate and the difference between actual and standard hours
- **OTHERWISE** Labor efficiency cannot be isolated from rate effects
