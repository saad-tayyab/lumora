# Prenumbered Documents Rule

## Statement

Documents used for authorization and approval should be prenumbered, accounted for, and safeguarded.

## Rationale

Helps prevent transactions from being recorded more than once or not at all. Helps prevent fraudulent transactions from being recorded.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using documents for authorization and approval
- **THEN** Prenumber, account for, and safeguard all documents
- **OTHERWISE** Transactions may be duplicated, omitted, or fraudulent
