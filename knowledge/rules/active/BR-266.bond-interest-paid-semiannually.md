# Bond Interest Paid Semiannually

## Statement

Most corporate bonds pay interest semiannually, with each payment equal to face value multiplied by half the annual contract rate.

## Rationale

Semiannual payments are the standard convention in the bond market, providing regular income to investors.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A bond pays interest semiannually
- **THEN** Each payment equals face value × contract rate × 1/2
- **OTHERWISE** Alternative payment schedules may exist (e.g., quarterly, annually)
