# DuPont Formula Decomposition

## Statement

ROI can be decomposed into Profit Margin multiplied by Investment Turnover, where Profit Margin equals Operating Income divided by Sales and Investment Turnover equals Sales divided by Invested Assets.

## Rationale

The DuPont formula helps identify whether ROI differences are driven by profitability (margin) or asset efficiency (turnover), enabling targeted improvement strategies.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing what drives ROI differences between divisions
- **THEN** Decompose ROI using DuPont formula to identify margin vs turnover contributions
- **OTHERWISE** Use basic ROI calculation
