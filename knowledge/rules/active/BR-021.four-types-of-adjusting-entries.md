# Four Types of Adjusting Entries

## Statement

Adjusting entries fall into four categories: accrue revenues, accrue expenses, defer revenues, and defer expenses.

## Rationale

These four categories cover all situations where cash and revenue/expense recognition timing differ.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing adjusting entries
- **THEN** Classify each adjustment as one of the four types
- **OTHERWISE** Adjustments may be incorrect or incomplete
