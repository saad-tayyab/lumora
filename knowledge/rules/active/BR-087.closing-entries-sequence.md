# Closing Entries Sequence

## Statement

Closing entries must transfer all revenue, expense, and drawing account balances to the owner's capital account, in sequence: close revenues to Income Summary, close expenses to Income Summary, close Income Summary to Capital, close Drawing to Capital.

## Rationale

To reset temporary account balances to zero for the next period and update owner's capital with net income or loss and withdrawals.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** At the end of each accounting period
- **THEN** Journalize and post closing entries in the prescribed sequence
- **OTHERWISE** Temporary accounts carry incorrect balances into the next period
