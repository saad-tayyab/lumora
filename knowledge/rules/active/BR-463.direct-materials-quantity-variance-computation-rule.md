# Direct Materials Quantity Variance Computation Rule

## Statement

Direct Materials Quantity Variance = (Actual Quantity Used - Standard Quantity Allowed) × Standard Price.

## Rationale

Separating the quantity variance allows the Production Department to be held accountable for material usage efficiency.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating direct materials cost variance
- **THEN** Compute quantity variance using standard price and the difference between actual and standard quantities
- **OTHERWISE** Usage efficiency cannot be isolated from price effects
