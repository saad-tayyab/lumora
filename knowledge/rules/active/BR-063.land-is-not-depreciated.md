# Land Is Not Depreciated

## Statement

Land is never depreciated because it has an indefinite useful life and does not wear out, become obsolete, or get consumed over time.

## Rationale

Depreciation represents the allocation of an asset's cost over its useful life. Since land does not have a limited useful life, there is no basis for allocating its cost.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Adjusting entries are prepared at period end
- **THEN** No depreciation entry is made for Land
- **OTHERWISE** Land would be incorrectly reduced on the balance sheet
