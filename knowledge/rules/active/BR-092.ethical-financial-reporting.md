# Ethical Financial Reporting

## Statement

Financial statements must present fairly and accurately the financial position of the entity. Misclassification of items (e.g., personal loans as trade receivables) is unethical and violates GAAP.

## Rationale

Stakeholders rely on financial statements for decision-making. Misrepresentation undermines trust and can lead to legal consequences.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Financial statements are prepared
- **THEN** All items must be accurately classified and disclosed
- **OTHERWISE** Misleading financial reporting, potential legal and ethical violations
