# Year-End Adjusting Entries

## Statement

Accounts such as depreciation, prepaid expenses, accrued liabilities, and deferred revenues normally require year-end adjusting entries to properly match revenues and expenses.

## Rationale

Accrual accounting requires adjusting entries to ensure financial statements reflect the correct amounts at period-end.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** At fiscal year-end before financial statements are prepared
- **THEN** Record adjusting entries for depreciation, accruals, deferrals, and estimates
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements would not accurately reflect the company's financial position and results
