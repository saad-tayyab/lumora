# Conversion Costs Definition

## Statement

Conversion costs equal direct labor plus manufacturing overhead.

## Rationale

Measures the total cost of converting raw materials into finished products.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Calculating total conversion costs
- **THEN** Sum direct labor cost and manufacturing overhead
- **OTHERWISE** Conversion costs cannot be determined
