# Owner Investments Increase Owner Equity

## Statement

When an owner invests personal assets into the business, the investment is credited to the owner's capital account, increasing total owner's equity.

## Rationale

Capital accounts track owner contributions. Investments increase the owner's claim on business assets.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An owner contributes cash or other assets to the business
- **THEN** Debit the asset received and credit the owner's capital account
- **OTHERWISE** Owner's equity will be understated and asset balances incorrect
