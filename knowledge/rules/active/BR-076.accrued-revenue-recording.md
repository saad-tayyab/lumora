# Accrued Revenue Recording

## Statement

Revenue earned but not yet billed or received must be recorded at period end via an adjusting entry.

## Rationale

Ensures all revenue earned during the period is reflected in the income statement.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Services have been performed or goods delivered but not yet billed or paid
- **THEN** An adjusting entry debits accounts receivable and credits the revenue account
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue is understated and assets are understated
