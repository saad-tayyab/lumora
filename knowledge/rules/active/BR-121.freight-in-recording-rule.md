# Freight In Recording Rule

## Statement

Freight paid when purchasing merchandise FOB shipping point is debited to Freight In, Transportation In, or a similar account.

## Rationale

Transportation costs are part of the cost of acquiring inventory and must be included in the total cost of merchandise purchased.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Purchasing merchandise with FOB shipping point terms and paying freight charges
- **THEN** Debit Freight In account
- **OTHERWISE** If FOB destination, the seller pays freight and buyer does not record freight costs
