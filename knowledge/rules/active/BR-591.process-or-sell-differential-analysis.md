# Process or Sell Differential Analysis

## Statement

When deciding whether to sell at an intermediate point or process further, compare the incremental revenue from further processing against the incremental cost of further processing.

## Rationale

Ensures that processing decisions are based on relevant incremental costs and revenues only.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A product can be sold at an intermediate stage or processed further
- **THEN** Process further if incremental revenue exceeds incremental cost; otherwise sell at split-off
- **OTHERWISE** Do not process further if it reduces profit
