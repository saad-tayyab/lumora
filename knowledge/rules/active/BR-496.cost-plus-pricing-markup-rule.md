# Cost-Plus Pricing Markup Rule

## Statement

The markup must be sufficient to cover any costs not included in the cost amount plus the desired profit.

## Rationale

If the markup does not cover all excluded costs and the target profit, the selling price will be insufficient to achieve the desired return.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using any cost-plus pricing method (product cost, total cost, or variable cost)
- **THEN** Markup percentage = (Desired Profit + Costs not in cost amount) / Cost Amount
- **OTHERWISE** Price is set by market or other means
