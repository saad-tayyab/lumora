# Depreciation Does Not Provide Cash for Replacement

## Statement

Depreciation does not provide cash to replace fixed assets as they wear out. It is a non-cash expense that reduces net income.

## Rationale

Depreciation is an accounting allocation, not a cash flow event.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording depreciation expense
- **THEN** No cash is generated or set aside
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
