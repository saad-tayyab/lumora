# Standards for Nonmanufacturing Activities

## Statement

Direct labor time standards can be developed for administrative, selling, and service activities when tasks are repetitive and produce a common output.

## Rationale

Standard costing principles apply beyond manufacturing for any repetitive operation that can be measured and controlled.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Activity involves repetitive tasks with common output (e.g., processing sales orders, insurance claims)
- **THEN** Develop time standards for cost control
- **OTHERWISE** Standards are less appropriate for non-repetitive work
