# Discounted Note Interest May Use Discount Account

## Statement

Instead of debiting Interest Expense directly, a contra account 'Discount on Notes Payable' may be debited and subsequently adjusted for interest expense incurred. When the note is issued and due in the same period, it is simpler to debit Interest Expense directly.

## Rationale

The discount represents prepaid interest that should be amortized over the note's term.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Issuing a discounted note that spans multiple accounting periods
- **THEN** Debit Discount on Notes Payable (contra account) and adjust at period end
- **OTHERWISE** If issued and due in same period, debit Interest Expense directly
