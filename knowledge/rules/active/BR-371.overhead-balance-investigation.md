# Overhead Balance Investigation

## Statement

If the manufacturing overhead balance (under- or overapplied) becomes large, the balance and related overhead rate should be investigated for potential causes such as changes in manufacturing methods.

## Rationale

Large variances indicate the predetermined rate may need revision.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** The overhead balance is material
- **THEN** Investigate causes and consider revising the overhead rate
- **OTHERWISE** Persistent variances distort product costs
