# Purchase Order Total Calculation

## Statement

A purchase order's total must equal the sum of all line item amounts plus tax.

## Rationale

This ensures financial accuracy of purchase orders. The PO total must be consistent with its line items to prevent budget overruns and ensure accurate procurement records.

## Scope

- Applies to: All purchase orders (CON-PROC-002)
- Bounded Context: BC-PROC (Procurement)
- Entities: PurchaseOrder, POLineItem

## Conditions

- WHEN a purchase order is created or updated
- THEN total MUST equal sum of all line item amounts plus tax_amount
- OTHERWISE the purchase order is rejected with error `ERR_PO_TOTAL_MISMATCH`

## Pseudocode

```
function validatePurchaseOrderTotal(po):
    lineItemTotal = sum(po.lineItems.map(item => item.amount))
    expectedTotal = lineItemTotal + po.tax_amount
    
    if po.total != expectedTotal:
        throw POTotalMismatchError(po.id, po.total, expectedTotal)
```

## Exceptions

- None — this rule has no exceptions.
