# Capitalize Only Necessary Costs

## Statement

Only costs necessary for preparing a fixed asset for its intended use are capitalized as part of the asset's cost. Unnecessary costs such as vandalism, installation errors, uninsured theft, damage during unpacking, and fines are expensed immediately.

## Rationale

Ensures the asset account reflects only its true acquisition cost and avoids inflating asset values with losses.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Acquiring a fixed asset
- **THEN** Capitalize purchase price, freight, installation, and testing; expense errors, damage, fines, and theft
- **OTHERWISE** Asset values are overstated and expenses are understated
