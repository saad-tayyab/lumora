# Revenue Recognition Principle

## Statement

Revenue must be recorded in the period when it is earned, regardless of when cash is received.

## Rationale

Ensures that financial statements reflect the true economic activity of the period, following accrual-basis accounting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A service is performed or goods are delivered to a customer
- **THEN** Revenue is recognized and recorded in that period
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue and income will be misstated
