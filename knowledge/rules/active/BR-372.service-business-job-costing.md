# Service Business Job Costing

## Statement

Service businesses use job order costing with direct labor and overhead as primary costs. Completed job costs transfer from Work in Process to Cost of Services.

## Rationale

Adapts job order costing principles to service industries where the 'product' is a service.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A service job is completed
- **THEN** Transfer costs from WIP to Cost of Services
- **OTHERWISE** Service costs are not properly matched with revenue
