# Contra-Purchases Account Credit Rule

## Statement

Purchases Discounts and Purchases Returns and Allowances are increased by credits and decreased by debits, with normal credit balances.

## Rationale

These are contra accounts to Purchases and reduce the net cost of merchandise purchased. They increase with credits opposite to the Purchases account.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording purchase returns, allowances, or discounts in the periodic system
- **THEN** Credit the respective contra-purchases account
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
