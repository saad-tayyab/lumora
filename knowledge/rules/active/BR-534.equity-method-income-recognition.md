# Equity Method - Income Recognition

## Statement

Under the equity method (20%-50% ownership), the investor records its proportional share of the investee's net income as an increase to the investment account and as investment income.

## Rationale

The investor has significant influence, so it recognizes its share of the investee's earnings as they are earned.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Investee reports net income and investor uses equity method
- **THEN** Debit Investment account and credit Equity Income for investor's share
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
