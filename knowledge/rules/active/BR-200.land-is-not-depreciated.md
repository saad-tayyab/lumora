# Land Is Not Depreciated

## Statement

Land has an unlimited life and should not be depreciated.

## Rationale

Depreciation allocates cost over useful life; land does not lose its ability to provide services over time.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A fixed asset is land
- **THEN** No depreciation expense is recorded
- **OTHERWISE** Depreciate the asset over its useful life
