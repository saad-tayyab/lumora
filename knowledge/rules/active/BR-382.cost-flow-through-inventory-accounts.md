# Cost Flow Through Inventory Accounts

## Statement

Costs flow from Materials → Work in Process → Finished Goods → Cost of Goods Sold. Each transfer is recorded as a journal entry.

## Rationale

Tracks the physical flow of goods through the manufacturing process using financial accounts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Materials are requisitioned for a job
- **THEN** Debit Work in Process, Credit Materials
- **OTHERWISE** No entry
