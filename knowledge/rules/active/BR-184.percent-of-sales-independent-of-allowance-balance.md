# Percent of Sales Independent of Allowance Balance

## Statement

Under the percent of sales method, the bad debt expense adjusting entry amount equals the estimated percentage of credit sales, regardless of the existing balance in Allowance for Doubtful Accounts.

## Rationale

Focuses on matching bad debt expense to the revenue that generated it, rather than targeting a specific allowance ending balance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using percent of sales method for bad debt estimation
- **THEN** Bad Debt Expense = Credit Sales × Bad Debt % (allowance balance is irrelevant to the adjusting entry)
- **OTHERWISE** Expense would not properly match the period's credit sales
