# Cost of Goods Manufactured Calculation

## Statement

Cost of Goods Manufactured = Direct Materials Used + Direct Labor + Manufacturing Overhead + Beginning Work in Process - Ending Work in Process.

## Rationale

Determines the total cost of goods completed during the period for the income statement.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the statement of cost of goods manufactured
- **THEN** Sum all manufacturing costs and adjust for WIP inventory changes
- **OTHERWISE** The cost of completed goods is not properly determined
