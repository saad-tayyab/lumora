# Liabilities Paid Before Partners

## Statement

Partnership creditors must be paid in full before any cash is distributed to partners during liquidation.

## Rationale

Creditors have priority claims over partners for partnership assets, as required by partnership law.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Partnership is being liquidated
- **THEN** Pay all liabilities in full before distributing cash to partners
- **OTHERWISE** Creditors' legal rights would be violated
