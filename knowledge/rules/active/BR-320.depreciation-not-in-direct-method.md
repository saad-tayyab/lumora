# Depreciation Not in Direct Method

## Statement

Depreciation expense is excluded from the direct method calculation because it does not affect cash. It is only used as an adjustment in the indirect method.

## Rationale

The direct method reports actual cash receipts and payments, so noncash expenses are irrelevant.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing cash paid for operating expenses under the direct method
- **THEN** Exclude depreciation expense from the calculation
- **OTHERWISE** Cash paid for operating expenses will be overstated
