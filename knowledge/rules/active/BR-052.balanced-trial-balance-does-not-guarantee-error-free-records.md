# Balanced Trial Balance Does Not Guarantee Error-Free Records

## Statement

A balanced trial balance proves mathematical equality of debits and credits but does not guarantee the absence of all errors. Errors of omission, commission, principle, or original entry can exist even when totals match.

## Rationale

Several types of errors do not affect the equality of debits and credits. A balanced trial balance is a necessary but not sufficient condition for accuracy.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** The trial balance totals are equal
- **THEN** Debit-credit equality is confirmed, but other error checks are still needed
- **OTHERWISE** A mathematical or posting error exists and must be found
