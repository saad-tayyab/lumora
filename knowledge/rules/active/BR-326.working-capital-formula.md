# Working Capital Formula

## Statement

Working capital is computed as current assets minus current liabilities.

## Rationale

Working capital indicates the short-term liquidity cushion available to meet obligations as they come due.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating a company's current position
- **THEN** Working capital = Current assets - Current liabilities
- **OTHERWISE** The absolute measure of short-term liquidity is unknown
