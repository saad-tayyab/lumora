# Weighted Average Cost Computation

## Statement

Under the weighted average method, compute the average cost per unit by dividing total cost of goods available for sale by total units available for sale. Apply this average cost to both units sold and units in ending inventory.

## Rationale

The weighted average method provides a compromise between FIFO and LIFO, smoothing out cost fluctuations.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using weighted average inventory cost flow method
- **THEN** Compute average cost per unit and apply to all units
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
