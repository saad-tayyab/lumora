# ROI Computation

## Statement

Return on Investment (ROI) = Operating Income / Invested Assets. Higher ROI indicates more effective use of assets to generate income.

## Rationale

ROI provides a common basis for comparing divisions of different sizes by measuring income per dollar invested.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating investment center performance
- **THEN** Compute ROI and compare across divisions and over time
- **OTHERWISE** Use operating income alone for profit centers
