# Free Cash Flow Computation

## Statement

Free cash flow is computed as net cash flows from operating activities minus cash used to purchase property, plant, and equipment. It can be expressed as a percentage of sales for comparison purposes.

## Rationale

Free cash flow indicates how much cash is available after maintaining current operations, which is useful for evaluating a company's financial flexibility.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating a company's operating cash flow sufficiency
- **THEN** Compute free cash flow = Operating cash flow - Capital expenditures
- **OTHERWISE** No measure of excess operating cash flow is available
