# Residual Income Computation

## Statement

Residual Income = Operating Income - (Invested Assets x Minimum Rate of Return). A positive residual income indicates the division earned more than the minimum required return.

## Rationale

Residual income克服了ROI的一个缺点：它鼓励 managers to accept any project that earns above the minimum rate, whereas ROI may discourage projects that lower the division's overall ROI even if they are profitable.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating investment center performance with a minimum required return
- **THEN** Compute residual income; accept projects with positive residual income
- **OTHERWISE** Use ROI for comparison across divisions
