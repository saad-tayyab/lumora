# Comparative Statement Base Year Selection

## Statement

When preparing horizontal analysis, use the earlier year as the base year and express changes as both dollar amounts and percentages.

## Rationale

Base year selection determines the direction and magnitude of percentage changes, providing consistent trend analysis.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing horizontal analysis of financial statements
- **THEN** Use the earlier year as the base year
- **OTHERWISE** Percentage changes are inverted and misleading
