# Rules of Debit and Credit

## Statement

Assets and expenses are increased by debits and decreased by credits. Liabilities, owner's equity, and revenue are increased by credits and decreased by debits.

## Rationale

These rules ensure consistent recording of transactions and that the accounting equation remains in balance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording any transaction
- **THEN** Apply the appropriate debit and credit rules based on the account type
- **OTHERWISE** Transaction will be recorded incorrectly
