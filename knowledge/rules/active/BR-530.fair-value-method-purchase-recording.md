# Fair Value Method - Purchase Recording

## Statement

Under the fair value method, equity investments of less than 20% ownership are initially recorded at cost.

## Rationale

Cost provides the most reliable measure of the initial investment value.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Purchasing equity securities representing less than 20% ownership
- **THEN** Debit Investments account and credit Cash at purchase price
- **OTHERWISE** Apply equity method or consolidation
