# Cash Paid for Operating Expenses Formula

## Statement

Cash paid for operating expenses equals operating expenses (excluding depreciation) plus an increase in prepaid expenses (or minus a decrease) plus a decrease in accrued expenses payable (or minus an increase).

## Rationale

Prepaid expenses represent cash paid before expense recognition; accrued expenses payable represent expense recognition before cash payment.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing cash paid for operating expenses under the direct method
- **THEN** Cash paid = Operating expenses (excl. depreciation) + Increase in prepaid - Decrease in prepaid + Decrease in accrued - Increase in accrued
- **OTHERWISE** Cash paid for operating expenses will be incorrect
