# Bill Approval Dual Approvers

## Statement

Vendor bills between the single_approver_threshold and dual_approver_threshold require two authorized approvers before they can be paid.

## Rationale

This ensures appropriate oversight for medium-value bills. Dual approval provides an additional layer of verification, reducing the risk of unauthorized or erroneous payments.

## Scope

- Applies to: All vendor bills (CON-AP-003) between single and dual thresholds
- Bounded Context: BC-AP (Accounts Payable)
- Entities: Bill, ApprovalWorkflow

## Conditions

- WHEN a vendor bill amount is greater than single_approver_threshold AND less than or equal to dual_approver_threshold
- THEN the bill requires exactly two authorized approvers
- OTHERWISE the bill cannot transition to 'approved' status

## Pseudocode

```
function approveBill(bill, approver, policy, approvalCount):
    if bill.total_amount > policy.single_approver_threshold AND bill.total_amount <= policy.dual_approver_threshold:
        if approvalCount >= 2 AND allApproversAuthorized():
            bill.status = 'approved'
            bill.approved_at = currentTimestamp()
        else:
            throw InsufficientApproversError(bill.id, approvalCount, 2)
    else:
        throw BillThresholdMismatchError(bill.id)
```

## Exceptions

- None — this rule has no exceptions.
