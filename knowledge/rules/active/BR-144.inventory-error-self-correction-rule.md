# Inventory Error Self-Correction Rule

## Statement

An inventory error in one period will self-correct over two consecutive periods, but the individual period financial statements remain incorrect for each period.

## Rationale

Ending inventory of period 1 becomes beginning inventory of period 2, causing offsetting errors in COGS across periods.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Ending inventory is misstated in period 1
- **THEN** Period 1 COGS and net income are misstated; Period 2 COGS and net income are misstated in the opposite direction
- **OTHERWISE** No correction occurs
