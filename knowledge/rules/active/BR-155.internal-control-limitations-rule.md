# Internal Control Limitations Rule

## Statement

Internal control systems can provide only reasonable assurance, not absolute assurance, for safeguarding assets, processing accurate information, and compliance with laws and regulations.

## Rationale

Limitations include the human element (errors, fatigue, collusion) and cost-benefit considerations where control costs should not exceed benefits.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating internal control effectiveness
- **THEN** Recognize that controls provide reasonable, not absolute, assurance
- **OTHERWISE** Overreliance on controls could lead to undetected problems
