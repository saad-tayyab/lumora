# Owner's Drawing Reduces Equity

## Statement

Owner withdrawals (drawing) decrease owner's equity but are not reported on the income statement as an expense.

## Rationale

Drawings represent distributions to the owner, not costs of doing business.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Owner withdraws cash or other assets for personal use
- **THEN** Record as a decrease in owner's equity (drawing account)
- **OTHERWISE** Expenses would be overstated and net income understated
