# Closing Entries for Retail Business

## Statement

The closing entries for a retail business are similar to a service business: close revenues and expenses (including COGS) to Income Summary, close Income Summary to Capital, and close Drawing to Capital.

## Rationale

Cost of Goods Sold is a temporary account and is closed like other expense accounts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** End of period for a retail business
- **THEN** Close all revenue accounts, all expense accounts (including COGS), and Drawing to Capital
- **OTHERWISE** Temporary accounts carry incorrect balances into the next period
