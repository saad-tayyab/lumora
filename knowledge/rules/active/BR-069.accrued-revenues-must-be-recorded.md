# Accrued Revenues Must Be Recorded

## Statement

At period end, revenues that have been earned but not yet billed or received must be recorded by creating a receivable and recognizing the revenue.

## Rationale

Revenue recognition requires recording income when earned, regardless of when cash is received. Accrued revenue ensures the income statement reflects all earned revenue.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Services have been performed or goods delivered but not yet billed or collected
- **THEN** Debit a receivable account and credit the appropriate revenue account
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue and assets are understated
