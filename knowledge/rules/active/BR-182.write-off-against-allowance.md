# Write-Off Against Allowance

## Statement

Under the allowance method, a specific account written off debits Allowance for Doubtful Accounts and credits Accounts Receivable. The net realizable value of receivables is not affected.

## Rationale

The estimated loss was already recognized as expense when the allowance was established. The write-off only reduces both the asset and contra-asset by the same amount.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A specific receivable is determined to be uncollectible
- **THEN** Debit Allowance for Doubtful Accounts, Credit Accounts Receivable
- **OTHERWISE** Net realizable value would be incorrect and the write-off would double-count the loss
