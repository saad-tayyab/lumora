# Revenue Per Employee Measures Efficiency

## Statement

Revenue per employee is a useful metric for comparing operational efficiency across firms in the same industry, with higher ratios generally indicating better productivity.

## Rationale

This metric normalizes revenue by workforce size, allowing meaningful comparisons between firms of different sizes.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing operational efficiency across firms
- **THEN** Compute revenue per employee as total revenue divided by number of employees
- **OTHERWISE** Raw revenue numbers may be misleading due to firm size differences
