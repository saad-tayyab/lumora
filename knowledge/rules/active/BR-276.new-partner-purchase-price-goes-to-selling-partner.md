# New Partner Purchase Price Goes to Selling Partner

## Statement

When a new partner is admitted by purchasing an interest from an existing partner, the purchase price is paid directly to the selling partner(s), not to the partnership.

## Rationale

The transaction is between the new partner and the selling partner personally; the partnership only records the transfer of capital.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A new partner purchases interest from an existing partner
- **THEN** Purchase price goes to the selling partner; only the capital transfer is recorded by the partnership
- **OTHERWISE** Partnership cash and capital would be misstated
