# Cash Dividend Declaration Creates Liability

## Statement

When the board of directors declares a cash dividend, a liability (Dividends Payable) is created and Retained Earnings (or Dividends account) is decreased.

## Rationale

The declaration creates a legal obligation to pay stockholders.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Board of directors declares a cash dividend
- **THEN** Debit Dividends (or Retained Earnings), credit Dividends Payable
- **OTHERWISE** No liability is recorded until declaration
