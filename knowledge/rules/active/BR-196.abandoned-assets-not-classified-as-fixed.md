# Abandoned Assets Not Classified as Fixed

## Statement

Fixed assets that have been abandoned or are no longer used in operations must be removed from the fixed assets classification.

## Rationale

Abandoned assets no longer meet the definition of fixed assets and should not be reported as such.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A fixed asset is abandoned or no longer used in operations
- **THEN** Remove from fixed assets classification and write off or reclassify
- **OTHERWISE** Fixed assets are overstated on the balance sheet
