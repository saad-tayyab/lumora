# Materials and Conversion Computed Separately

## Statement

Equivalent units for direct materials and conversion costs are computed separately because materials and conversion costs typically enter production at different times and rates.

## Rationale

Provides more accurate cost allocation when materials are added at different points than conversion costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing equivalent units in process costing
- **THEN** Compute materials and conversion equivalent units in separate calculations
- **OTHERWISE** Inaccurate cost allocation
