# Purchase Discount Reduces Inventory Cost

## Statement

When a buyer pays an invoice within the discount period, the discount is credited to Inventory, reducing the net cost of the merchandise.

## Rationale

The Inventory account should reflect the actual cost paid, including the benefit of early payment discounts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Payment is made within the discount period under the perpetual system
- **THEN** Credit Inventory for the discount amount
- **OTHERWISE** Inventory is overstated and the discount benefit is not captured
