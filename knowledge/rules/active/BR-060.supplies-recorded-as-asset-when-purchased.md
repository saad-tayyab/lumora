# Supplies Recorded as Asset When Purchased

## Statement

When supplies are purchased, they are initially recorded as an asset (Supplies). They are expensed only as they are consumed.

## Rationale

Supplies represent a future economic benefit until used. Recording them as assets initially follows the matching principle.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Supplies are purchased for business use
- **THEN** Debit the Supplies asset account for the purchase price
- **OTHERWISE** Expenses may be overstated in the purchase period and understated in usage periods
