# Margin of Safety Rule

## Statement

Margin of Safety = Actual Sales - Break-Even Sales. It measures how much sales can decline before losses occur.

## Rationale

Provides a risk measure indicating the cushion between current sales and the break-even point.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Assessing business risk
- **THEN** Calculate margin of safety in dollars and as a percentage of sales
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot evaluate the risk of losses from sales decline
