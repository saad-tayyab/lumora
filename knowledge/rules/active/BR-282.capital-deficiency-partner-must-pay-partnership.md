# Capital Deficiency Partner Must Pay Partnership

## Statement

A partner with a capital deficiency (debit balance) must pay the deficiency amount to the partnership to enable full distribution to other partners.

## Rationale

The deficient partner has a legal obligation to contribute enough to cover their share of losses.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A partner has a debit balance in their capital account after realization
- **THEN** The partner must pay the deficiency to the partnership
- **OTHERWISE** Other partners will receive less than their capital balances
