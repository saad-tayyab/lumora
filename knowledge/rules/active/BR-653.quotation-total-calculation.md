# Quotation Total Calculation

## Statement

A quotation's total must equal the sum of line item totals minus discounts plus tax.

## Rationale

This ensures financial accuracy of quotations. The total must be consistent with its components to provide accurate pricing information to customers.

## Scope

- Applies to: All quotations (CON-SALES-004)
- Bounded Context: BC-SALES (Sales & Orders)
- Entities: Quotation, QuotationLineItem

## Conditions

- WHEN a quotation is created or updated
- THEN total MUST equal sum of line item totals minus discount_amount plus tax_amount
- OTHERWISE the quotation is rejected with error `ERR_QUOTATION_TOTAL_MISMATCH`

## Pseudocode

```
function validateQuotationTotal(quotation):
    lineItemTotal = sum(quotation.lineItems.map(item => item.amount))
    expectedTotal = lineItemTotal - (quotation.discount_amount || 0) + quotation.tax_amount
    
    if quotation.total != expectedTotal:
        throw QuotationTotalMismatchError(quotation.id, quotation.total, expectedTotal)
```

## Exceptions

- None — this rule has no exceptions.
