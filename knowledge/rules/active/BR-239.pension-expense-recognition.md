# Pension Expense Recognition

## Statement

For defined contribution plans, pension expense equals the employer's contribution amount and is recorded when the obligation is incurred.

## Rationale

Matches the cost of employee retirement benefits to the period the services are rendered.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A defined contribution pension plan requires quarterly or annual contributions
- **THEN** Debit Pension Expense and credit Cash or Pension Payable
- **OTHERWISE** The obligation is not reflected in the financial statements
