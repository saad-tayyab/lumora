# Petty Cash Replenishment Rule

## Statement

When replenishing a petty cash fund, the Petty Cash account is NOT debited or credited. Instead, only the accounts affected by the petty cash receipts and any Cash Short and Over are recorded. A check is written for the amount needed to restore the fund.

## Rationale

The Petty Cash account balance only changes when the fund is initially established, increased, decreased, or eliminated.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Replenishing a petty cash fund
- **THEN** Debit expense accounts from receipts, credit Cash; do not debit/credit Petty Cash
- **OTHERWISE** Petty Cash account balance would be incorrectly changed
