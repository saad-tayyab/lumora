# No Gain Recognized Without Commercial Substance

## Statement

If an exchange of similar assets lacks commercial substance, no gain is recognized. Instead, the cost of the new asset is reduced by the unrecognized gain.

## Rationale

Without commercial substance, the transaction is not sufficiently different from the old asset to justify gain recognition.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Exchange of similar assets lacks commercial substance AND trade-in allowance > book value
- **THEN** New asset recorded at cost minus unrecognized gain
- **OTHERWISE** If commercial substance exists, recognize the gain
