# Asset Classification Three-Step Test

## Statement

To classify a purchased cost: (1) Does it provide a future benefit? If no, expense it. If yes, proceed. (2) Is it long-lived? If no, classify as current asset. If yes, proceed. (3) Is it used in normal operations? If yes, classify as fixed or intangible asset. If no, classify as investment.

## Rationale

Ensures proper financial statement classification of all acquired costs based on their nature and use.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording a purchased cost
- **THEN** Apply three-step classification: future benefit → long-lived → used in operations
- **OTHERWISE** Asset may be misclassified, leading to incorrect financial statements
