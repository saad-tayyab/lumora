# Overtime Premium Rate

## Statement

Sales personnel and hourly employees must be paid 1.5 times the regular hourly rate for all hours worked in excess of 40 hours per week.

## Rationale

Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA) requires overtime premium pay for non-exempt employees.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An hourly employee works more than 40 hours in a week
- **THEN** Overtime hours are paid at 1.5 × regular rate
- **OTHERWISE** Standard hourly rate applies
