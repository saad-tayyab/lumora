# Cash Distributed Based on Capital Balances

## Statement

Upon liquidation, cash is distributed to partners based on their capital account balances after realization gains/losses have been allocated and liabilities have been paid, not based on the income-sharing ratio.

## Rationale

Each partner has a legal claim to their capital balance; the income-sharing ratio determines how gains/losses are divided, not how cash is distributed.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Partnership is being liquidated and cash is available for distribution
- **THEN** Distribute cash to partners in proportion to their final capital account balances
- **OTHERWISE** Partners would not receive their rightful share of remaining equity
