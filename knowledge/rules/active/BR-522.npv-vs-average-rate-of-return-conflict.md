# NPV vs Average Rate of Return Conflict

## Statement

When a project has a higher average rate of return but lower NPV than an alternative, the NPV method is generally preferred because it accounts for the time value of money and the timing of cash flows.

## Rationale

NPV considers when cash flows occur, while average rate of return treats all years equally. Earlier cash flows are more valuable due to time value of money.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Two projects have conflicting rankings under ARR vs NPV
- **THEN** Prefer the project with higher NPV
- **OTHERWISE** Both methods agree on project ranking
