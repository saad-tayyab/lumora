# Prepaid Insurance Recorded as Asset

## Statement

An insurance policy premium paid in advance is initially recorded as an asset (Prepaid Insurance) and expensed monthly over the coverage period.

## Rationale

Prepaid insurance represents future economic benefit (coverage). The cost is allocated over the periods benefited.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An insurance premium is paid covering future periods
- **THEN** Debit Prepaid Insurance for the full premium amount
- **OTHERWISE** Expenses will be overstated in the payment period and understated in subsequent periods
