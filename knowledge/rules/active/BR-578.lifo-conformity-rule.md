# LIFO Conformity Rule

## Statement

If LIFO is used for tax purposes, it must also be used for financial reporting.

## Rationale

IRS LIFO conformity rule prevents companies from using different inventory methods for tax and book purposes.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** LIFO is used for tax reporting
- **THEN** LIFO must also be used for financial statement reporting
- **OTHERWISE** Tax benefits from LIFO may be lost
