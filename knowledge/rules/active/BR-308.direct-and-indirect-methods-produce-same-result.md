# Direct and Indirect Methods Produce Same Result

## Statement

Both the direct and indirect methods must report the same amount of net cash flows from operating activities.

## Rationale

They are different presentations of the same underlying cash flow data.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the statement of cash flows using either method
- **THEN** Net cash flows from operating activities must be identical under both methods
- **OTHERWISE** An error exists
