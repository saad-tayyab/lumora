# Purchase Returns and Allowances Reduce Inventory

## Statement

Under the perpetual inventory system, purchase returns and allowances are recorded by debiting Accounts Payable and crediting Inventory.

## Rationale

Returns and allowances reduce both the amount owed and the cost of inventory on hand.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Merchandise is returned or a price allowance is granted
- **THEN** Debit Accounts Payable, credit Inventory
- **OTHERWISE** Both Accounts Payable and Inventory are overstated
