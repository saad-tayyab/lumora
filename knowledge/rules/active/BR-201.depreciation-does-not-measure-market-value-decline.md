# Depreciation Does Not Measure Market Value Decline

## Statement

Depreciation is an allocation of cost to expense, not a measurement of decline in market value. Book value (cost less accumulated depreciation) usually does not agree with market value.

## Rationale

Fixed assets are held for use in operations, not for resale; historical cost allocation is the appropriate accounting treatment.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing depreciation for financial reporting
- **THEN** Depreciation allocates historical cost, not fair value
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
