# Cash Payback Period Calculation

## Statement

When annual net cash flows are equal, the cash payback period = Amount to Be Invested / Annual Net Cash Flow.

## Rationale

The payback period measures liquidity and risk by showing how quickly an investment recovers its cost.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Annual net cash flows are equal each year
- **THEN** Use simple division to calculate payback period
- **OTHERWISE** Use cumulative cash flow approach for unequal cash flows
