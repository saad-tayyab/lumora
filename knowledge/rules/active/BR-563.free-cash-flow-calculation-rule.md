# Free Cash Flow Calculation Rule

## Statement

Free cash flow equals cash flow from operations minus capital expenditures minus dividends paid.

## Rationale

Represents the actual cash available for discretionary use after maintaining and growing the business.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating a company's financial flexibility and liquidity
- **THEN** Compute free cash flow as cash flow from operations less capital expenditures less dividends
- **OTHERWISE** The measure of discretionary cash availability is inaccurate
