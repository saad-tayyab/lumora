# Daily Cash Deposit

## Statement

All cash receipts must be deposited in the bank's night depository at the end of each day.

## Rationale

Minimizes the risk of theft or loss by reducing the amount of cash held on premises.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** End of business day
- **THEN** All cash receipts must be deposited
- **OTHERWISE** Increased risk of theft and misappropriation
