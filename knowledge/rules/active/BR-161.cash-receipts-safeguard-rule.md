# Cash Receipts Safeguard Rule

## Statement

Cash must be controlled from the time it is received until it is deposited in a bank. Cash on hand should be kept in a cash register or safe.

## Rationale

Cash is the asset most likely to be stolen or used improperly. Physical safeguards reduce theft risk.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Handling physical cash
- **THEN** Store in cash register or safe; deposit promptly in bank
- **OTHERWISE** Cash is vulnerable to theft
