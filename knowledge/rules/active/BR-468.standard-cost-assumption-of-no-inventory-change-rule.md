# Standard Cost Assumption of No Inventory Change Rule

## Statement

When simplifying direct materials variance analysis, assume no change in beginning and ending materials inventories so that materials purchased equals materials used.

## Rationale

This simplification allows the price variance to be computed on the same quantity as the quantity variance, avoiding timing differences.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing direct materials variances for educational or simplified analysis
- **THEN** Assume materials purchased = materials used (no inventory change)
- **OTHERWISE** Price variance should be computed on quantity purchased, and quantity variance on quantity used
