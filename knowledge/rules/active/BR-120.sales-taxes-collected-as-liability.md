# Sales Taxes Collected as Liability

## Statement

Sales taxes are collected by the seller at the time of sale and recorded as a liability (Sales Tax Payable), not as revenue.

## Rationale

Sales tax collected belongs to the state and must be remitted to the taxing authority.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A sale is made subject to sales tax
- **THEN** Credit Sales Tax Payable for the tax amount
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue is overstated and the tax liability is not recorded
