# Cost Principle

## Statement

Assets should be recorded at their actual cost at the time of acquisition, not at market value or appraised value.

## Rationale

Provides objective, verifiable, and consistent measurement of assets across businesses and time periods.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An asset is acquired by the business
- **THEN** Record the asset at its historical cost (purchase price plus necessary expenditures)
- **OTHERWISE** Asset values would be subjective and inconsistent
