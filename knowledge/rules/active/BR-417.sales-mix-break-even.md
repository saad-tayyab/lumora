# Sales Mix Break-Even

## Statement

When selling multiple products, the break-even point depends on the sales mix. Use weighted average contribution margin based on the relative sales proportions of each product.

## Rationale

Different products have different contribution margins; the mix affects the overall break-even point.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing break-even for a multi-product company
- **THEN** Calculate weighted average CM based on sales mix proportions
- **OTHERWISE** Break-even point will be inaccurate
