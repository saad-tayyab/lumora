# Investigating Cost Variances

## Statement

When job costs differ significantly from estimates or prior jobs, investigate the causes. For materials, check quantity used and price paid. For labor, check hours worked and rate paid.

## Rationale

Root cause analysis enables corrective action to prevent future cost overruns.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A job shows significant cost variance
- **THEN** Investigate materials quantity/price and labor hours/rate
- **OTHERWISE** Cost problems recur without corrective action
