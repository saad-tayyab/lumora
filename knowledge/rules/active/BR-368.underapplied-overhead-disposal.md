# Underapplied Overhead Disposal

## Statement

If there is a debit balance (underapplied) in Manufacturing Overhead at year-end, debit Cost of Goods Sold and credit Manufacturing Overhead to close it.

## Rationale

Underapplied overhead means actual costs were higher than applied; COGS must increase.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Manufacturing Overhead has a debit balance at year-end
- **THEN** Debit COGS, credit Manufacturing Overhead
- **OTHERWISE** COGS is understated and assets are overstated
