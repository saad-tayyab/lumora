# Operating Leverage Interpretation

## Statement

A company with higher operating leverage will experience greater percentage changes in operating income for a given percentage change in sales.

## Rationale

Operating leverage measures the sensitivity of operating income to sales changes; higher leverage means more fixed costs relative to variable costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing companies or periods with different cost structures
- **THEN** The company with higher operating leverage has higher risk but also higher potential reward from sales increases
- **OTHERWISE** Misjudging risk exposure from sales volatility
