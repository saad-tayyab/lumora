# Separate Related Operations Rule

## Statement

The responsibility for related operations should be divided among two or more people to decrease the possibility of errors and fraud.

## Rationale

Prevents abuses such as placing orders based on friendship, not verifying receipt quality, stealing supplies, and paying false invoices.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Multiple related operations exist (e.g., ordering, receiving, paying)
- **THEN** Divide responsibilities among different people or departments
- **OTHERWISE** Single person could manipulate the entire process
