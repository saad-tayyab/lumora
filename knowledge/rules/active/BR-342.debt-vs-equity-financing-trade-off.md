# Debt vs Equity Financing Trade-off

## Statement

Using only equity financing and avoiding all debt is not optimal because debt provides tax advantages (interest is tax-deductible) and can amplify returns through leverage.

## Rationale

Leverage increases return on equity when return on assets exceeds the cost of debt, though it also increases risk.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Deciding between debt and equity financing
- **THEN** Consider both the tax benefits of debt and the risk of financial distress
- **OTHERWISE** May over-restrict financing options or take on excessive risk
