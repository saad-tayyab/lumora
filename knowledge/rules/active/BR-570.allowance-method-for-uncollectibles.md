# Allowance Method for Uncollectibles

## Statement

Uncollectible accounts must be estimated and recorded using the allowance method, not the direct write-off method, to match expenses with revenues.

## Rationale

Matching principle requires estimating bad debt expense in the same period as the related revenue.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Accounts receivable exist that may become uncollectible
- **THEN** Estimate and record an allowance for doubtful accounts at period end
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue is overstated and expenses understated in the period of sale
