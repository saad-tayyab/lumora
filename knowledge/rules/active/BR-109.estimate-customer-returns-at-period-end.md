# Estimate Customer Returns at Period End

## Statement

At the end of the accounting period, a seller must estimate the amount of returns, refunds, and allowances that may be issued to customers in the future, and record two adjusting entries.

## Rationale

Ensures revenue is not overstated and liabilities are properly recognized for expected future customer refunds.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** End of accounting period for a seller with return/allowance policy
- **THEN** Record two adjusting entries: (1) Debit Sales, credit Customer Refunds Payable; (2) Debit Estimated Returns Inventory, credit COGS
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue overstated, liabilities understated, and COGS overstated
