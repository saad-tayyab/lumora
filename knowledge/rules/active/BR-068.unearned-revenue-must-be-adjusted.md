# Unearned Revenue Must Be Adjusted

## Statement

At period end, unearned revenue must be adjusted to recognize the portion that has been earned during the period, reducing the liability and recording revenue.

## Rationale

Cash received before services are performed creates a liability. As services are performed, the liability decreases and revenue is recognized.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A portion of unearned revenue has been earned by performing services
- **THEN** Debit the Unearned Revenue liability and credit the Revenue account
- **OTHERWISE** Liabilities are overstated and revenue is understated
