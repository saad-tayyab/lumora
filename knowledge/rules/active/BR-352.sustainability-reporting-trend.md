# Sustainability Reporting Trend

## Statement

Virtually all Fortune 500 companies now report on sustainability activities, with adoption growing from 20% in 2011 to nearly 100% by 2023.

## Rationale

Investors and consumers increasingly expect management to engage in and report on sustainability efforts that have a positive impact on society.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Company is a large publicly traded corporation
- **THEN** Should prepare and publish sustainability/ESG reports
- **OTHERWISE** May face pressure from investors and stakeholders
