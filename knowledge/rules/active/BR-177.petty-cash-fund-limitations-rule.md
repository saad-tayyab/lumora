# Petty Cash Fund Limitations Rule

## Statement

The company may place restrictions on the maximum amount and the types of payments that can be made from a petty cash fund.

## Rationale

Controls the use of petty cash and prevents it from being used for unauthorized or inappropriate payments.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Operating a petty cash fund
- **THEN** Establish maximum payment limits and eligible expense types
- **OTHERWISE** Fund could be depleted by inappropriate or excessive payments
