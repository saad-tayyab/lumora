# Remittance Advice Control Rule

## Statement

When cash is received in the mail, the employee opening the mail should compare the amount of cash received with the amount indicated on the remittance advice. If no remittance advice is provided, one should be prepared.

## Rationale

Ensures that the posting to the customer's account is for the amount of cash actually received.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Receiving customer payments by mail
- **THEN** Compare cash received to remittance advice; prepare one if missing
- **OTHERWISE** Customer accounts may be incorrectly credited
