# Present Value Bond Pricing

## Statement

The price of a bond is determined by computing the present value of the face amount (single sum) plus the present value of the periodic interest payments (annuity), both discounted at the market rate.

## Rationale

Investors value bonds based on the time value of money—future cash flows are worth less today.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Pricing a bond at issuance
- **THEN** Price = (Face Amount × PV factor of $1) + (Interest Payment × PV factor of annuity)
- **OTHERWISE** The bond price cannot be determined
