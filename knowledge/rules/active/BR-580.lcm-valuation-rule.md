# LCM Valuation Rule

## Statement

Inventory must be reported at the lower of its historical cost or current market value.

## Rationale

Conservatism principle requires recognizing potential losses when they are probable.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing financial statements at period end
- **THEN** Compare cost to market value and report the lower amount
- **OTHERWISE** Inventory and income may be overstated
