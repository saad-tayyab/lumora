# Probable but Not Estimable Contingent Liability Disclosure

## Statement

When a contingent liability is probable but the amount cannot be reasonably estimated, it must be disclosed in the notes to the financial statements but not accrued.

## Rationale

Users of financial statements need to be aware of potential obligations even when amounts are uncertain.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A contingent liability is probable but not estimable
- **THEN** Disclose in notes to financial statements only
- **OTHERWISE** No disclosure required if not probable
