# Fiscal Year Selection

## Statement

Companies may choose a fiscal year that does not align with the calendar year if it better reflects their natural business cycle.

## Rationale

A fiscal year ending at a natural business year-end (e.g., post-holiday season for retailers) provides more meaningful financial reporting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A company's business cycle has a natural low point
- **THEN** The fiscal year may end at that natural low point rather than December 31
- **OTHERWISE** The calendar year would be used as the default fiscal year
