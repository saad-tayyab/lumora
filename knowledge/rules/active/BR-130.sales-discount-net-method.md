# Sales Discount - Net Method

## Statement

Under the net method, record the sale at the invoice amount minus the available discount. If payment is received after the discount period, record the forfeited discount as Interest Revenue.

## Rationale

The net method assumes the customer will take the discount, and revenue from forfeited discounts is recorded as interest.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording sales under the net method
- **THEN** Record sale net of discount
- **OTHERWISE** If customer pays after discount period, record forfeited discount as Interest Revenue
