# Depletion Is Computed Using Units-of-Activity Method

## Statement

Periodic depletion is computed by multiplying the quantity of natural resource extracted during the period by the depletion rate (Cost / Estimated Total Units).

## Rationale

Depletion should match the consumption of the natural resource with the revenue it generates.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Natural resources are harvested, mined, or extracted
- **THEN** Debit Depletion Expense and credit Accumulated Depletion
- **OTHERWISE** No depletion recorded
