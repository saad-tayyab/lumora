# Owner Withdrawals Reduce Owner Equity

## Statement

When an owner withdraws assets from the business, the withdrawal is debited to the owner's drawing account, which reduces total owner's equity.

## Rationale

Drawing accounts are contra-equity accounts. They track distributions separately from capital to provide transparency on owner transactions.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An owner withdraws cash or other assets for personal use
- **THEN** Debit the owner's drawing account and credit the asset distributed
- **OTHERWISE** Owner's equity will be overstated and asset balances incorrect
