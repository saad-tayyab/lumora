# Current Ratio Comparison

## Statement

When comparing liquidity between companies of different sizes, the current ratio is more useful than working capital because it is a relative (percentage) measure rather than an absolute dollar amount.

## Rationale

Working capital is affected by company size; the current ratio normalizes for size differences.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing short-term liquidity across companies of different sizes
- **THEN** Use current ratio rather than working capital alone
- **OTHERWISE** Comparisons may be misleading due to size differences
