# Multi-Department Labor Rate Differentiation Rule

## Statement

When multiple departments are involved in production, each department may have a different hourly labor rate that must be applied separately.

## Rationale

Different departments may require different skill levels and have different compensation structures.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Production requires work in multiple departments
- **THEN** Calculate labor cost per department using department-specific hours and rates, then sum
- **OTHERWISE** Labor costs will be misstated if a single average rate is used
