# Cash Flows Classified into Three Activities

## Statement

All cash inflows and outflows must be classified into one of three categories: operating activities, investing activities, or financing activities.

## Rationale

Classification helps users understand where cash comes from and how it is used.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the statement of cash flows
- **THEN** Classify every cash transaction as operating, investing, or financing
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
