# Contract Rate vs. Market Rate

## Statement

If the contract rate equals the market rate, bonds sell at face value. If contract rate < market rate, bonds sell at a discount. If contract rate > market rate, bonds sell at a premium.

## Rationale

Investors compare the bond's contract rate to what they can earn elsewhere in the market to determine what they are willing to pay.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Bonds are issued
- **THEN** Compare contract rate to market rate to determine issue price relative to face value
- **OTHERWISE** The bond cannot be properly priced
