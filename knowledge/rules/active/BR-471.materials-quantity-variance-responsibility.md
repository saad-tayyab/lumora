# Materials Quantity Variance Responsibility

## Statement

The Production Department is typically responsible for the direct materials quantity variance, as production supervisors control material usage and waste.

## Rationale

Production managers control manufacturing processes, equipment maintenance, and material handling that affect quantity efficiency.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Materials quantity variance is identified
- **THEN** Report to Production Supervisor for investigation
- **OTHERWISE** If caused by inferior materials from Purchasing, report to Purchasing
