# Construction in Progress Reclassification

## Statement

Costs accumulated in Construction in Progress must be reclassified to the appropriate fixed asset account (e.g., Building) when construction is complete.

## Rationale

Construction in Progress is a temporary account; the completed asset must be properly classified for depreciation and reporting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Construction of a fixed asset is completed
- **THEN** Credit Construction in Progress and debit the appropriate fixed asset account
- **OTHERWISE** The asset remains in a temporary account and is not properly depreciated
