# Accrual vs Cash Performance Difference

## Statement

Accrual accounting may show profitability while cash basis shows losses (or vice versa) for the same period, making accrual the required method for GAAP compliance.

## Rationale

Cash basis can be misleading because it ignores the timing of revenue earning and expense incurrence.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing accrual and cash basis results for the same period
- **THEN** Accrual basis is the required method for GAAP reporting
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements may not reflect true operating performance
