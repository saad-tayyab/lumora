# LLC Uses Member Equity Instead of Capital

## Statement

In a limited liability company, equity accounts are called 'Member Equity' accounts rather than 'Capital' accounts, but the accounting treatment is identical to partnerships.

## Rationale

LLCs use different terminology but follow similar accounting principles to partnerships for equity reporting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** The business is organized as an LLC
- **THEN** Use 'Member Equity' account titles instead of 'Capital' account titles
- **OTHERWISE** Standard partnership capital account titles apply
