# Extraordinary Repairs Reduce Accumulated Depreciation

## Statement

Costs that extend a fixed asset's useful life are capital expenditures recorded as a decrease to accumulated depreciation.

## Rationale

Extending useful life recovers previously recognized depreciation.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A repair extends the asset's useful life
- **THEN** Debit Accumulated Depreciation
- **OTHERWISE** If repair does not extend life, treat as ordinary repair
