# Times Interest Earned Analysis

## Statement

A declining times interest earned ratio indicates reduced ability to meet interest obligations and may concern debtholders.

## Rationale

This ratio measures the margin of safety for interest payments; a lower ratio signals higher risk.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing a company's debt-servicing ability
- **THEN** Compute times interest earned and compare across periods
- **OTHERWISE** Inability to assess credit risk
