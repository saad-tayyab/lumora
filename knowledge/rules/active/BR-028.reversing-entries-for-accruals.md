# Reversing Entries for Accruals

## Statement

Reversing entries may be used for accrued revenues and accrued expenses to simplify the recording of subsequent transactions.

## Rationale

Reversing entries allow the subsequent cash transaction to be recorded in the usual way without regard to the prior adjustment.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** First day of new period after accrual adjustments
- **THEN** Optionally reverse prior period accrual adjustments
- **OTHERWISE** Subsequent recording may require special analysis
