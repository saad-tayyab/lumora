# Job Cost Comparison for Analysis

## Statement

Job order costing accumulates and records product costs by jobs. The resulting total and unit product costs can be compared to similar jobs, compared over time, or compared to expected costs for evaluating and controlling costs.

## Rationale

Comparative analysis identifies cost trends, inefficiencies, and opportunities for improvement.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing job performance
- **THEN** Compare total and unit costs across jobs, over time, or against expectations
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot identify cost problems or improvement opportunities
