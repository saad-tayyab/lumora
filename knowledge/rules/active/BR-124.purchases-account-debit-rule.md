# Purchases Account Debit Rule

## Statement

The Purchases account is increased by a debit and decreased by a credit, with a normal debit balance.

## Rationale

Purchases is an expense-type temporary account in the periodic system that accumulates the cost of merchandise acquired for resale.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording merchandise purchases under periodic inventory system
- **THEN** Debit Purchases account
- **OTHERWISE** Under perpetual system, debit Inventory account directly
