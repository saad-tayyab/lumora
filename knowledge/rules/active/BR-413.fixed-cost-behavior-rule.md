# Fixed Cost Behavior Rule

## Statement

Fixed cost per unit decreases as the activity level increases and increases as the activity level decreases, while total fixed cost remains constant.

## Rationale

Fixed costs are spread over more units as volume increases, reducing per-unit cost.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing cost behavior within the relevant range
- **THEN** Total fixed cost is constant; fixed cost per unit is inversely proportional to activity
- **OTHERWISE** Cost predictions will be inaccurate
