# Double-Entry Bookkeeping

## Statement

Every transaction must be recorded with at least one debit and one credit, and total debits must equal total credits.

## Rationale

Ensures completeness and accuracy of financial records. Provides an automatic check on the recording process.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A business transaction occurs
- **THEN** Record at least one debit and one credit of equal amounts
- **OTHERWISE** The trial balance will not agree
