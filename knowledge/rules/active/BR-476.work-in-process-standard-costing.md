# Work in Process Standard Costing

## Statement

Work in Process is debited at standard cost for materials, labor, and applied overhead. Under a standard cost system, the cost of goods sold is reported at standard cost.

## Rationale

Recording at standard simplifies accounting and isolates variances for management analysis.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Manufacturing costs are recorded in accounts
- **THEN** Debit Work in Process at standard cost, credit variance accounts for differences
- **OTHERWISE** Variance accounts accumulate until period-end disposition
