# Materials Requisition Requirement

## Statement

Materials must not be released from the storeroom without a materials requisition specifying the job, quantity, and type of materials.

## Rationale

Ensures materials costs are properly traced to specific jobs for accurate cost accumulation.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Materials are needed for production
- **THEN** Issue a materials requisition before release
- **OTHERWISE** Materials costs cannot be traced to jobs
