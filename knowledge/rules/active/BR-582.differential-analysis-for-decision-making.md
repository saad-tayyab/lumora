# Differential Analysis for Decision Making

## Statement

When choosing between alternatives, only consider revenues and costs that differ between the alternatives (relevant costs).

## Rationale

Sunk costs and future costs that do not differ are irrelevant to the decision.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Making a choice between two or more alternatives
- **THEN** Identify and compare only differential revenues and costs
- **OTHERWISE** Decision may be based on irrelevant information
