# Production Budget Coordination Rule

## Statement

Production requirements must be carefully coordinated with the sales budget to avoid overproduction or stockouts.

## Rationale

Overproduction ties up capital in excess inventory, while underproduction leads to lost sales and customer dissatisfaction.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the production budget
- **THEN** Base production on sales forecast plus desired ending inventory minus beginning inventory
- **OTHERWISE** Production levels will not align with demand and inventory targets
