# Budget Gaming Prevention

## Statement

Organizations should separate the budget into planning and incentive components to reduce gaming behavior by managers.

## Rationale

When budgets serve dual purposes (planning and performance evaluation), managers are incentivized to manipulate estimates. Separating these functions reduces perverse incentives.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Budgets are used for both resource planning and performance evaluation
- **THEN** Maintain separate budgets for planning purposes and incentive-based targets
- **OTHERWISE** Managers will pad, sandbag, or wastefully spend budget allocations
