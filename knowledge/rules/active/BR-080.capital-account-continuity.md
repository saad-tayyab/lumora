# Capital Account Continuity

## Statement

The ending owner's capital balance from one period becomes the beginning balance for the next period.

## Rationale

Maintains the continuity of the owner's equity across accounting periods.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A new accounting period begins
- **THEN** The beginning capital balance equals the prior period's ending capital balance
- **OTHERWISE** Owner's equity is misstated
