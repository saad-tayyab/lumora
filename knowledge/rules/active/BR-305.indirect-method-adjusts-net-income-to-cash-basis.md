# Indirect Method Adjusts Net Income to Cash Basis

## Statement

The indirect method starts with net income and adjusts for non-cash items (depreciation, gains/losses) and changes in working capital (accounts receivable, inventory, accounts payable) to derive cash flows from operations.

## Rationale

Net income is based on accrual accounting; the indirect method converts it to cash basis.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using the indirect method for operating cash flows
- **THEN** Start with net income; add back non-cash expenses; deduct non-cash revenues; adjust for changes in current assets and liabilities
- **OTHERWISE** Use the direct method instead
