# Controllable Variance Responsibility

## Statement

Department supervisors are responsible for controlling variable manufacturing overhead controllable variance, as they manage departmental spending.

## Rationale

Variable overhead costs like indirect materials and factory supplies are controllable at the department level.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Controllable variance is identified
- **THEN** Report to department supervisors for corrective action
- **OTHERWISE** If variance is minimal, no action required
