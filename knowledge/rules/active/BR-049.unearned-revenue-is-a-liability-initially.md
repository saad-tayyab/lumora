# Unearned Revenue Is a Liability Initially

## Statement

Cash received before services are performed is recorded as a liability (unearned revenue), then converted to revenue when earned.

## Rationale

The business has an obligation to perform services or deliver goods, creating a liability.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Cash is received before services are performed
- **THEN** Record as a liability (unearned revenue), then adjust to revenue when earned
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue will be overstated in the receipt period
