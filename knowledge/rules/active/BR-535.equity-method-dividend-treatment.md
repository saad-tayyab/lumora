# Equity Method - Dividend Treatment

## Statement

Under the equity method, dividends received from the investee decrease the investment account rather than being recorded as revenue.

## Rationale

Dividends represent a return of the investment, not income, since the investor already recognized its share of the investee's earnings.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Receiving dividends on equity investments accounted for under the equity method
- **THEN** Debit Cash and credit Investment account
- **OTHERWISE** Under the fair value method, dividends are recorded as revenue
