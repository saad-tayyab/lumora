# Days' Cash on Hand Adequacy Rule

## Statement

Days' cash on hand greater than 50 days is generally considered adequate for businesses. However, an excessively high number may indicate the company has not invested cash back into the business or returned profits to owners.

## Rationale

Provides a benchmark for evaluating a company's liquidity position and ability to meet cash commitments.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating a company's liquidity
- **THEN** Compare days' cash on hand to 50-day benchmark; consider context
- **OTHERWISE** May misinterpret liquidity position
