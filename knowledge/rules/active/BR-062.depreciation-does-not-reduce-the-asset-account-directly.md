# Depreciation Does Not Reduce the Asset Account Directly

## Statement

When recording depreciation, the fixed asset account is not credited. Instead, an Accumulated Depreciation contra account is credited. The original cost remains in the asset account.

## Rationale

Both the original cost and the accumulated depreciation are reported on the balance sheet, providing transparency about the asset's age and remaining book value. Directly crediting the asset would lose the historical cost information.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Depreciation is recorded for a fixed asset
- **THEN** Debit Depreciation Expense and credit Accumulated Depreciation—[Asset Type]
- **OTHERWISE** Historical cost information is lost and the asset's book value cannot be determined
