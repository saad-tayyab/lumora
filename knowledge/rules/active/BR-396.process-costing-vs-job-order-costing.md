# Process Costing vs Job Order Costing

## Statement

Process costing is appropriate for continuous, homogeneous production (e.g., oil refining, food processing, chemicals). Job order costing is appropriate for unique, custom products (e.g., custom furniture, construction, special printing).

## Rationale

Selecting the correct costing system depends on the nature of the production process.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Determining appropriate costing system
- **THEN** Process costing for homogeneous continuous production; Job order costing for unique custom production
- **OTHERWISE** Incorrect cost allocation and reporting
