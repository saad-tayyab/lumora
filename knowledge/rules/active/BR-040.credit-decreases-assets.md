# Credit Decreases Assets

## Statement

Decreases in asset accounts are recorded on the credit (right) side of the account.

## Rationale

The opposite side records decreases, maintaining the balance of the account.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An asset account decreases
- **THEN** Record the decrease on the credit (right) side
- **OTHERWISE** The account balance will be incorrect
