# Absorption vs Variable Costing Income

## Statement

Under absorption costing, fixed manufacturing overhead is included in inventory. Under variable costing, it is expensed as a period cost. Operating income differs between the two methods when inventory levels change.

## Rationale

Absorption costing is required for external reporting; variable costing is useful for internal decision-making.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing absorption and variable costing income statements
- **THEN** Income differs by the amount of fixed overhead in ending inventory minus beginning inventory
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot reconcile the two costing methods
