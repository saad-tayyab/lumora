# Fair Value Method - Dividend Revenue

## Statement

Under the fair value method, dividends received from equity investments are recorded as dividend revenue on the income statement.

## Rationale

With less than 20% ownership, the investor has no significant influence, so dividends represent income earned.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Receiving dividends on equity investments accounted for under the fair value method
- **THEN** Debit Cash and credit Dividend Revenue
- **OTHERWISE** Under the equity method, dividends reduce the investment account
