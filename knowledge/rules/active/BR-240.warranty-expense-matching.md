# Warranty Expense Matching

## Statement

Warranty expense must be estimated and recorded in the same period as the related sale.

## Rationale

The matching principle requires expenses to be recognized in the same period as the revenue they help generate.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A product is sold under warranty
- **THEN** Record estimated warranty expense as a percentage of sales
- **OTHERWISE** Expenses are understated in the sale period and overstated when claims occur
