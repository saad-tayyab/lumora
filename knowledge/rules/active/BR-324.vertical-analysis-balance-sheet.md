# Vertical Analysis Balance Sheet

## Statement

In vertical analysis of the balance sheet, each asset item is expressed as a percent of total assets, and each liability and stockholders' equity item is expressed as a percent of total liabilities and stockholders' equity.

## Rationale

This shows the relative composition of assets, liabilities, and equity within a single period.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Performing vertical analysis on a balance sheet
- **THEN** Each item / Total assets (or total liabilities and equity) × 100
- **OTHERWISE** The relative proportions of balance sheet components are not measured
