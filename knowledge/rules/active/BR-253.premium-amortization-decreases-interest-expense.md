# Premium Amortization Decreases Interest Expense

## Statement

When bond premium is amortized, the amortized amount is deducted from interest expense, reducing it below the cash interest paid.

## Rationale

The premium represents a reduction in interest cost because the bond was issued above face value.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A bond was issued at a premium
- **THEN** Interest expense = Cash interest - Premium amortization
- **OTHERWISE** No premium amortization is needed
