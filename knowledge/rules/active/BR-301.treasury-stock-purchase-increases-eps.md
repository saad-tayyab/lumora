# Treasury Stock Purchase Increases EPS

## Statement

When a corporation purchases treasury stock, the number of shares outstanding decreases, which increases earnings per share (all else equal).

## Rationale

Fewer shares outstanding means the same net income is spread across fewer shares.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Treasury stock is purchased
- **THEN** Shares outstanding decrease, EPS increases
- **OTHERWISE** When treasury stock is sold, shares outstanding increase, EPS decreases
