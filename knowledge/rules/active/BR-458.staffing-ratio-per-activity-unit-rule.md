# Staffing Ratio Per Activity Unit Rule

## Statement

Nonmanufacturing staffing requirements should be calculated by dividing the expected activity level by the number of activity units each staff member can handle.

## Rationale

Staffing needs directly correlate with activity levels in service businesses.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Determining staffing requirements for service businesses
- **THEN** Staff needed = Total activity units ÷ Activity units per staff member
- **OTHERWISE** Overstaffing or understaffing will occur
