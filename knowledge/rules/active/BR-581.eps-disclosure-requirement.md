# EPS Disclosure Requirement

## Statement

Publicly traded companies must report earnings per share on the income statement.

## Rationale

Provides investors with a standardized measure of profitability per share of stock.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Company has publicly traded common stock
- **THEN** Report basic and diluted EPS on the income statement
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements do not comply with GAAP
