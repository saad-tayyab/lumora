# Losses on Disposal Reported in Other Revenue and Expense

## Statement

Gains or losses on the disposal (sale or discarding) of fixed assets are reported in the 'Other revenue and expense' section of the income statement.

## Rationale

Disposal gains and losses are not part of normal operations and should be separately disclosed.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A fixed asset is sold or discarded
- **THEN** Report any gain or loss in the Other Revenue and Expense section
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
