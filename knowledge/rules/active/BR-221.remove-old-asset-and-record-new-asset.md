# Remove Old Asset and Record New Asset

## Statement

When recording an exchange, remove the old asset's cost and its accumulated depreciation from the accounts, record the new asset at fair market value, record cash paid, and recognize any gain or loss.

## Rationale

The exchange replaces one asset with another; old asset must be derecognized and new asset recognized at its fair value.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording an exchange of similar fixed assets
- **THEN** Debit Accumulated Depreciation (old), debit New Asset (FMV), credit Old Asset (cost), credit Cash (paid), debit/credit Gain/Loss
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
