# Credit Card Sales Recorded as Cash

## Statement

Credit card and debit card sales are normally recorded as cash sales because the retailer receives cash within a few days via a clearinghouse.

## Rationale

Simplifies accounting for most credit card transactions since cash is received quickly.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A sale is made using a credit card or debit card
- **THEN** Record as a cash sale (Debit Cash, Credit Sales)
- **OTHERWISE** Accounts receivable would need to be tracked for each card company
