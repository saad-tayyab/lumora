# Journal Before Ledger

## Statement

All transactions must first be recorded in the journal before being posted to the ledger accounts.

## Rationale

Ensures a complete chronological record of all transactions exists. The journal serves as the book of original entry, providing an audit trail.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A business transaction occurs
- **THEN** Record it in the journal first, then post to the appropriate ledger accounts
- **OTHERWISE** The transaction record is incomplete and may be lost or duplicated
