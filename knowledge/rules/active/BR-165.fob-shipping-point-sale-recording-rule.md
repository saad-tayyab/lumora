# FOB Shipping Point Sale Recording Rule

## Statement

Under FOB shipping point terms, the sale is recorded when goods are shipped, not when they arrive at the buyer's location. The buyer owns goods in transit.

## Rationale

Properly matches revenue recognition with the transfer of ownership and risk.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Shipping goods FOB shipping point
- **THEN** Record sale when goods are shipped; buyer includes goods in transit in inventory
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue and inventory would be misstated
