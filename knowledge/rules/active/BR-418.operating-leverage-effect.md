# Operating Leverage Effect

## Statement

Operating Leverage = Contribution Margin / Operating Income. A higher operating leverage means a small percentage change in sales produces a larger percentage change in operating income.

## Rationale

Measures the sensitivity of profit to changes in sales volume.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing the impact of sales changes on operating income
- **THEN** Calculate operating leverage and apply to percentage sales change
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot quantify the magnification effect of fixed costs on profit
