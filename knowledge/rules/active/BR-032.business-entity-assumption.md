# Business Entity Assumption

## Statement

The activities of the business must be kept separate and distinct from the activities of its owner and all other economic entities.

## Rationale

Ensures that financial statements reflect only the business's activities, not personal activities of the owner.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording transactions
- **THEN** Only record transactions that affect the specific business entity
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements would be misleading
