# Principle of Exceptions

## Statement

Management by exception means investigating only significant variances that exceed predetermined thresholds, focusing attention on areas requiring corrective action.

## Rationale

Not all variances warrant investigation; focusing on significant variances maximizes management efficiency.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Variance exceeds threshold (e.g., $1,000 or 10% of budget)
- **THEN** Investigate and report the variance
- **OTHERWISE** No investigation needed
