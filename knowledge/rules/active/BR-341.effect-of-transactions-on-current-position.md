# Effect of Transactions on Current Position

## Statement

Transactions affect working capital, current ratio, and quick ratio differently depending on whether they involve current assets and/or current liabilities.

## Rationale

Understanding transaction effects helps evaluate how business decisions impact liquidity.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing the effect of a transaction on current position analysis
- **THEN** Consider which current asset and current liability accounts are affected
- **OTHERWISE** Incorrect assessment of liquidity impact
