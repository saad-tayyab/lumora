# Patent and Copyright Amortization

## Statement

Patents and copyrights are amortized over their estimated useful life (not to exceed legal life) using the straight-line method. Amortization is credited directly to the intangible asset account.

## Rationale

These assets have finite useful lives and their cost should be systematically allocated to expense.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A patent or copyright is held for use in operations
- **THEN** Record amortization expense annually using straight-line method
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
