# Ratio Analysis Requires Comparison

## Statement

Financial ratios are most useful when compared to industry averages, prior periods, or competitors.

## Rationale

A single ratio in isolation provides limited insight. Comparisons reveal trends and relative performance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing financial ratios
- **THEN** Compare to industry benchmarks, historical trends, or peer companies
- **OTHERWISE** Ratios cannot be properly interpreted
