# Multiple Allocation Methods Comparison

## Statement

When overhead allocation methods produce significantly different per-unit costs, the method that best reflects actual resource consumption (typically ABC) should be used for pricing and strategic decisions.

## Rationale

Different allocation methods can produce materially different product costs, affecting pricing, product mix, and discontinuation decisions.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Different overhead allocation methods yield significantly different product costs
- **THEN** Evaluate which method most accurately traces costs to products and use that for decision-making
- **OTHERWISE** May make incorrect strategic decisions based on distorted costs
