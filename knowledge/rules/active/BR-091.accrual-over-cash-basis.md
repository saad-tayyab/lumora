# Accrual Over Cash Basis

## Statement

Under GAAP, the accrual basis of accounting must be used because it better matches revenues earned with expenses incurred, providing a more accurate picture of financial performance.

## Rationale

Accrual basis prevents distortion of financial results caused by timing of cash flows.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A business prepares financial statements under GAAP
- **THEN** Accrual basis accounting is required
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements do not conform to GAAP
