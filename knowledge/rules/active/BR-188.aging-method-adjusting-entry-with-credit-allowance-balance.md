# Aging Method Adjusting Entry with Credit Allowance Balance

## Statement

When the Allowance for Doubtful Accounts has a credit balance before adjustment, the adjusting entry amount equals the desired ending credit balance minus the existing credit balance.

## Rationale

The existing credit balance already covers part of the estimated uncollectibles.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Allowance for Doubtful Accounts has a credit balance and aging method is used
- **THEN** Adjusting entry = Desired ending credit balance - Unadjusted credit balance
- **OTHERWISE** The allowance will be overstated
