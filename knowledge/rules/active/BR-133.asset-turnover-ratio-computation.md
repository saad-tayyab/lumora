# Asset Turnover Ratio Computation

## Statement

Asset Turnover Ratio = Sales / Average Total Assets, where Average Total Assets = (Beginning Total Assets + Ending Total Assets) / 2.

## Rationale

This ratio measures how efficiently a company uses its assets to generate revenue. A higher ratio indicates more efficient asset utilization.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing company efficiency using financial statements
- **THEN** Compute ratio using sales and average total assets
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
