# Normal Selling Price Must Cover All Costs

## Statement

The normal selling price must be set high enough to cover all costs and expenses (fixed and variable) and provide a reasonable profit for long-term business survival.

## Rationale

Special prices may exceed variable costs in the short term, but normal prices must cover full costs for business viability.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Setting normal selling prices
- **THEN** Markup must cover all costs plus desired profit
- **OTHERWISE** Business will not survive long-term
