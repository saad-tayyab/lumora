# Balance Sheet Presentation of Bonds

## Statement

Bonds payable are reported in the long-term liabilities section. Any current portion due within one year is reclassified as a current liability.

## Rationale

Proper classification helps users assess short-term vs. long-term obligations.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the balance sheet
- **THEN** Report bonds payable (net of unamortized discount or plus unamortized premium) in long-term liabilities
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statement users cannot properly assess the company's liability structure
