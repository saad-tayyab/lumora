# Employer Payroll Tax Obligation

## Statement

Employers must pay matching FICA taxes plus federal unemployment tax (0.8%) and state unemployment tax (5.4%) on applicable wages.

## Rationale

Employers bear additional tax obligations beyond employee withholding.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Employees earn wages subject to payroll taxes
- **THEN** Record Payroll Tax Expense with credits to the respective payable accounts
- **OTHERWISE** Non-compliance results in penalties
