# Bank Reconciliation Journal Entry Rule

## Statement

Journal entries are required for all items in the company section of the bank reconciliation (additions and deductions from the company's records). No journal entries are needed for items in the bank section.

## Rationale

The company's records must be updated for items the bank has recorded but the company has not (credit memos, debit memos, and company errors).

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Completing a bank reconciliation
- **THEN** Journalize all items in the company section (credit memos, debit memos, company errors)
- **OTHERWISE** Company's cash account will not reflect the true adjusted balance
