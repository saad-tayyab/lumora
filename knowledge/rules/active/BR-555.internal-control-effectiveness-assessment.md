# Internal Control Effectiveness Assessment

## Statement

Management must annually evaluate and report on the effectiveness of internal control over financial reporting based on the COSO framework.

## Rationale

SOX Section 404 requires management assessment of internal controls for publicly traded companies.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** At fiscal year-end
- **THEN** Management evaluates internal controls and the independent auditor opines on their effectiveness
- **OTHERWISE** The company may not be in compliance with SEC reporting requirements
