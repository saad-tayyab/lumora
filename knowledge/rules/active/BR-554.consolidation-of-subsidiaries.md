# Consolidation of Subsidiaries

## Statement

Consolidated financial statements must include the accounts of the parent company and all subsidiaries, with all significant intercompany transactions and balances eliminated.

## Rationale

GAAP requires presenting the economic entity as a single enterprise for a complete financial picture.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing consolidated financial statements
- **THEN** Combine all subsidiary accounts and eliminate intercompany transactions
- **OTHERWISE** The consolidated statements would not fairly represent the group's financial position
