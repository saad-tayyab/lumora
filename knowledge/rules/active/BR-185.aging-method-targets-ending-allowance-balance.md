# Aging Method Targets Ending Allowance Balance

## Statement

Under the analysis of receivables method, the sum of estimated uncollectibles by age class equals the desired ending balance of Allowance for Doubtful Accounts. The adjusting entry amount depends on the existing unadjusted balance.

## Rationale

Ensures the allowance balance reflects the current estimated collectibility of outstanding receivables.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using analysis of receivables method
- **THEN** Desired ending Allowance balance = Σ(Age Class Total × Uncollectible %); Adjusting entry = Desired ending - Unadjusted balance (considering debit/credit)
- **OTHERWISE** The allowance would not reflect the true estimated uncollectible amount
