# Sensitivity Analysis for Key Assumptions

## Statement

When performing sensitivity analysis, vary one assumption at a time (e.g., cash flows, discount rate, useful life) and recalculate NPV to determine which factors have the greatest impact on project viability.

## Rationale

Sensitivity analysis identifies the most critical assumptions and helps assess the risk of a capital investment decision.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating risk and uncertainty in capital investment analysis
- **THEN** Systematically vary key assumptions and observe NPV changes
- **OTHERWISE** Base-case analysis alone may be misleading
