# Post-Closing Trial Balance Verification

## Statement

After closing entries are posted, a post-closing trial balance must be prepared to verify that total debits equal total credits.

## Rationale

Ensures the ledger is in balance before the next accounting period begins.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Closing entries have been posted to the ledger
- **THEN** A post-closing trial balance is prepared containing only permanent accounts
- **OTHERWISE** Errors in closing may go undetected
