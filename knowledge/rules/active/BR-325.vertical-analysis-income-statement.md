# Vertical Analysis Income Statement

## Statement

In vertical analysis of the income statement, each item is expressed as a percent of sales (or net sales).

## Rationale

This shows what portion of each sales dollar is consumed by each expense or contributes to profit.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Performing vertical analysis on an income statement
- **THEN** Each item / Sales × 100
- **OTHERWISE** The relationship of expenses to sales is not measured
