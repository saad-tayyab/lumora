# Job Cost Sheet Completeness

## Statement

A complete job cost sheet must include direct materials, direct labor, and applied manufacturing overhead (or direct labor and overhead for services).

## Rationale

Ensures all product costs are captured for accurate job costing and decision-making.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording costs on a job cost sheet
- **THEN** Include DM, DL, and applied OH (or DL and OH for services)
- **OTHERWISE** Job cost is incomplete and decisions based on it are flawed
