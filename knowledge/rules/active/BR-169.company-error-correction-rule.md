# Company Error Correction Rule

## Statement

Company errors should be added to or deducted from the company's cash balance in the reconciliation, and a journal entry should be made to correct the company's records.

## Rationale

The company must correct its own accounting records to reflect the correct cash balance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Company makes an error in recording a transaction
- **THEN** Adjust company section of reconciliation and record correcting journal entry
- **OTHERWISE** Company's cash account will be misstated
