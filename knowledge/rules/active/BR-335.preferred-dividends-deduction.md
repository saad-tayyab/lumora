# Preferred Dividends Deduction

## Statement

Preferred dividends are subtracted from net income when computing return on common stockholders' equity and earnings per share on common stock.

## Rationale

Preferred stockholders rank ahead of common stockholders in their claim on earnings.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preferred and common stock are both outstanding
- **THEN** Deduct preferred dividends from net income
- **OTHERWISE** Return and EPS overstate common stockholders' earnings
