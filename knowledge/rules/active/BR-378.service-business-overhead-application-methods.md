# Service Business Overhead Application Methods

## Statement

Service businesses may use different activity bases for overhead allocation depending on their operations (e.g., direct labor hours for consulting, media purchases for advertising).

## Rationale

The activity base should reflect the primary driver of overhead costs for the specific service business.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Selecting an overhead allocation base for a service business
- **THEN** Choose the base that best reflects the consumption of overhead resources
- **OTHERWISE** Overhead allocation is arbitrary and potentially misleading
