# Process Further Decision Rule

## Statement

A product should be processed further only if the incremental revenue from further processing exceeds the incremental cost of further processing.

## Rationale

Processing further is only profitable when the additional revenue generated exceeds the additional costs incurred.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A product can be sold as-is or processed further into a higher-value product
- **THEN** Process further if (Revenue after processing - Revenue as-is) > Additional processing cost
- **OTHERWISE** Sell the product as-is at the split-off point
