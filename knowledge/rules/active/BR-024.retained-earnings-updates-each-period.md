# Retained Earnings Updates Each Period

## Statement

Retained earnings is updated at the end of each period by adding net income and subtracting dividends.

## Rationale

Reflects the cumulative undistributed earnings of the corporation.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the statement of retained earnings
- **THEN** Beginning RE + Net Income - Dividends = Ending RE
- **OTHERWISE** Retained earnings balance will be incorrect
