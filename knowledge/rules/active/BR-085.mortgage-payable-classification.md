# Mortgage Payable Classification

## Statement

The current portion of a long-term liability (e.g., mortgage payments due within one year) must be classified as a current liability, with the remainder as a long-term liability.

## Rationale

Correctly classifies obligations by their due date for liquidity analysis.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A long-term liability has payments due within one year
- **THEN** The portion due within one year is a current liability; the rest is long-term
- **OTHERWISE** Current liabilities are understated and long-term liabilities overstated
