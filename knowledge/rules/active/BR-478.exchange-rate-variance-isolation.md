# Exchange Rate Variance Isolation

## Statement

Multinational corporations must isolate exchange rate fluctuations from purchasing performance when analyzing direct materials price variances.

## Rationale

Purchasing agents cannot control exchange rate changes, so these variances should be separated from controllable purchasing variances.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Company operates internationally and purchases in foreign currencies
- **THEN** Isolate exchange rate effects from materials price variance analysis
- **OTHERWISE** Standard price variance analysis applies
