# Spreadsheet Column Equality

## Statement

On an end-of-period spreadsheet, the Income Statement and Balance Sheet columns must each balance. The difference between debit and credit totals in the Income Statement columns equals net income (or net loss), which must be entered to balance both sections.

## Rationale

Ensures the spreadsheet is internally consistent and net income is correctly determined.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An end-of-period spreadsheet is completed
- **THEN** Income Statement and Balance Sheet columns are balanced by the net income amount
- **OTHERWISE** An error in extending or computing amounts exists
