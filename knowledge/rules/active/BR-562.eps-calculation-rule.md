# EPS Calculation Rule

## Statement

Earnings per share must be computed as net income less preferred dividends divided by the average number of common shares outstanding.

## Rationale

Ensures accurate measurement of profitability attributable to each common share and excludes preferred stock claims.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Calculating earnings per share on common stock
- **THEN** Subtract preferred dividends from net income and divide by average common shares outstanding
- **OTHERWISE** EPS figure is misleading as it does not account for preferred stock claims
