# Trial Balance Equality

## Statement

The total of all debit account balances in the trial balance must equal the total of all credit account balances.

## Rationale

Serves as a mathematical check on the ledger. If totals are unequal, an error has been made in recording, posting, or balancing.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A trial balance is prepared from the ledger
- **THEN** Total debits column equals total credits column
- **OTHERWISE** An error exists that must be identified and corrected before proceeding
