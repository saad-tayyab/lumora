# Prime Costs Definition

## Statement

Prime costs equal direct materials plus direct labor.

## Rationale

Identifies the primary direct costs of production for cost control and analysis.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Calculating total prime costs for a product
- **THEN** Sum direct materials cost and direct labor cost
- **OTHERWISE** Prime costs cannot be determined
