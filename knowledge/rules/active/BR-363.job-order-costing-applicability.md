# Job Order Costing Applicability

## Statement

Job order costing is appropriate when products are unique, custom-made, or produced in distinct batches. Process costing is appropriate for continuous production of indistinguishable units.

## Rationale

Selecting the right cost system ensures accurate cost accumulation matching the production process.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Products are custom or produced in distinct batches
- **THEN** Use job order costing
- **OTHERWISE** Use process costing for continuous homogeneous production
