# Compensating Balance Disclosure Rule

## Statement

Compensating balance requirements must be disclosed in notes to the financial statements.

## Rationale

Users of financial statements need to know about restrictions on cash availability.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Company has a compensating balance requirement
- **THEN** Disclose in notes to financial statements
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements would be incomplete
