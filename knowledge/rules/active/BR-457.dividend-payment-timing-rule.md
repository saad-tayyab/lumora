# Dividend Payment Timing Rule

## Statement

Quarterly dividends should be budgeted based on the number of shares outstanding and the dividend per share, with attention to declaration and payment dates.

## Rationale

Dividends are declared in one period but may be paid in a subsequent period, affecting cash flow timing.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Budgeting for dividend payments
- **THEN** Record dividend liability when declared and cash outflow when paid
- **OTHERWISE** Cash budget timing will be incorrect
