# Closing Entries Zero Out Temporary Accounts

## Statement

All temporary accounts (revenue, expense, and drawing/dividends) must be closed to zero at the end of each period.

## Rationale

Ensures each period starts with zero balances for temporary accounts, preventing double-counting of revenues and expenses.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** End of accounting period
- **THEN** Close all temporary accounts to Owner's Capital or Retained Earnings
- **OTHERWISE** Period balances will carry forward incorrectly
