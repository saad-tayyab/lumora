# Manufacturing Cost Payment Timing Rule

## Statement

When estimating cash payments for manufacturing costs, account for the fact that some costs (e.g., depreciation, insurance, property taxes) are non-cash or paid at specific times during the year.

## Rationale

Not all manufacturing costs result in immediate cash outflows; depreciation is non-cash, and insurance/taxes may be paid annually.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the cash disbursements schedule
- **THEN** Separate non-cash costs and deferred payment costs from current-period cash payments
- **OTHERWISE** Cash budget will overstate or understate actual cash requirements
