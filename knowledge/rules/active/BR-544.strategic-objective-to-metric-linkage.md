# Strategic Objective to Metric Linkage

## Statement

Each strategic objective in the balanced scorecard must have at least one performance metric assigned to measure progress toward achieving it.

## Rationale

Without metrics, there is no way to quantitatively assess whether strategic objectives are being achieved, making performance management impossible.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Defining strategic objectives in the balanced scorecard
- **THEN** Assign at least one performance metric to each objective
- **OTHERWISE** The objective cannot be measured or managed effectively
