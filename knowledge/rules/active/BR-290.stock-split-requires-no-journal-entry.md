# Stock Split Requires No Journal Entry

## Statement

A stock split does not require a journal entry. Only the par value per share and number of shares outstanding change. Total par value outstanding remains unchanged.

## Rationale

The stock split merely changes the unit of ownership; total equity is unaffected.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Board declares a stock split
- **THEN** Reduce par value proportionally, increase shares outstanding proportionally, no journal entry needed
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
