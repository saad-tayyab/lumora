# Perpetual Inventory Purchase Recording

## Statement

When merchandise is purchased under the perpetual inventory system, debit the Inventory account for the purchase amount. Purchase returns and allowances are credited to Inventory, and purchase discounts are credited to Inventory.

## Rationale

The perpetual system continuously updates the Inventory account for all purchase-related transactions, keeping the inventory balance current at all times.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording purchase-related transactions under perpetual inventory system
- **THEN** Debit Inventory for purchases, credit Inventory for returns and discounts
- **OTHERWISE** Under periodic system, use Purchases account instead
