# Matching Principle

## Statement

Expenses should be recognized in the same period as the revenues they help generate.

## Rationale

Proper matching of expenses with revenues produces a more accurate measure of income.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A revenue is earned
- **THEN** Recognize all related expenses in the same period
- **OTHERWISE** Income will be misstated
