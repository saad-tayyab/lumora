# Preferred Stock Dividend Preference

## Statement

Preferred stockholders have first rights to dividends before common stockholders. Common dividends cannot be paid until the current preferred dividend is paid.

## Rationale

The preference feature makes preferred stock more attractive to conservative investors.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Dividends are declared and both preferred and common stock are outstanding
- **THEN** Preferred stockholders receive their stated dividend before any common stockholders receive dividends
- **OTHERWISE** If preferred dividends are not fully paid, common stockholders receive nothing
