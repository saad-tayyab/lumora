# Relevant Range Constraint

## Statement

Cost behavior assumptions (variable costs per unit constant, total fixed costs constant) are valid only within the relevant range of activity.

## Rationale

Outside the relevant range, cost patterns may change due to capacity constraints, step costs, or other factors.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Applying CVP analysis
- **THEN** Ensure analysis is within the relevant range of activity
- **OTHERWISE** Cost behavior assumptions are invalid and results will be misleading
