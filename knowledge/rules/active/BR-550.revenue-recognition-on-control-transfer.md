# Revenue Recognition on Control Transfer

## Statement

Revenue must be recognized when control of goods transfers to the customer, which occurs upon shipment or receipt depending on the sales channel and geographic agreement.

## Rationale

ASC 606 revenue recognition standard requires recording revenue when the customer obtains control of the promised goods.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A sale transaction occurs and control transfers to the customer
- **THEN** Record revenue at the transaction price (invoiced amount less estimated variable consideration)
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue must not be recognized before control transfer
