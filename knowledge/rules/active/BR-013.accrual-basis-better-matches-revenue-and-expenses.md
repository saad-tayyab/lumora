# Accrual Basis Better Matches Revenue and Expenses

## Statement

Accrual accounting provides a better indicator of a company's operating performance by matching revenues earned with expenses incurred.

## Rationale

Cash basis accounting can mislead users about a company's profitability. For example, a profitable company under accrual may show a loss under cash basis if cash timing differs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating a company's true operating performance
- **THEN** Use accrual accounting results rather than cash basis
- **OTHERWISE** Operating performance may be misstated
