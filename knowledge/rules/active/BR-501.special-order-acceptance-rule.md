# Special Order Acceptance Rule

## Statement

A special order should be accepted if the special price exceeds the variable cost per unit and the order does not affect normal sales or exceed existing capacity.

## Rationale

Any price above variable cost contributes to covering fixed costs, and excess capacity means fixed costs are unaffected by the order.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A special order is received at a price below normal selling price with excess capacity available
- **THEN** Accept the order if special price > variable cost per unit (including any incremental costs specific to the order)
- **OTHERWISE** Reject the order
