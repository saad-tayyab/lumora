# Ending Cash Reconciliation

## Statement

The net increase or decrease in cash for the period, when added to the beginning cash balance, must equal the ending cash balance reported on the balance sheet.

## Rationale

This reconciliation verifies the mathematical accuracy of the statement of cash flows and its agreement with the balance sheet.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Completing the statement of cash flows
- **THEN** Beginning cash + Net change in cash = Ending cash balance on balance sheet
- **OTHERWISE** There is an error in the statement of cash flows
