# Employer FICA Matching

## Statement

Employers must match employee FICA contributions: 6.2% for Social Security and 1.45% for Medicare.

## Rationale

Federal law requires employers to contribute an equal amount to the employee's FICA taxes.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An employee earns wages subject to FICA
- **THEN** Employer records Payroll Tax Expense for matching amounts
- **OTHERWISE** The employer is non-compliant with federal tax law
