# Voucher System Documentation Rule

## Statement

Before a voucher is approved for payment, all necessary supporting documents must be received: supplier's invoice, purchase order, and receiving report.

## Rationale

Ensures that payments are made only for authorized transactions and that goods were actually received at the agreed price.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Approving a voucher for payment
- **THEN** Verify supplier's invoice, purchase order, and receiving report match
- **OTHERWISE** Company may pay for goods not received or at incorrect prices
