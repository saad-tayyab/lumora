# Times Interest Earned Indicates Solvency

## Statement

The times interest earned ratio measures a company's ability to meet its interest obligations; a higher ratio indicates greater protection for creditors.

## Rationale

Creditors use this ratio to assess the risk of default on interest payments, with ratios below 1.0 indicating inability to cover interest from operations.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating credit risk or solvency
- **THEN** Compute times interest earned as income before tax divided by interest expense
- **OTHERWISE** Ratio is not meaningful if interest expense is zero
