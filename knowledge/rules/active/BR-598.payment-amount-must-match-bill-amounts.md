# Payment Amount Must Match Bill Amounts

## Statement

A vendor payment's total amount must equal the sum of the amounts of all bills being paid by that payment.

## Rationale

This ensures financial integrity of payment transactions. The payment header total must be consistent with the bills it pays to prevent overpayment or underpayment and maintain accurate accounts payable records.

## Scope

- Applies to: All vendor payments (CON-AP-002)
- Bounded Context: BC-AP (Accounts Payable)
- Entities: VendorPayment, Bill

## Conditions

- WHEN a vendor payment is created or updated
- THEN total_amount MUST equal sum of all linked bill amounts being paid
- OTHERWISE the payment is rejected with error `ERR_PAYMENT_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`

## Pseudocode

```
function validatePaymentAmount(payment):
    paidBillTotal = sum(payment.paidBills.map(bill => bill.amount))
    
    if payment.total_amount != paidBillTotal:
        throw PaymentAmountMismatchError(payment.id, payment.total_amount, paidBillTotal)
```

## Exceptions

- None — this rule has no exceptions.
