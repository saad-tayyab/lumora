# Expense Recognition on Incurrence

## Statement

Expenses are recorded when incurred (goods consumed or services used), regardless of when cash is paid.

## Rationale

Under accrual accounting, expenses are matched to the revenues they help generate in the same period.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An expense is incurred (rent used, supplies consumed, services received)
- **THEN** Record the expense (debit the expense account) for the amount incurred
- **OTHERWISE** Expenses will be understated and net income overstated
