# Direct Method Requires Reconciliation

## Statement

When using the direct method, a separate schedule reconciling net income to net cash flows from operating activities must be included as part of the statement of cash flows.

## Rationale

The reconciliation provides the same information as the indirect method and ensures users can verify the operating cash flow total.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** The direct method is used for operating activities
- **THEN** Include a reconciliation schedule (equivalent to the indirect method operating section)
- **OTHERWISE** The statement is incomplete
