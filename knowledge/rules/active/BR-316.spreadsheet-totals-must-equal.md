# Spreadsheet Totals Must Equal

## Statement

On the spreadsheet (worksheet) used to prepare the statement of cash flows, the total of the Debit Transactions column must equal the total of the Credit Transactions column after all noncash accounts have been analyzed.

## Rationale

The equality of debits and credits verifies that all changes in noncash accounts have been properly analyzed and classified.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using the spreadsheet method to prepare the statement of cash flows
- **THEN** Total Debit Transactions = Total Credit Transactions
- **OTHERWISE** An account change was missed or misclassified
