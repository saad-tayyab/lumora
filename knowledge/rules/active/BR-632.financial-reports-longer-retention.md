# Financial Reports Longer Retention

## Statement

Financial reports may have longer retention periods than other report types to meet compliance requirements.

## Rationale

Financial regulations often require retaining financial records for extended periods (typically 7 years). This rule ensures the report retention policy supports compliance with these requirements.

## Scope

- Applies to: All reports (CON-REPORT-001) with report_type = 'financial'
- Bounded Context: BC-REPORT (Reporting & Analytics)
- Entities: Report, ReportRetentionPolicy

## Conditions

- WHEN a report retention policy is evaluated for financial reports
- THEN the retention_days MUST be sufficient to meet applicable regulatory requirements
- OTHERWISE the system may violate compliance requirements

## Pseudocode

```
function evaluateFinancialReportRetention(report, policy):
    if report.report_type == 'financial':
        minRetentionDays = getRegulatoryMinimumRetentionDays()
        if policy.retention_days < minRetentionDays:
            throw InsufficientFinancialRetentionError(report.id, policy.retention_days, minRetentionDays)
```

## Exceptions

- None — this rule has no exceptions.
