# Sale Triggers COGS Recognition

## Statement

When a job is sold, the cost of the job must be transferred from Finished Goods to Cost of Goods Sold, and the sale must be recorded at the selling price.

## Rationale

Matches the cost of the product sold with the revenue earned in the same period (matching principle).

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A completed job is sold
- **THEN** Debit Cost of Goods Sold, Credit Finished Goods; Record sale revenue
- **OTHERWISE** No COGS entry
