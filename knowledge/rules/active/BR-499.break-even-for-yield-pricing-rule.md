# Break-Even for Yield Pricing Rule

## Statement

When offering discounted prices to fill excess capacity, the discounted price must exceed the variable cost per customer to generate a positive contribution margin.

## Rationale

Any price above variable cost contributes to covering fixed costs and increases profit, even if the price is well below the normal selling price.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Offering discounted or standby pricing to fill unused capacity
- **THEN** Minimum acceptable price = Variable cost per unit; any price above this generates positive contribution margin
- **OTHERWISE** Use normal pricing
