# Income Divided Equally Without Agreement

## Statement

If the partnership agreement does not specify how income and losses are to be divided, they are divided equally among all partners.

## Rationale

Default partnership law provides for equal distribution when no agreement exists, ensuring fairness among partners.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** No income-sharing agreement exists
- **THEN** Divide net income and losses equally among all partners
- **OTHERWISE** Follow the terms specified in the partnership agreement
