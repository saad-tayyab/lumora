# Compounding Interest Calculation Rule

## Statement

Interest earning interest (compounding) causes a sum to grow at an increasing rate over time; the future value of $1 invested for n periods at rate r is (1+r)^n.

## Rationale

Compounding reflects the realistic growth of invested money and is the foundation for present value calculations.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing the future value of an investment or the present value of a future amount
- **THEN** Apply compounding: FV = PV × (1+r)^n; apply discounting: PV = FV × PV factor
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
