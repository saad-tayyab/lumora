# Multiple-Step Income Statement Classification

## Statement

Operating expenses must be classified as either selling expenses or administrative expenses on a multiple-step income statement.

## Rationale

Classifying expenses helps management and users analyze the costs of selling vs. general administration.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a multiple-step income statement
- **THEN** Classify each operating expense as selling or administrative
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot determine operating income by expense category
