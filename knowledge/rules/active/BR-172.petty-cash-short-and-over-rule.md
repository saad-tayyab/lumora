# Petty Cash Short and Over Rule

## Statement

If the amount needed to replenish the petty cash fund does not equal the total of the petty cash receipts, the difference is recorded in the Cash Short and Over account.

## Rationale

Captures any discrepancies between actual cash disbursed and documented receipts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Replenishing petty cash and receipts total differs from cash needed
- **THEN** Record difference in Cash Short and Over (debit for shortage, credit for overage)
- **OTHERWISE** Cash balance would not be correctly adjusted
