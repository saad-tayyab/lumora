# Selling/Admin ABC for Internal Use

## Statement

Selling and administrative expenses may be allocated to products using activity-based costing for internal managerial decision-making, even though GAAP requires them to be period expenses externally.

## Rationale

ABC provides more accurate product profitability analysis when products consume selling/admin activities differently.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing product profitability for internal decisions
- **THEN** Consider allocating selling/admin expenses using ABC based on actual activity consumption
- **OTHERWISE** Product profitability analysis may be misleading
