# Target Profit Formula

## Statement

Required Sales (units) = (Fixed Costs + Target Profit) / Unit Contribution Margin

## Rationale

Determines the sales volume needed to achieve a desired profit level.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Determining sales needed to achieve a target profit
- **THEN** Add target profit to fixed costs and divide by unit contribution margin
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot plan for desired profit levels
