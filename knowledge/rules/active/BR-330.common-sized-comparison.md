# Common-Sized Comparison

## Statement

Common-sized statements, which express all items as percentages, are useful for comparing one company with another or for comparing a company with industry averages regardless of company size.

## Rationale

Removing dollar amounts eliminates the effect of size differences, enabling direct comparison of operational efficiency and cost structure.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing companies of different sizes or against industry benchmarks
- **THEN** Use common-sized statements (all items as percentages)
- **OTHERWISE** Dollar amount differences may obscure meaningful operational comparisons
