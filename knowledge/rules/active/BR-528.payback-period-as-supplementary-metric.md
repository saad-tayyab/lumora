# Payback Period as Supplementary Metric

## Statement

The payback period should be used as a supplementary risk measure alongside NPV, not as the primary investment decision criterion. A shorter payback period indicates lower risk but does not capture total profitability.

## Rationale

Payback ignores cash flows beyond the payback period and does not account for the time value of money, but it provides useful information about liquidity and risk.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating capital investments
- **THEN** Use payback period alongside NPV and IRR as a risk indicator
- **OTHERWISE** Relying solely on payback period may lead to rejecting profitable long-term investments
