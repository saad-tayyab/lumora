# Treasury Stock Recorded at Cost

## Statement

Under the cost method, treasury stock is debited for the cost (purchase price) when reacquired and credited for the same cost when resold.

## Rationale

The cost method is the most commonly used method and most straightforward for recording treasury stock transactions.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Corporation reacquires its own stock
- **THEN** Debit Treasury Stock for the purchase price; credit Cash
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
