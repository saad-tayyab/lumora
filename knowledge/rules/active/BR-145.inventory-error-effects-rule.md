# Inventory Error Effects Rule

## Statement

An understatement of ending inventory causes an overstatement of cost of goods sold, an understatement of gross profit and net income, and an understatement of total assets and owner's equity on the balance sheet.

## Rationale

COGS = Beginning Inventory + Purchases - Ending Inventory. Lower ending inventory increases COGS and reduces profit.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Ending inventory is understated
- **THEN** COGS is overstated, gross profit and net income are understated, total assets and equity are understated
- **OTHERWISE** Opposite effects occur when ending inventory is overstated
