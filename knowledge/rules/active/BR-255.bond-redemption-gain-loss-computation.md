# Bond Redemption Gain/Loss Computation

## Statement

Gain or loss on bond redemption equals the carrying amount at redemption date minus the redemption price. Gain if carrying amount > redemption price; loss if carrying amount < redemption price.

## Rationale

The company either saves (gain) or pays extra (loss) compared to the book value when retiring bonds early.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Bonds are redeemed before maturity
- **THEN** Compute gain or loss: Carrying amount - Redemption price
- **OTHERWISE** No gain or loss at maturity
