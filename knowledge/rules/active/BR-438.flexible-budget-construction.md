# Flexible Budget Construction

## Statement

A flexible budget must separate costs into variable and fixed components. Variable costs are expressed as a rate per unit of activity; fixed costs are expressed as total amounts.

## Rationale

Only by separating cost behavior can the budget be adjusted to reflect different activity levels accurately.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Building a flexible budget
- **THEN** Identify variable cost per unit and total fixed costs for each cost category, then compute total budgeted cost at each activity level
- **OTHERWISE** The budget cannot adapt to varying activity levels
