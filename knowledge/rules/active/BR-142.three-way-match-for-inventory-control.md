# Three-Way Match for Inventory Control

## Statement

Before recording inventory, the receiving report, purchase order, and vendor's invoice must agree. Any differences should be investigated and reconciled.

## Rationale

Three-way matching ensures that the company pays only for what was ordered, received, and properly invoiced, preventing fraud and errors.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Receiving and recording inventory purchases
- **THEN** Compare receiving report, purchase order, and vendor's invoice
- **OTHERWISE** Investigate and reconcile any discrepancies before recording
