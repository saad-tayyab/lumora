# Balance Sheet Date Reporting

## Statement

The balance sheet reports the financial position as of a specific date, not for a period of time.

## Rationale

Distinguishes the balance sheet (point in time) from the income statement (period of time).

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a balance sheet
- **THEN** Report as of a specific date (e.g., 'As of December 31, 20Y3')
- **OTHERWISE** Users may misunderstand the temporal nature of the data
