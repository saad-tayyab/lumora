# Interest-Bearing Note Accounting

## Statement

Interest on a note is computed as Face Amount × Interest Rate × Time (in days/365 or months/12) and recorded as Interest Expense.

## Rationale

Matches the cost of borrowing to the period in which the funds are used.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An interest-bearing note is outstanding
- **THEN** Accrue interest expense proportionally over the note's term
- **OTHERWISE** No interest expense is recorded
