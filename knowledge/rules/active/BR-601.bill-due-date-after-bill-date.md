# Bill Due Date After Bill Date

## Statement

A vendor bill's due date must be on or after the bill date.

## Rationale

This ensures logical consistency of payment schedules. A bill cannot be due before it is issued, which would create invalid payment terms and confuse accounts payable processing.

## Scope

- Applies to: All vendor bills (CON-AP-003) with payment schedules (CON-AP-005)
- Bounded Context: BC-AP (Accounts Payable)
- Entities: Bill, PaymentSchedule

## Conditions

- WHEN a vendor bill is created or its payment schedule is updated
- THEN due_date MUST be on or after bill_date
- OTHERWISE the bill is rejected with error `ERR_BILL_DUE_DATE_INVALID`

## Pseudocode

```
function validateBillDueDate(bill):
    if bill.due_date < bill.bill_date:
        throw BillDueDateError(bill.id, bill.due_date, bill.bill_date)
```
