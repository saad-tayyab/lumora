# Treasury Stock Resale Loss Limited to Paid-In Capital

## Statement

When treasury stock is resold below cost, the loss is debited to Paid-In Capital from Sale of Treasury Stock to the extent of its credit balance. Any excess loss is debited to Retained Earnings.

## Rationale

Paid-In Capital from Sale of Treasury Stock cannot have a debit balance, so losses beyond available credit fall to Retained Earnings.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Treasury stock is resold at a price below its reacquisition cost
- **THEN** Debit Paid-In Capital from Sale of Treasury Stock up to its credit balance; debit Retained Earnings for any excess loss
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
