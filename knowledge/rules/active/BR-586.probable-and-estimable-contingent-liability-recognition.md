# Probable and Estimable Contingent Liability Recognition

## Statement

When a contingent liability is both probable and the amount can be reasonably estimated, it must be accrued (recorded as a liability) on the balance sheet.

## Rationale

Ensures that known obligations are reflected in the financial statements for accurate reporting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A contingent liability exists and is probable and estimable
- **THEN** Record a liability on the balance sheet and disclose in notes
- **OTHERWISE** Only disclose in notes (if probable but not estimable) or do nothing (if remote)
