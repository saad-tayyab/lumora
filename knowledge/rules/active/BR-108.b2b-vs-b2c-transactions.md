# B2B vs B2C Transactions

## Statement

Transactions between companies (B2B) differ from transactions between retailers and final consumers (B2C), particularly regarding sales taxes and trade discounts.

## Rationale

B2B transactions are typically exempt from sales taxes; trade discounts are common in B2B.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording merchandise transactions
- **THEN** Determine whether the transaction is B2B or B2C to apply correct tax and discount rules
- **OTHERWISE** Incorrect tax or discount application
