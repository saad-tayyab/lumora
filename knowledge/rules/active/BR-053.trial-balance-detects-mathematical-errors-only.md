# Trial Balance Detects Mathematical Errors Only

## Statement

A balanced trial balance confirms that total debits equal total credits, but it cannot detect errors where both sides are equally wrong (e.g., omission of an entire transaction, posting to the wrong account, or compensating errors).

## Rationale

The trial balance is a necessary but not sufficient control. Many error types (omission, commission, principle, original entry, reversal, compensation) do not affect debit-credit equality.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a trial balance
- **THEN** Verify that debit and credit columns are equal, but do not assume records are error-free
- **OTHERWISE** Mathematical or posting errors may go undetected
