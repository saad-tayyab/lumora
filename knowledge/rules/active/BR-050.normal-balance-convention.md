# Normal Balance Convention

## Statement

The normal balance of an account is on the side (debit or credit) that receives increases. Assets and expenses have normal debit balances; liabilities, equity, and revenue have normal credit balances.

## Rationale

Provides a consistent framework for determining whether a balance is normal or indicates an error. An abnormal balance signals a potential issue.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An account balance is determined
- **THEN** The balance should be on the normal side for its account type
- **OTHERWISE** Investigate for potential errors or unusual transactions
