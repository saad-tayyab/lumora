# Credit Increases Revenue

## Statement

Revenue accounts increase on the credit side because revenue increases owner's equity.

## Rationale

Revenue increases equity, so it follows the same debit/credit rules as equity accounts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Revenue is earned
- **THEN** Record the increase on the credit side of the revenue account
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue and net income will be understated
