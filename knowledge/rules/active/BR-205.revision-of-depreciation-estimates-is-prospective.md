# Revision of Depreciation Estimates Is Prospective

## Statement

When estimates of residual values or useful lives change, the new estimates are used to determine depreciation expense in future periods only. Earlier depreciation is not affected.

## Rationale

Changes in estimate are accounted for prospectively under GAAP; no restatement of prior periods.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Management revises estimated useful life or residual value
- **THEN** Apply new estimates to remaining book value over revised remaining life
- **OTHERWISE** Continue with original estimates
