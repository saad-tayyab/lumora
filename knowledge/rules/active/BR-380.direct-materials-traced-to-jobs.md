# Direct Materials Traced to Jobs

## Statement

Direct materials costs must be traced to specific jobs using materials requisition forms. Only materials that can be clearly identified with a particular job are charged as direct materials.

## Rationale

Ensures accurate job costing by attributing material costs to the correct job.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Materials are requisitioned for production
- **THEN** Charge direct materials to the specific job cost sheet
- **OTHERWISE** Charge indirect materials to Manufacturing Overhead
