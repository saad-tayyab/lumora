# Profit Center Evaluation Across Divisions

## Statement

Operating income of profit centers should not be compared across divisions because divisions typically differ in size, products, and customers. Instead, evaluate each profit center by comparing its operating income to its own budget over time.

## Rationale

Cross-division comparison of absolute profit is misleading because it does not account for differences in division scale and market conditions.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating profit center performance
- **THEN** Compare actual operating income to budget for each division independently
- **OTHERWISE** Cross-division comparisons of absolute profit may be misleading
