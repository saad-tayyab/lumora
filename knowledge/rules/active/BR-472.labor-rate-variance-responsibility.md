# Labor Rate Variance Responsibility

## Statement

The Human Resources or Scheduling Department is responsible for the direct labor rate variance, as they control employee assignments and wage rates.

## Rationale

Improper scheduling can result in using highly paid employees for jobs normally done by lower-paid workers, causing rate variances.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Labor rate variance is unfavorable
- **THEN** Report to managers who schedule work assignments
- **OTHERWISE** Rate variances may be due to market wage rates
