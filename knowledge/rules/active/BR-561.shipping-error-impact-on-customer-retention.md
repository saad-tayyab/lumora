# Shipping Error Impact on Customer Retention

## Statement

In a measure map, shipping errors above a threshold directly reduce customer retention, which in turn reduces market share — establishing a quantifiable cause-and-effect chain.

## Rationale

Operational performance directly impacts customer satisfaction, which drives market outcomes.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Shipping errors exceed a defined threshold per month
- **THEN** Each additional error reduces customer retention by a defined percentage
- **OTHERWISE** Retention and market share remain at baseline levels
