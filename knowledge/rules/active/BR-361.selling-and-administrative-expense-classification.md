# Selling and Administrative Expense Classification

## Statement

Selling expenses relate to marketing and delivering products. Administrative expenses relate to general management. Both are period costs expensed as incurred.

## Rationale

Proper classification enables analysis of operating efficiency by function.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A period cost is incurred
- **THEN** Classify as selling or administrative based on its function
- **OTHERWISE** Operating expenses are not properly reported by function
