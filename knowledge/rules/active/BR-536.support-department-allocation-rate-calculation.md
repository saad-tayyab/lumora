# Support Department Allocation Rate Calculation

## Statement

Support department allocation rates are computed as: Support Department Expense / Total Support Department Usage across all divisions.

## Rationale

This rate provides a consistent basis for allocating shared costs to divisions based on their actual consumption of services.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Allocating support department costs to operating divisions
- **THEN** Compute allocation rate and multiply by each division's usage
- **OTHERWISE** Costs remain in the support department without allocation
