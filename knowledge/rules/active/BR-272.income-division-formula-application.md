# Income Division Formula Application

## Statement

Partnership income is divided by applying the agreed-upon formula in order: first salary allowances, then interest allowances, then any remaining income divided per the agreement.

## Rationale

The formula must be applied in a specific order to correctly allocate income according to the partnership agreement.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A partnership agreement specifies multiple allocation methods
- **THEN** Apply allocations in order: salaries, interest, then remaining income
- **OTHERWISE** The allocation may not reflect the partners' intent
