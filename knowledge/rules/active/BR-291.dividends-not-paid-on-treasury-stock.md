# Dividends Not Paid on Treasury Stock

## Statement

No dividends (cash or stock) are paid on treasury stock. Doing so would result in the corporation paying dividends to itself.

## Rationale

Treasury stock is issued but not outstanding; dividends are paid only on outstanding shares.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Dividends are declared
- **THEN** Dividends are computed only on outstanding shares (issued shares minus treasury shares)
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
