# Petty Cash Fund Establishment Rule

## Statement

A petty cash fund is established by writing and cashing a check for the estimated amount needed. The entry debits Petty Cash and credits Cash.

## Rationale

Creates a controlled cash fund for small disbursements without requiring individual checks for each small payment.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Creating a petty cash fund
- **THEN** Debit Petty Cash and credit Cash for the fund amount
- **OTHERWISE** No petty cash fund exists for small disbursements
