# Target Cost Reduction

## Statement

Target costing requires reducing costs from the current level to the target cost through design simplification, material cost reduction, labor cost reduction, or waste elimination.

## Rationale

In competitive markets, prices are market-determined, so profit can only be maintained by controlling costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Market price is expected to decline below current cost
- **THEN** Implement cost reduction strategies to achieve target cost
- **OTHERWISE** Profit margin will be squeezed or eliminated
