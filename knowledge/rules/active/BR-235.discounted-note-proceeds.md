# Discounted Note Proceeds

## Statement

Proceeds from a discounted note equal Face Amount minus (Face Amount × Discount Rate × Discount Period ÷ 365).

## Rationale

The discount represents interest deducted in advance, so the borrower receives less than face value.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A note is discounted by the bank
- **THEN** Proceeds = Face Amount - Discount
- **OTHERWISE** Standard interest-bearing note accounting applies
