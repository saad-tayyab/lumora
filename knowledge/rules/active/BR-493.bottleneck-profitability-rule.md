# Bottleneck Profitability Rule

## Statement

In a production bottleneck operation, the best measure of a product's profitability is its unit contribution margin per bottleneck constraint hour, not its unit contribution margin alone.

## Rationale

When a bottleneck limits total output, profit is maximized by emphasizing products that generate the highest contribution margin per unit of the constrained resource.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A production bottleneck exists and multiple products share the bottleneck resource
- **THEN** Rank products by unit contribution margin per bottleneck hour and emphasize the highest-ranked product
- **OTHERWISE** Use standard unit contribution margin for product emphasis decisions
