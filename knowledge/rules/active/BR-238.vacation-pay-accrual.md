# Vacation Pay Accrual

## Statement

Vacation pay expense must be accrued in the period the vacation is earned by employees, not when taken.

## Rationale

Matching principle requires expenses to be recorded in the period they are incurred.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Employees earn vacation benefits
- **THEN** Debit Vacation Pay Expense and credit Vacation Pay Payable
- **OTHERWISE** Liabilities and expenses are understated
