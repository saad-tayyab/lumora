# Inventory Turnover Interpretation Rule

## Statement

Generally, a larger inventory turnover and lower days' sales in inventory indicate more efficient and effective inventory management.

## Rationale

Higher turnover means inventory is being sold and replaced more frequently, reducing carrying costs and obsolescence risk.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing inventory efficiency across periods or companies
- **THEN** Higher turnover and lower days' sales in inventory are favorable
- **OTHERWISE** Lower turnover and higher days' sales may indicate inefficiency
