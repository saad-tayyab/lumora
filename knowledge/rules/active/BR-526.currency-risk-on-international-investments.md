# Currency Risk on International Investments

## Statement

When a foreign investment generates revenues in local currency but the parent company reports in a home currency, exchange rate fluctuations directly impact the realized internal rate of return. A depreciation of the local currency reduces the home-currency value of local revenues.

## Rationale

Exchange rate changes affect the conversion of foreign cash flows to the reporting currency, potentially turning a profitable investment into a loss-making one.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Foreign subsidiary produces and sells in local currency
- **THEN** Exchange rate depreciation reduces IRR in home currency terms
- **OTHERWISE** If revenues are in home currency (exported), exchange rate impact is reduced
