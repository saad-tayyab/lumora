# ROI Calculation

## Statement

Return on Investment equals Operating Income divided by Invested Assets.

## Rationale

ROI provides a standardized measure to compare profitability across divisions or investment opportunities of different sizes.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating the profitability of a division or investment
- **THEN** Calculate ROI as Operating Income / Invested Assets
- **OTHERWISE** Use alternative metrics like profit margin or investment turnover
