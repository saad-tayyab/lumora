# Desired Ending Inventory Policy Rule

## Statement

Finished goods ending inventory is typically set as a percentage of the next period's estimated sales to ensure adequate stock levels.

## Rationale

Maintaining a buffer inventory protects against unexpected demand increases and production delays.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Determining desired ending finished goods inventory
- **THEN** Set ending inventory as a percentage (e.g., 10-20%) of next period's estimated sales
- **OTHERWISE** Risk of stockouts or excessive inventory holding costs
