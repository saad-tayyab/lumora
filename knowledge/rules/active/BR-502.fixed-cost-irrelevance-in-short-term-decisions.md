# Fixed Cost Irrelevance in Short-Term Decisions

## Statement

Fixed costs that are unaffected by a decision should be excluded from differential analysis since they will be incurred regardless of the alternative chosen.

## Rationale

Only costs that differ between alternatives are relevant to the decision; common fixed costs are irrelevant.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A decision does not affect the total fixed costs
- **THEN** Exclude fixed costs from the differential analysis
- **OTHERWISE** Include only the differential fixed costs that change between alternatives
