# Prime Cost and Conversion Cost Formulas

## Statement

Prime Cost = Direct Materials + Direct Labor. Conversion Cost = Direct Labor + Manufacturing Overhead.

## Rationale

Provides alternative groupings of manufacturing costs for analysis purposes.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing the components of product costs
- **THEN** Use prime cost to measure direct inputs and conversion cost to measure transformation costs
- **OTHERWISE** Analysis of cost structure is incomplete
