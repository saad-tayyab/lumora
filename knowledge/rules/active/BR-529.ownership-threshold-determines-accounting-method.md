# Ownership Threshold Determines Accounting Method

## Statement

The percentage of an investee's outstanding stock acquired determines the accounting method: less than 20% uses the fair value method; 20%-50% uses the equity method; over 50% requires consolidation.

## Rationale

Ownership percentage indicates the degree of control the investor has over the investee, which determines the appropriate accounting treatment.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Investor purchases equity securities of another company
- **THEN** Apply the accounting method corresponding to the ownership percentage
- **OTHERWISE** Default to fair value method for less than 20% ownership
