# Partner Withdrawals Close to Capital

## Statement

At the end of each accounting period, each partner's drawing account is closed to that partner's capital account, reducing the capital balance.

## Rationale

Drawing accounts are temporary accounts that track withdrawals during the period; closing them updates capital to reflect actual equity.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** End of the accounting period
- **THEN** Debit each partner's capital account and credit their drawing account to close
- **OTHERWISE** Capital accounts would not reflect current equity
