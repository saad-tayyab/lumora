# Weekday vs Weekend Staffing Differentiation Rule

## Statement

When demand varies between weekdays and weekends, staffing budgets should be prepared separately for each period type to accurately reflect labor needs.

## Rationale

Hotels, restaurants, and retail stores experience significantly different demand patterns on weekdays versus weekends.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Activity levels differ significantly between weekdays and weekends
- **THEN** Prepare separate staffing calculations for each period type and combine for the total budget
- **OTHERWISE** Average staffing will overstaff some days and understaff others
