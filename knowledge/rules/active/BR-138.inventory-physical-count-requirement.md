# Inventory Physical Count Requirement

## Statement

A physical inventory count should be taken near year-end to ensure the quantity of inventory reported in the financial statements is accurate.

## Rationale

Physical verification ensures the inventory quantities in the records match the actual inventory on hand, detecting any shrinkage or errors.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing year-end financial statements
- **THEN** Perform physical inventory count
- **OTHERWISE** Inventory balances may be inaccurate
