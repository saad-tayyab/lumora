# Accrual Basis Recognition Rule

## Statement

Revenues and expenses must be reported on the income statement in the period in which they are earned or incurred, regardless of when cash is received or paid.

## Rationale

Accrual basis provides a more accurate picture of economic activity than cash basis accounting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing financial statements under GAAP
- **THEN** Record revenues when earned and expenses when incurred
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements misrepresent the company's financial performance
