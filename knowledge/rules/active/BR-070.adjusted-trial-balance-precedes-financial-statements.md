# Adjusted Trial Balance Precedes Financial Statements

## Statement

The adjusted trial balance must be prepared after all adjusting entries are posted and before financial statements are generated. It serves as the source for all financial statement amounts.

## Rationale

Financial statements must reflect the true financial position after all accruals and deferrals. The adjusted trial balance ensures all adjustments are captured.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** All adjusting entries have been posted to the ledger
- **THEN** Prepare the adjusted trial balance and verify debit-credit equality
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements may contain unadjusted or incorrect amounts
