# Period Cost Recognition

## Statement

Period costs (selling, general, and administrative expenses) are expensed in the period in which they are incurred.

## Rationale

These costs are not directly tied to production and cannot be inventoried.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Selling, general, or administrative costs are incurred
- **THEN** Expense immediately on the income statement
- **OTHERWISE** No capitalization as inventory
