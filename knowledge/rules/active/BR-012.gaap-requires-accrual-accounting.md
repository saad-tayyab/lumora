# GAAP Requires Accrual Accounting

## Statement

Generally Accepted Accounting Principles require the use of accrual basis of accounting for financial reporting.

## Rationale

Accrual accounting better matches revenues and expenses, providing a more accurate measure of a company's profitability and financial position.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing financial statements for external reporting
- **THEN** Use accrual basis of accounting
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements will not comply with GAAP
