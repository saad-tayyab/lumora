# Special Price Acceptance Criteria

## Statement

A special price order should be accepted if the price exceeds differential costs and does not displace regular sales, assuming excess capacity exists.

## Rationale

Any price above variable cost contributes to fixed cost coverage and profit when excess capacity exists.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Receiving a special order at below-normal price
- **THEN** Accept if price exceeds variable cost and excess capacity exists
- **OTHERWISE** Reject if price does not cover variable costs or displaces regular sales
