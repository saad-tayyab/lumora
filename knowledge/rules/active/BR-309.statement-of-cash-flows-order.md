# Statement of Cash Flows Order

## Statement

The statement of cash flows must always report operating activities first, followed by investing activities, and then financing activities, in that order.

## Rationale

Standardized reporting order ensures consistency and comparability across companies and periods.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a statement of cash flows
- **THEN** Report operating, investing, and financing activities in that order
- **OTHERWISE** The statement format is non-compliant with GAAP
