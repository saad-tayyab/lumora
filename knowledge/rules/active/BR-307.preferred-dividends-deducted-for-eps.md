# Preferred Dividends Deducted for EPS

## Statement

When computing earnings per share, preferred dividends are deducted from net income before dividing by average common shares outstanding, regardless of whether preferred dividends were declared.

## Rationale

Preferred dividends represent a prior claim on earnings that is not available to common stockholders.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing EPS and cumulative preferred stock is outstanding
- **THEN** Deduct the current year's preferred dividend from net income (even if not declared)
- **OTHERWISE** For non-cumulative preferred stock, deduct only if dividends were declared
