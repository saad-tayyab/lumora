# Adjusting Entries Must Balance

## Statement

Every adjusting entry must have equal debits and credits.

## Rationale

Adjusting entries follow the same double-entry bookkeeping rules as all other journal entries.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Recording an adjusting entry at period end
- **THEN** Total debits must equal total credits
- **OTHERWISE** The adjusted trial balance will not balance
