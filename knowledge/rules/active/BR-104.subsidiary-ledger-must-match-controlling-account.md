# Subsidiary Ledger Must Match Controlling Account

## Statement

The sum of the balances of accounts in a subsidiary ledger must equal the balance of the related controlling account in the general ledger.

## Rationale

Ensures the integrity of the accounting system by verifying subsidiary records reconcile with the general ledger.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A subsidiary ledger is maintained
- **THEN** Total subsidiary ledger balances must equal the controlling account balance
- **OTHERWISE** An error exists in the subsidiary ledger or general ledger
