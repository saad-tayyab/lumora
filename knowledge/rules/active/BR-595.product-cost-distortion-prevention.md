# Product Cost Distortion Prevention

## Statement

When products consume overhead resources in different proportions, traditional volume-based allocation (e.g., direct labor hours) will distort product costs. Activity-based costing (ABC) should be used to more accurately allocate overhead.

## Rationale

Prevents overcosting high-volume products and undercosting low-volume products, leading to better pricing and product mix decisions.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Products consume overhead resources disproportionately
- **THEN** Use activity-based costing or multiple allocation bases to allocate overhead
- **OTHERWISE** A single volume-based allocation base may be sufficient
