# No Par Stock with Stated Value

## Statement

When no-par stock has a stated value assigned by the board, the stated value is treated like par value for recording purposes. The excess over stated value is credited to Paid-In Capital in Excess of Stated Value.

## Rationale

Stated value provides a par-equivalent for no-par stock in jurisdictions that require it.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** No-par stock with stated value is issued
- **THEN** Credit stock account for stated value per share; credit excess to Paid-In Capital in Excess of Stated Value
- **OTHERWISE** For no-par stock without stated value, credit entire proceeds to the stock account
