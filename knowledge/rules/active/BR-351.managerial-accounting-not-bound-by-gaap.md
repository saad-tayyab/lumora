# Managerial Accounting Not Bound by GAAP

## Statement

Managerial accounting reports do not have to be prepared according to GAAP, as GAAP may not always be relevant to the specific decision-making needs of management.

## Rationale

Management needs flexible, timely information tailored to specific decisions rather than standardized external reporting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing managerial accounting reports
- **THEN** Reports can be designed to meet management's specific needs without GAAP constraints
- **OTHERWISE** Reports may not provide the most useful information for internal decisions
