# Working Capital Positive Indicator

## Statement

Working capital (Current Assets minus Current Liabilities) should be positive to indicate the business can meet short-term obligations.

## Rationale

Positive working capital implies the business has sufficient liquid assets to pay its current liabilities.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating short-term liquidity
- **THEN** Working capital should be positive (Current Assets > Current Liabilities)
- **OTHERWISE** The business may face difficulty meeting short-term obligations
