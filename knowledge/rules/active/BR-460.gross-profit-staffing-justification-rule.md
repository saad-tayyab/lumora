# Gross Profit Staffing Justification Rule

## Statement

Before adding staff for increased seasonal demand, verify that the additional gross profit from increased sales exceeds the additional labor cost.

## Rationale

Staffing for peak demand should be economically justified; the incremental revenue must cover the incremental labor cost.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Considering additional staffing for seasonal demand increases
- **THEN** Calculate: (Additional sales × Gross profit %) vs. (Additional labor cost)
- **OTHERWISE** Staffing additions may reduce profitability
