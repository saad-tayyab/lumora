# Warning Signs of Control Problems Rule

## Statement

Management should monitor for warning signs including: abrupt lifestyle changes, close supplier relationships, refusal to take vacations, missing documentation, unexplained cash shortages, and unusual increases in customer refunds.

## Rationale

These indicators often precede or accompany fraud and internal control failures.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Monitoring internal control system
- **THEN** Watch for behavioral and accounting system warning signs
- **OTHERWISE** Fraud may continue undetected
