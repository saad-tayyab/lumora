# FIFO Cost Flow Rule

## Statement

Under FIFO, the first units purchased are the first units charged to cost of goods sold. Ending inventory consists of the most recently purchased units.

## Rationale

Assumes costs are charged against revenue in the order they were incurred, matching actual physical flow for many businesses.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Inventory is valued using the FIFO method
- **THEN** Oldest unit costs are assigned to COGS first; newest costs remain in ending inventory
- **OTHERWISE** A different cost flow assumption applies
