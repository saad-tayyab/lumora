# Multi-Phase Revenue NPV Calculation

## Statement

When evaluating investments with sequential revenue phases, discount each phase's net cash flows to present value using the desired rate of return, then sum all present values and subtract the total initial investment to determine NPV.

## Rationale

Different revenue streams occur at different times and must each be discounted appropriately to reflect the time value of money.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An investment generates revenue through multiple sequential distribution channels or phases
- **THEN** Discount each phase's cash flows independently and sum for total NPV
- **OTHERWISE** Use standard single-phase NPV calculation
