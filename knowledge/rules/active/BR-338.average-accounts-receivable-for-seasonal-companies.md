# Average Accounts Receivable for Seasonal Companies

## Statement

When sales are seasonal, monthly balances of receivables should be used to compute average accounts receivable.

## Rationale

Beginning and end of year balances may not represent typical receivable levels during seasonal periods.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Company has seasonal sales patterns
- **THEN** Use monthly average receivables instead of beginning and ending balances
- **OTHERWISE** Average receivables may be distorted
