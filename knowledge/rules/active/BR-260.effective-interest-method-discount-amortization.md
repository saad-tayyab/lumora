# Effective Interest Method Discount Amortization

## Statement

Under the effective interest method for discount bonds: Interest Expense = Carrying Amount × Market Rate; Amortization = Interest Expense - Cash Interest Paid.

## Rationale

This method produces a constant rate of interest expense relative to the carrying amount.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using the effective interest rate method for a discount bond
- **THEN** Interest expense = Beginning carrying amount × semiannual market rate
- **OTHERWISE** Use straight-line or other method
