# Present Value Factor Selection

## Statement

When computing present value for bonds, use the market rate per period (e.g., semiannual market rate = annual market rate ÷ 2) and the total number of periods (e.g., semiannual periods = years × 2).

## Rationale

Bond pricing uses the same compounding frequency as the interest payments.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing present value of bond cash flows
- **THEN** Match the discount rate and period count to the payment frequency
- **OTHERWISE** The computed price will be incorrect
