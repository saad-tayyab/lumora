# Net Method Preferred Under GAAP

## Statement

The net method of recording sales discounts is consistent with the GAAP requirement that revenue be recorded at the amount most likely to be received.

## Rationale

By recording sales at net, the revenue figure already reflects expected discounts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Choosing between gross and net methods for sales discounts
- **THEN** The net method is more consistent with GAAP revenue recognition
- **OTHERWISE** May require year-end adjustments under the gross method
