# Direct vs Indirect Cost Classification

## Statement

Whether a cost is classified as direct or indirect depends on the cost object. The same cost can be direct for one cost object and indirect for another.

## Rationale

Cost classification is relative to the cost object, enabling flexible cost assignment for different decision-making needs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Classifying a cost as direct or indirect
- **THEN** First identify the cost object, then determine if the cost can be traced to it
- **OTHERWISE** Costs may be misclassified leading to inaccurate product costing
