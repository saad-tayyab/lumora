# Adjusted Trial Balance Equality

## Statement

The total debits must equal total credits on the adjusted trial balance after all adjustments have been posted.

## Rationale

Verifies mathematical accuracy of the ledger before preparing financial statements.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Adjusting entries have been posted
- **THEN** Prepare adjusted trial balance and verify debit/credit equality
- **OTHERWISE** Errors exist that must be found and corrected before financial statements
