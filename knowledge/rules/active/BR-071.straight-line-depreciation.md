# Straight-Line Depreciation

## Statement

Depreciation Expense per period equals (Cost minus Residual Value) divided by Useful Life in periods. This method allocates an equal amount of depreciation to each period.

## Rationale

Straight-line is the simplest and most commonly used depreciation method. It assumes the asset provides equal benefit over its useful life.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using the straight-line depreciation method
- **THEN** Depreciation Expense = (Cost - Residual Value) / Useful Life
- **OTHERWISE** Depreciation amounts will be incorrect
