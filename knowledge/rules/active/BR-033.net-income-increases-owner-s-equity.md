# Net Income Increases Owner's Equity

## Statement

Net income for a period increases owner's equity (capital). A net loss decreases owner's equity.

## Rationale

Profit belongs to the owner and increases their claim on the business assets.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Calculating owner's equity changes
- **THEN** Add net income (or subtract net loss) to beginning capital
- **OTHERWISE** Owner's equity will be misstated
