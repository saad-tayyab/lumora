# Ending Cash Must Match Balance Sheet

## Statement

The ending cash balance on the statement of cash flows must equal the cash balance reported on the balance sheet at the end of the period.

## Rationale

The statement of cash flows reconciles beginning to ending cash, which must agree with the balance sheet.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Completing the statement of cash flows
- **THEN** Beginning cash + net change in cash = ending cash, which must equal balance sheet cash
- **OTHERWISE** An error exists in the statement of cash flows or the balance sheet
