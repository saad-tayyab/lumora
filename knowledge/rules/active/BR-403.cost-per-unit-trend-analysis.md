# Cost Per Unit Trend Analysis

## Statement

When analyzing process costs across periods, compare cost per equivalent unit for each cost category to identify trends. Rising costs may indicate inefficiency, material waste, or machine maintenance issues.

## Rationale

Identifies cost performance issues and opportunities for process improvement.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing costs across multiple periods
- **THEN** Analyze cost per equivalent unit trends for materials and conversion separately
- **OTHERWISE** Missed opportunities for cost reduction
