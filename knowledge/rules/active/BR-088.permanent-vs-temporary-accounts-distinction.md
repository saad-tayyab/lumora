# Permanent vs. Temporary Accounts Distinction

## Statement

Only temporary accounts (revenues, expenses, drawing) are closed at year-end. Permanent accounts (assets, liabilities, owner's equity) are never closed and appear on the post-closing trial balance.

## Rationale

Permanent accounts reflect cumulative balances that must carry forward. Temporary accounts must start fresh each period.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Closing entries are prepared
- **THEN** Only revenue, expense, and drawing accounts are closed
- **OTHERWISE** Accounting equation becomes unbalanced or historical data is lost
