# Manufacturing Costs Flow

## Statement

Manufacturing costs flow from Materials Inventory to Work in Process (as direct materials are used) to Finished Goods (when production is complete) to Cost of Goods Sold (when products are sold).

## Rationale

Tracks the movement of costs through the manufacturing process matching the physical flow of goods.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Raw materials are requisitioned for production
- **THEN** Cost moves from Materials to Work in Process
- **OTHERWISE** Cost accumulation is disrupted
