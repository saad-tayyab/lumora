# Matching Principle

## Statement

Expenses must be matched with the revenues they helped generate in the same accounting period.

## Rationale

Ensures accurate measurement of net income by pairing costs with the revenues they produce.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Expenses are incurred to generate revenue
- **THEN** Record the expense in the same period as the related revenue
- **OTHERWISE** Net income will be overstated or understated
