# Departmental Responsibility for Variances Rule

## Statement

Different cost elements and their variances are the responsibility of different departments: Purchasing controls materials price, Production controls materials quantity and labor efficiency, HR controls labor rates.

## Rationale

Effective cost control requires holding the appropriate department accountable for the variances they can influence.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Assigning responsibility for cost variances
- **THEN** Assign price variances to Purchasing, quantity/efficiency variances to Production, rate variances to HR
- **OTHERWISE** Departments cannot be held accountable for variances outside their control
