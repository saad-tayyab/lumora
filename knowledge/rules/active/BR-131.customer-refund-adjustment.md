# Customer Refund Adjustment

## Statement

At period end, estimate the amount of customer refunds and allowances and the cost of estimated merchandise returns. Record an adjusting entry debiting Sales and crediting Customer Refunds Payable, and debiting Estimated Returns Inventory and crediting Cost of Goods Sold.

## Rationale

Revenue recognition requires that expected returns and allowances be matched against the period in which the sale occurred, following the matching principle.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing end-of-period financial statements
- **THEN** Record estimated customer refunds and estimated inventory returns
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue and COGS would be overstated in the current period
