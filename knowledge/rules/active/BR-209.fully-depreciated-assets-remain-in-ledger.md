# Fully Depreciated Assets Remain in Ledger

## Statement

If a fixed asset is still being used, its cost and accumulated depreciation should remain in the ledger even if fully depreciated.

## Rationale

Accounts must contain evidence of continued existence and support property/income tax reporting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A fixed asset is fully depreciated but still in use
- **THEN** Keep cost and accumulated depreciation in the ledger
- **OTHERWISE** Remove from accounts only upon disposal
