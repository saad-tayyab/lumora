# Carrying Amount Converges to Face Amount

## Statement

As discount or premium is amortized, the bond's carrying amount converges toward the face amount, reaching it exactly at maturity.

## Rationale

The unamortized discount/premium decreases to zero over the bond's life.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Discount or premium is being amortized each period
- **THEN** Carrying amount approaches face amount
- **OTHERWISE** Carrying amount equals face amount at maturity
