# Sales Forecast Based on Historical Trend Rule

## Statement

When forecasting sales, apply historical percentage increases or decreases to actual prior period results to estimate future sales volume.

## Rationale

Historical trends provide a reasonable baseline for sales forecasting when no other information is available.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Actual sales results differ from budgeted amounts by a certain percentage
- **THEN** Apply the same percentage change to forecast sales for the next period
- **OTHERWISE** Use the original budget assumptions for forecasting
