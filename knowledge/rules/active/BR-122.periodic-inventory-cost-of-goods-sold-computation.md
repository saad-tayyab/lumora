# Periodic Inventory Cost of Goods Sold Computation

## Statement

Under the periodic system, cost of goods sold is computed as: Beginning Inventory + Purchases - Purchase Returns & Allowances - Purchase Discounts + Freight In - Ending Inventory.

## Rationale

The periodic system does not maintain a continuous inventory record, so cost of goods sold must be determined indirectly at period end through a computation.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing financial statements under the periodic inventory system
- **THEN** Compute cost of goods sold using the periodic formula
- **OTHERWISE** Under perpetual system, cost of goods sold is recorded at each sale transaction
