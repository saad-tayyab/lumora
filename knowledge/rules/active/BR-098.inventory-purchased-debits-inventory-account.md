# Inventory Purchased Debits Inventory Account

## Statement

Under the perpetual inventory system, purchases of merchandise are recorded by debiting the Inventory account.

## Rationale

The Inventory account continuously tracks the cost of merchandise available for sale.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Merchandise is purchased under the perpetual inventory system
- **THEN** Debit Inventory, credit Cash or Accounts Payable
- **OTHERWISE** Inventory balance will be understated
