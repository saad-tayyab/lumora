# Financial Statement Preparation Order

## Statement

Financial statements must be prepared in a specific order: Income Statement first, then Statement of Owner's Equity, then Balance Sheet, and finally Statement of Cash Flows.

## Rationale

Each statement depends on data from the previous one. Net income flows to the statement of owner's equity, which flows to the balance sheet.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing financial statements at the end of an accounting period
- **THEN** Prepare statements in order: Income Statement → Statement of Owner's Equity → Balance Sheet → Statement of Cash Flows
- **OTHERWISE** Statements will contain incorrect or inconsistent data
