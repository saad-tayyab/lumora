# Gross Profit Computation

## Statement

Gross profit is computed as Sales minus Cost of Goods Sold. It represents profit before operating expenses.

## Rationale

Gross profit is a key measure of a merchandising business's core profitability before overhead costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a multiple-step income statement
- **THEN** Compute Gross Profit = Sales - COGS
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot determine operating income or assess merchandise profitability
