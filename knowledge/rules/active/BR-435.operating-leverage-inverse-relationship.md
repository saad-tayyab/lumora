# Operating Leverage Inverse Relationship

## Statement

Companies with higher fixed costs relative to variable costs have higher operating leverage, making them more sensitive to sales volume changes.

## Rationale

Operating leverage amplifies both gains and losses; understanding this helps in risk assessment and strategic planning.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing cost structures between companies or periods
- **THEN** Higher operating leverage means greater profit volatility with sales changes
- **OTHERWISE** Risk of overestimating stability in high-leverage companies
