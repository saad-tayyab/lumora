# Flavor Changeover Cost Allocation

## Statement

Flavor changeover costs should be allocated to production runs based on the number of changeovers. Higher-demand flavors produced in larger runs have lower changeover cost per unit.

## Rationale

Ensures accurate product costing by properly allocating batch-level costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Producing multiple flavors or product variants
- **THEN** Allocate changeover costs based on number of production runs
- **OTHERWISE** Misallocation of batch-level costs
