# Increase in Current Liability Increases Operating Cash Flow

## Statement

An increase in a current operating liability (e.g., accounts payable, accrued expenses) is added to net income in the indirect method.

## Rationale

An increase in a current liability means an expense was recognized but cash has not yet been paid.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A current operating liability increases during the period
- **THEN** Add the increase to net income in the operating activities section
- **OTHERWISE** A decrease in a current operating liability is deducted from net income
