# Accounts Receivable Collection Pattern Rule

## Statement

Credit sales collections should be estimated based on historical patterns, such as 70% collected in the month following sale and 30% in the second month following sale.

## Rationale

Credit sales are not collected immediately; realistic collection timing assumptions are essential for accurate cash flow forecasting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Estimating cash receipts from credit sales
- **THEN** Apply historical collection percentages to current and prior period credit sales
- **OTHERWISE** Cash budget will overstate available cash
