# Balanced Scorecard Completeness

## Statement

A balanced scorecard must include all four performance perspectives: Financial, Customer, Internal Processes, and Learning and Growth.

## Rationale

Including all four perspectives ensures a balanced view of performance, preventing overemphasis on financial metrics at the expense of long-term value drivers.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Creating or evaluating a balanced scorecard
- **THEN** Include all four performance perspectives
- **OTHERWISE** The scorecard is incomplete and may lead to unbalanced performance management
