# Investigate Controllable Variances

## Statement

Variances should be investigated to determine whether they are controllable by specific managers, and only controllable variances should be used to evaluate performance.

## Rationale

Managers should not be held accountable for variances beyond their control, such as market-wide price changes.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Variance is identified
- **THEN** Determine if it is controllable by the responsible manager
- **OTHERWISE** Consider external factors when evaluating performance
