# Bonds Issued at Discount When Contract Rate < Market Rate

## Statement

When the contract (stated) rate of interest on bonds is less than the market (effective) rate, the bonds will sell at a discount, meaning proceeds are less than face value.

## Rationale

Investors will pay less for bonds that offer below-market interest rates, requiring a discount to yield the market rate of return.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Contract rate of interest is less than the market rate
- **THEN** Bonds sell at a discount (below face value)
- **OTHERWISE** Bonds sell at par or premium
