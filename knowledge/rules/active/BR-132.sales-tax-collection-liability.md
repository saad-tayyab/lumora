# Sales Tax Collection Liability

## Statement

Sales tax collected from customers is a liability owed to the government. Debit Cash for the total collected, credit Sales for the sales price, and credit Sales Tax Payable for the tax amount.

## Rationale

The seller acts as an agent collecting tax on behalf of the government and must remit the collected tax to the appropriate authority.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Making a sale subject to sales tax
- **THEN** Collect sales tax and record as a liability (Sales Tax Payable)
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
