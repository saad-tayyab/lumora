# Long-Term Investments Exclusion from Asset Turnover

## Statement

Long-term investments are excluded from average total assets when computing asset turnover.

## Rationale

Long-term investments are unrelated to normal operations and sales.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing asset turnover ratio
- **THEN** Exclude long-term investments from total assets
- **OTHERWISE** Asset turnover ratio is distorted
