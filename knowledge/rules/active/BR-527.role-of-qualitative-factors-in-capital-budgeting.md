# Role of Qualitative Factors in Capital Budgeting

## Statement

Capital investment analysis should consider both quantitative (NPV, IRR, payback) and qualitative factors (strategic importance, competitive necessity, uncertainty). In highly uncertain environments, qualitative judgment may outweigh quantitative analysis.

## Rationale

Quantitative methods rely on assumptions that may be unreliable in high-uncertainty environments. Qualitative factors capture strategic value that financial metrics may miss.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Investment involves high uncertainty or strategic considerations
- **THEN** Use NPV as a reality check rather than sole decision criterion; weight qualitative factors appropriately
- **OTHERWISE** Quantitative analysis may be sufficient as the primary decision tool
