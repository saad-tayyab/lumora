# Four-Step Liquidation Process

## Statement

Partnership liquidation follows four steps in order: (1) sell noncash assets and record gain/loss, (2) allocate gain/loss to partners, (3) pay liabilities, (4) distribute remaining cash to partners based on capital balances.

## Rationale

The order of operations ensures that creditors are paid before partners and that capital balances accurately reflect each partner's equity.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A partnership is being liquidated
- **THEN** Follow the four-step liquidation process in order
- **OTHERWISE** The liquidation may not properly satisfy all claims
