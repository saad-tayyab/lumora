# Operating Cycle Differs by Industry

## Statement

The time to complete an operating cycle differs significantly among merchandising businesses based on the nature of their merchandise.

## Rationale

Grocery items sell quickly (short cycle); expensive jewelry may take months to sell (long cycle).

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing operating cycles across industries
- **THEN** Consider the nature of merchandise when evaluating cycle length
- **OTHERWISE** Cross-industry comparisons may be misleading
