# Credit Check At Least One Stage Required

## Statement

At least one of check_on_order or check_on_delivery must be true in the credit check policy.

## Rationale

A credit check policy with no check stages provides no credit control. At least one stage must be enabled to ensure credit limits are enforced at some point in the order lifecycle.

## Scope

- Applies to: All credit check policies (CON-SALES-016)
- Bounded Context: BC-SALES (Sales & Orders)
- Entities: CreditCheckPolicy

## Conditions

- WHEN a credit check policy is created or updated
- THEN at least one of check_on_order or check_on_delivery MUST be true
- OTHERWISE the policy is rejected with error `ERR_CREDIT_CHECK_NO_STAGE`

## Pseudocode

```
function validateCreditCheckPolicy(policy):
    if not policy.check_on_order AND not policy.check_on_delivery:
        throw CreditCheckNoStageError(policy.policy_id)
```

## Exceptions

- None — this rule has no exceptions.
