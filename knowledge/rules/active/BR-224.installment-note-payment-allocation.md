# Installment Note Payment Allocation

## Statement

Each installment note payment includes an interest component (interest rate × carrying amount at beginning of period) and a principal component (total payment minus interest). Interest expense decreases over time as principal is repaid.

## Rationale

As the carrying amount decreases with each payment, the interest component declines and the principal repayment increases.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Making periodic payments on an installment note
- **THEN** Debit Interest Expense (6% × beginning carrying amount), debit Notes Payable (payment minus interest), credit Cash
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
