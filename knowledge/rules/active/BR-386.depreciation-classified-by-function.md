# Depreciation Classified by Function

## Statement

Depreciation expense must be classified by function: factory equipment depreciation is manufacturing overhead; office equipment and building depreciation are period expenses.

## Rationale

Factory depreciation is a product cost (inventoriable); office depreciation is a period cost (expensed immediately).

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Depreciation is recorded
- **THEN** Factory equipment depreciation → Manufacturing Overhead; Office equipment/building depreciation → Depreciation Expense
- **OTHERWISE** Misclassification of costs
