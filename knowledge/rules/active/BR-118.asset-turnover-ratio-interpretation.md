# Asset Turnover Ratio Interpretation

## Statement

A higher asset turnover ratio indicates more effective use of assets to generate sales. The ratio should be compared across periods and against industry averages.

## Rationale

Asset turnover is a key measure of operational efficiency for merchandising businesses.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating a company's operating performance
- **THEN** Compute asset turnover ratio and compare over time and to industry
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot assess how efficiently the company uses its assets
