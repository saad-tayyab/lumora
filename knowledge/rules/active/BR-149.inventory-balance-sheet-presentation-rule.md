# Inventory Balance Sheet Presentation Rule

## Statement

Inventory is usually presented in the Current assets section of the balance sheet, following receivables. The methods used for determining cost and valuing inventory must be disclosed.

## Rationale

Provides users of financial statements with information about the liquidity and valuation basis of inventory.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a balance sheet
- **THEN** Inventory appears in Current Assets after receivables; costing method is disclosed
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements would be incomplete
