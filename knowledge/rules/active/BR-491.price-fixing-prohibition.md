# Price Fixing Prohibition

## Statement

Federal law prevents companies competing in similar markets from sharing cost and price information or conspiring to fix prices.

## Rationale

Price fixing violates antitrust laws and undermines fair competition in the marketplace.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Setting product prices
- **THEN** Do not coordinate pricing with competitors
- **OTHERWISE** Violates federal antitrust laws (FTC enforcement)
