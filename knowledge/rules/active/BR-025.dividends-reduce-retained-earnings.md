# Dividends Reduce Retained Earnings

## Statement

Dividends declared reduce retained earnings and are not reported on the income statement as an expense.

## Rationale

Dividends are a distribution of earnings, not an expense of generating revenue.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A corporation declares and pays dividends
- **THEN** Reduce Retained Earnings (not report as expense)
- **OTHERWISE** Net income will be understated
