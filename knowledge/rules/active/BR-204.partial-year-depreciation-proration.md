# Partial-Year Depreciation Proration

## Statement

When an asset is purchased during the year, depreciation is prorated based on the number of months the asset is in service. Assets placed in service during the first half of a month are treated as purchased on the first day of that month; second-half purchases treated as first day of next month.

## Rationale

Depreciation should reflect the actual time the asset was available for use.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Fixed asset is placed in service other than the first day of the fiscal year
- **THEN** Prorate depreciation based on months in service during the first year
- **OTHERWISE** Full year depreciation in the first year
