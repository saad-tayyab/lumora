# Fully Depreciated Asset Status

## Statement

When accumulated depreciation equals depreciable cost, the asset status automatically changes to 'fully_depreciated' and no further depreciation is posted.

## Rationale

Prevents over-depreciation beyond the depreciable cost (acquisition cost minus salvage value). The automatic status change provides clear visibility into asset lifecycle and eliminates the risk of erroneous additional depreciation entries.

## Scope

- Applies to: All depreciable fixed assets (BC-ASSET)
- Bounded Context: BC-ASSET (Fixed Asset Management)
- Entities: FixedAsset, DepreciationEntry

## Conditions

- **WHEN** accumulated depreciation equals or exceeds depreciable cost (acquisition cost minus salvage value)
- **THEN** asset status MUST automatically change to 'fully_depreciated'
- **AND** no further depreciation entries may be posted for this asset
- **OTHERWISE** the depreciation posting must be rejected with error `ERR_ASSET_FULLY_DEPRECIATED`

## Pseudocode

```
function postDepreciation(entry, assetId):
    asset = findFixedAsset(assetId)
    depreciableCost = asset.acquisition_cost - asset.salvage_value
    
    if asset.accumulated_depreciation >= depreciableCost:
        asset.status = 'fully_depreciated'
        persist(asset)
        throw FullyDepreciatedError(assetId)
    
    entry.amount = min(entry.amount, depreciableCost - asset.accumulated_depreciation)
    asset.accumulated_depreciation += entry.amount
    persist(entry)
    persist(asset)
```

## Exceptions

- If an asset's useful life is extended, the depreciable cost may be recalculated with appropriate approval.

## Related Rules

- BR-209: Accumulated depreciation cannot exceed depreciable cost

## Related Concepts

- FixedAsset: A long-term tangible asset used in business operations
- DepreciationEntry: An accounting entry recording depreciation expense
- Fully Depreciated: An asset status indicating no further depreciation will be posted
