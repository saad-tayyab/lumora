# Costs Recorded by Job

## Statement

Direct materials, direct labor, and applied manufacturing overhead are accumulated by job. Indirect materials and indirect labor are not recorded directly to individual jobs.

## Rationale

Ensures each job carries only its fair share of manufacturing costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Manufacturing costs are incurred
- **THEN** Direct costs traced to specific jobs; indirect costs pooled and allocated
- **OTHERWISE** Job costs are inaccurate
