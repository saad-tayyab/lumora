# Warranty vs. Recall Distinction

## Statement

A warranty is a promise made at time of sale that creates a liability at sale; a product recall is issued later and creates a liability only when the recall is announced.

## Rationale

Different timing for recognizing the liability based on the nature of the obligation.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A product warranty exists
- **THEN** Record liability at time of sale based on estimates
- **OTHERWISE** A product recall creates a new liability only when the recall is issued
