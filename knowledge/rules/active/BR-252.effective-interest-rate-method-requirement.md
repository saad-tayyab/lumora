# Effective Interest Rate Method Requirement

## Statement

The effective interest rate method is required by GAAP when its results differ significantly from the straight-line method.

## Rationale

The effective interest method more accurately reflects the true cost of borrowing based on the market rate at issuance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** There is a material difference between straight-line and effective interest amortization
- **THEN** Must use the effective interest rate method
- **OTHERWISE** Straight-line method may be used
