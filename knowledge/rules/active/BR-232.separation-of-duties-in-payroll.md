# Separation of Duties in Payroll

## Statement

The employee responsible for purchasing should be separated from the employee responsible for paying. This separation of duties helps prevent unauthorized purchases or payments.

## Rationale

Internal control requires segregation of duties to reduce the risk of fraud and errors.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Processing accounts payable and payroll
- **THEN** Ensure purchasing and payment functions are performed by different individuals
- **OTHERWISE** Risk of unauthorized transactions increases
