# Cost Reconciliation in Process Costing

## Statement

Total costs to account for (Beginning WIP costs + Costs added during period) must equal total costs accounted for (Costs of completed units + Costs of ending WIP).

## Rationale

Ensures all costs are properly allocated and no costs are lost or duplicated.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Completing a cost of production report
- **THEN** Total costs in = Total costs assigned out
- **OTHERWISE** Error in cost allocation
