# Accounting Equation Must Balance

## Statement

After every transaction, the accounting equation (Assets = Liabilities + Owner's Equity) must remain in balance.

## Rationale

The equation represents the fundamental relationship between what a business owns, owes, and the owner's claim. Every transaction affects at least two elements.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Any business transaction is recorded
- **THEN** Total assets must equal total liabilities plus owner's equity
- **OTHERWISE** An error has occurred in recording the transaction
