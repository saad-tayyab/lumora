# Bank Reconciliation Equality Rule

## Statement

The adjusted balance from the bank section and the adjusted balance from the company section of the bank reconciliation must be equal. If they are not equal, an item has been overlooked and must be found.

## Rationale

The adjusted balance represents the true cash balance that should appear on the balance sheet. Both sections must arrive at the same amount to ensure all reconciling items have been identified.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a bank reconciliation
- **THEN** Adjusted bank balance must equal adjusted company balance
- **OTHERWISE** An item has been overlooked or an error exists that must be corrected
