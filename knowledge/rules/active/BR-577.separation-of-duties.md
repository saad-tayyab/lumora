# Separation of Duties

## Statement

The responsibilities for authorizing transactions, recording transactions, and maintaining custody of assets should be separated among different employees.

## Rationale

Prevents any single employee from having control over all aspects of a transaction, reducing fraud risk.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Designing internal controls
- **THEN** Assign authorization, recording, and custody to different individuals
- **OTHERWISE** Increased risk of fraud and errors going undetected
