# Direct Materials Inventory Coordination Rule

## Statement

The timing of direct materials purchases must be coordinated with the production budget to ensure materials are available when needed.

## Rationale

Purchasing too early ties up cash in inventory; purchasing too late causes production delays.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the direct materials purchases budget
- **THEN** Calculate purchases as: materials needed for production + desired ending inventory - beginning inventory
- **OTHERWISE** Materials shortages or excess inventory will occur
