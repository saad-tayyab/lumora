# Cash Balance Sheet Presentation Rule

## Statement

Cash is normally listed as the first asset in the Current assets section of the balance sheet. Companies may present a single cash amount by combining all bank and cash fund accounts.

## Rationale

Cash is the most liquid asset and is presented first in current assets for financial statement users.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a balance sheet
- **THEN** List Cash (and cash equivalents) as first current asset
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements would not follow standard presentation
