# Management Discussion and Analysis Required

## Statement

Corporate annual reports must include Management's Discussion and Analysis (MD&A) of financial condition and results of operations.

## Rationale

Provides context and management's perspective on the financial data, helping users understand the company's performance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a corporate annual report
- **THEN** Include MD&A section with analysis of financial condition and results
- **OTHERWISE** Annual report will not meet regulatory requirements
