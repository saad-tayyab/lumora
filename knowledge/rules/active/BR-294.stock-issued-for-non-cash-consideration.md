# Stock Issued for Non-Cash Consideration

## Statement

When stock is issued for non-cash assets (land, buildings, equipment, services), the assets or services are recorded at their fair market value.

## Rationale

The fair market value provides the most reliable measure of the consideration received.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Stock is issued in exchange for non-cash assets or services
- **THEN** Record the assets or services at fair market value; credit stock accounts for par value; credit excess to Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
