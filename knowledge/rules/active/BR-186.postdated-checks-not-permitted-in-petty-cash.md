# Postdated Checks Not Permitted in Petty Cash

## Statement

Postdated employee checks or any checks not immediately negotiable should not be placed in the petty cash fund.

## Rationale

Petty cash should only contain currency and coins for immediate disbursement. Postdated checks violate the fund's purpose and internal control principles.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Managing petty cash fund contents
- **THEN** Only cash and valid receipts should be in the fund
- **OTHERWISE** Fund integrity is compromised and proper accounting is hindered
