# Product Cost vs Period Cost Classification

## Statement

Product costs are inventoried and expensed as COGS when sold. Period costs are expensed in the period incurred.

## Rationale

Ensures proper matching of costs with revenue per the matching principle.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A cost is incurred
- **THEN** Classify as product cost (manufacturing) or period cost (selling/admin)
- **OTHERWISE** Cost will be misclassified, distorting inventory values and income
