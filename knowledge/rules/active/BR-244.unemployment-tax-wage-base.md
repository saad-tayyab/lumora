# Unemployment Tax Wage Base

## Statement

FUTA and SUTA taxes apply only to the first $7,000 (or state-specified amount) of each employee's annual earnings.

## Rationale

Federal and state unemployment taxes are capped at a wage base to limit employer liability.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An employee's year-to-date earnings are below the wage base
- **THEN** Unemployment taxes apply to earnings up to the cap
- **OTHERWISE** No additional unemployment tax is owed on excess earnings
