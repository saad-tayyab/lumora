# Opportunity Cost Inclusion Rule

## Statement

When evaluating a decision, the opportunity cost (income foregone from the best alternative use of funds) must be included as a cost of the proposed course of action.

## Rationale

Failing to include opportunity costs understates the true cost of a decision and may lead to accepting investments that return less than available alternatives.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A proposed investment uses funds that could earn a return elsewhere
- **THEN** Include the foregone return as an opportunity cost in the analysis
- **OTHERWISE** No opportunity cost to include
