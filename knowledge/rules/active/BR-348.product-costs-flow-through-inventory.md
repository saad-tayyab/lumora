# Product Costs Flow Through Inventory

## Statement

Product costs (direct materials, direct labor, manufacturing overhead) are first recorded as inventory on the balance sheet, then expensed as cost of goods sold on the income statement when the inventory is sold.

## Rationale

Matching principle requires costs to be expensed in the same period as the related revenue.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Manufacturing costs are incurred
- **THEN** Record as inventory assets on balance sheet
- **OTHERWISE** Costs would be expensed prematurely before revenue is earned
