# Bond Proceeds Calculation

## Statement

Proceeds from issuing bonds equal the issue price, which may be at a discount (below face value), premium (above face value), or par (equal to face value).

## Rationale

Determines the actual cash received and the carrying value of the bond liability.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Bonds are issued
- **THEN** Record proceeds equal to the issue price; recognize discount or premium as the difference from face value
- **OTHERWISE** Bond liability cannot be properly recorded
