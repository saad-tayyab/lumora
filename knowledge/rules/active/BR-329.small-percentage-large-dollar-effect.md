# Small Percentage Large Dollar Effect

## Statement

A small percentage change in a financial statement item can have a large dollar effect when applied to a large base amount, such as sales.

## Rationale

Understanding this helps analysts recognize that seemingly minor percentage shifts can represent significant financial impacts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing percentage changes in financial statement items
- **THEN** Consider both the percentage and the dollar amount to assess materiality
- **OTHERWISE** Material impacts may be overlooked if only percentages are examined
