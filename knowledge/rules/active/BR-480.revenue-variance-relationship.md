# Revenue Variance Relationship

## Statement

Total Revenue Variance equals Revenue Price Variance plus Revenue Volume Variance.

## Rationale

Revenue variances decompose total revenue difference into price and volume components for analysis.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing total revenue variance
- **THEN** Separate into price variance (selling price impact) and volume variance (quantity impact)
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot identify root cause of revenue differences
