# Aging Days Calculation

## Statement

To determine days past due: (1) find the due date by adding credit terms days to the invoice date excluding the invoice date, (2) subtract due date from the analysis date, (3) place the account in the appropriate age class.

## Rationale

Accurately classifying receivables by age is essential for estimating uncollectible accounts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Performing an aging analysis of receivables
- **THEN** Days past due = Analysis date - Due date; classify into appropriate age class
- **OTHERWISE** Receivables will be misclassified and the allowance estimate will be inaccurate
