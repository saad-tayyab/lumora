# Fair Value Method - Sale of Stock

## Statement

When selling equity securities under the fair value method, record a gain if proceeds exceed the investment account balance, or a loss if proceeds are less than the balance.

## Rationale

The sale results in a realized gain or loss that must be recognized on the income statement.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Selling equity securities accounted for under the fair value method
- **THEN** Compare sale proceeds to investment account balance; record gain or loss accordingly
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
