# Contra Account Balance Is Opposite to Related Account

## Statement

A contra account always has a normal balance opposite to its related account. Accumulated Depreciation (contra asset) has a credit balance, while the related fixed asset has a debit balance.

## Rationale

The contra account is deducted from the related account on the balance sheet. Having an opposite balance allows simple subtraction to arrive at book value.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A contra account is created or updated
- **THEN** Its normal balance must be the opposite of the related account
- **OTHERWISE** The net book value calculation would be incorrect
