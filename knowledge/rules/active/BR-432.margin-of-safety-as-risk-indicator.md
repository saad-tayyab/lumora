# Margin of Safety as Risk Indicator

## Statement

The margin of safety measures the cushion between actual sales and break-even sales; a higher margin indicates lower risk of operating at a loss.

## Rationale

Provides management with a measure of how much sales can decline before losses are incurred.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating business risk or performance
- **THEN** Compute margin of safety in dollars and as a percentage of sales
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot assess the risk of operating at a loss
