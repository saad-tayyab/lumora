# Expired Tax Rates Blocked

## Statement

Tax rates with an expiry date in the past cannot be applied to new transactions. Only tax rates with status 'active' and future or current effective dates are eligible.

## Rationale

Prevents the application of outdated or superseded tax rates to new transactions, ensuring compliance with current tax regulations and accurate tax calculations.

## Scope

- Applies to: All tax rate applications to new transactions (BC-TAX)
- Bounded Context: BC-TAX (Tax Management)
- Entities: TaxRate, TaxRateVersion

## Conditions

- **WHEN** a tax rate is evaluated for application to a new transaction
- **THEN** the tax rate MUST have status 'active' AND its effective date MUST be in the past or present AND its expiry date MUST be in the future or absent
- **OTHERWISE** the tax rate must be rejected with error `ERR_TAX_RATE_EXPIRED`

## Pseudocode

```
function applyTaxRate(taxRateId, transactionDate):
    taxRate = findTaxRate(taxRateId)
    
    if taxRate.status != 'active':
        throw TaxRateExpiredError(taxRateId, 'inactive')
    
    if taxRate.effective_date > transactionDate:
        throw TaxRateExpiredError(taxRateId, 'not_yet_effective')
    
    if taxRate.expiry_date && taxRate.expiry_date < transactionDate:
        throw TaxRateExpiredError(taxRateId, 'expired')
    
    return taxRate
```

## Exceptions

- None — expired tax rates must never be applied to new transactions.

## Related Rules

- INV-TAX-001: Every tax rate must have an effective date and optional expiry date for temporal versioning
- BR-017: Expired tax rates cannot be applied to new transactions

## Related Concepts

- TaxRate: A specific tax percentage or amount applied to transactions
- TaxRateVersion: A temporal record of a tax rate with effective and expiry dates
