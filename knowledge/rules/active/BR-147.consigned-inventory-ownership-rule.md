# Consigned Inventory Ownership Rule

## Statement

Consigned inventory belongs to the consignor, not the consignee. The consignee holds the goods but does not own them.

## Rationale

Ensures inventory is properly reported on the correct party's balance sheet and prevents double-counting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Goods are held on consignment
- **THEN** Title remains with consignor; goods are included in consignor's inventory
- **OTHERWISE** Goods would be included in consignee's inventory (which is incorrect)
