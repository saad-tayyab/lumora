# Indirect Costs Become Manufacturing Overhead

## Statement

Indirect materials and indirect labor costs that are not directly traceable to individual products are classified as manufacturing overhead.

## Rationale

These costs are necessary for production but cannot be economically traced to specific units, so they are allocated as overhead.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A manufacturing cost cannot be directly traced to a specific product
- **THEN** Classify as manufacturing overhead
- **OTHERWISE** Product costs would be understated
