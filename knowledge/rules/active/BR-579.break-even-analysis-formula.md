# Break-Even Analysis Formula

## Statement

Break-even point in units equals fixed costs divided by unit contribution margin.

## Rationale

Identifies the minimum sales volume needed to avoid a loss.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Determining the break-even point
- **THEN** Compute fixed costs / (selling price per unit - variable cost per unit)
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot determine minimum sales target
