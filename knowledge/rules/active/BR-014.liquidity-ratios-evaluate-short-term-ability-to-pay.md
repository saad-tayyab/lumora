# Liquidity Ratios Evaluate Short-Term Ability to Pay

## Statement

Working capital and the current ratio are the primary measures for evaluating a business's short-term liquidity.

## Rationale

These ratios indicate whether a company has sufficient current assets to meet its current liabilities as they come due.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Assessing short-term financial health
- **THEN** Compute working capital and current ratio
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot properly evaluate short-term solvency
