# Stock Dividend Capitalizes Retained Earnings at Market Value

## Statement

A stock dividend is recorded by capitalizing Retained Earnings at the fair market value of the shares to be issued (for small stock dividends under 20-25%).

## Rationale

The fair market value represents the economic value transferred from retained earnings to paid-in capital.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A small stock dividend (less than 20-25% of outstanding shares) is declared
- **THEN** Debit Retained Earnings for market value of shares; credit Stock Dividends Distributable for par value; credit Paid-In Capital in Excess of Par for the difference
- **OTHERWISE** For large stock dividends (over 20-25%), capitalize at par value only
