# Contingent Liability Recognition

## Statement

A contingent liability must be recorded if it is probable and the amount can be reasonably estimated.

## Rationale

Ensures financial statements reflect all probable obligations for faithful representation.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A contingent liability is probable AND the amount is estimable
- **THEN** Record a journal entry debiting an expense and crediting a liability
- **OTHERWISE** Disclose in notes if reasonably possible; ignore if remote
