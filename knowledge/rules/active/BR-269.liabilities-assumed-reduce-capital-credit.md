# Liabilities Assumed Reduce Capital Credit

## Statement

When a partnership assumes liabilities from a contributing partner, the liabilities assumed reduce the partner's capital credit.

## Rationale

The partner's net investment equals assets contributed minus liabilities assumed, so the capital account must reflect this net amount.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A partner contributes assets and the partnership assumes liabilities
- **THEN** Capital credit = Assets at market value − Liabilities assumed
- **OTHERWISE** The partner's capital would be overstated
