# Business Entity Transaction Classification

## Statement

Each transaction must be classified as belonging to a specific business entity. Personal transactions of the owner are not recorded in the business's books.

## Rationale

Maintains the integrity of the business entity assumption, ensuring financial statements reflect only business activities.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A transaction occurs involving the business owner
- **THEN** Determine if the transaction is for business purposes or personal purposes; only business transactions are recorded in the entity's books
- **OTHERWISE** Personal and business finances would be commingled, violating the entity assumption
