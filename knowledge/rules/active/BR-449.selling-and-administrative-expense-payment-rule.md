# Selling and Administrative Expense Payment Rule

## Statement

All selling and administrative expenses are typically paid in cash in the period they are incurred.

## Rationale

These expenses generally do not involve accounts payable timing like manufacturing costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the cash budget
- **THEN** Include full amount of selling and administrative expenses as cash disbursements in the period
- **OTHERWISE** Cash budget will understate cash outflows
