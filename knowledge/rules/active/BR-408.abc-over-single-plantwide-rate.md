# ABC Over Single Plantwide Rate

## Statement

Activity-based costing provides more accurate product costs than a single plantwide overhead rate when products consume overhead activities in proportions different from the single allocation base.

## Rationale

Single plantwide rates can distort product costs, leading to incorrect pricing and product mix decisions.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Products consume overhead activities differently relative to a single volume-based driver
- **THEN** Use ABC to avoid product cost distortion
- **OTHERWISE** Product costs may be materially misstated, causing pricing errors
