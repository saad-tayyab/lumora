# Retained Earnings Restrictions Disclosure

## Statement

Restrictions on retained earnings (legal, contractual, or discretionary) must be disclosed on the balance sheet or in notes to the financial statements.

## Rationale

Users of financial statements need to know how much retained earnings is available for dividends.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Retained earnings are restricted by law, contract, or board action
- **THEN** Disclose the restriction type and amount on the balance sheet or in notes
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
