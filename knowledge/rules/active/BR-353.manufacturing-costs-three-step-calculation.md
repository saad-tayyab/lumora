# Manufacturing Costs Three-Step Calculation

## Statement

Cost of goods manufactured is determined in three steps: (1) Determine cost of direct materials used, (2) Determine total manufacturing costs incurred, (3) Determine cost of goods manufactured.

## Rationale

The three-step process ensures all manufacturing costs are properly accumulated and assigned to products completed during the period.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing statement of cost of goods manufactured
- **THEN** Follow the three-step calculation process
- **OTHERWISE** Manufacturing costs may be misstated
