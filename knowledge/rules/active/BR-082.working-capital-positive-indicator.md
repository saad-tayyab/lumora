# Working Capital Positive Indicator

## Statement

Positive working capital indicates a company is able to pay its current liabilities; increasing working capital improves liquidity.

## Rationale

Working capital measures the buffer between short-term assets and short-term obligations.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Analyzing a company's short-term financial health
- **THEN** Working capital = Current Assets - Current Liabilities; positive is favorable
- **OTHERWISE** Negative working capital signals potential liquidity problems
