# LCM Application Methods

## Statement

The lower of cost or market method can be applied on an item-by-item basis, by major class or category, or to total inventory. Item-by-item application always yields the lowest inventory value, while total application yields the highest.

## Rationale

Different application methods produce different inventory valuations, and the choice affects the amount of market decline recognized in COGS.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Applying LCM method
- **THEN** Choose application basis (item, category, or total) consistently
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
