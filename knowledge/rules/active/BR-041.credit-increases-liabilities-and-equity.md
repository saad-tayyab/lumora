# Credit Increases Liabilities and Equity

## Statement

Increases in liability and owner's equity (capital) accounts are recorded on the credit (right) side.

## Rationale

Liability and equity accounts have the opposite normal balance from assets.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A liability or equity account increases
- **THEN** Record the increase on the credit (right) side
- **OTHERWISE** The account balance will be incorrect
