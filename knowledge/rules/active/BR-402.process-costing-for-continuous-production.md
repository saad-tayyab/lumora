# Process Costing for Continuous Production

## Statement

Process costing is most appropriate for businesses where manufacturing is conducted by continuous operations involving a series of uniform production processes, such as oil refining, food processing, or chemical manufacturing.

## Rationale

Process costing efficiently handles homogeneous products flowing through continuous processes.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Determining appropriate costing method
- **THEN** Use process costing for continuous, homogeneous production
- **OTHERWISE** Use job order costing for unique, custom products
