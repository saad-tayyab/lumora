# Noncash Assets Recorded at Market Value

## Statement

When forming a partnership, noncash assets contributed by partners are recorded at their current market values as agreed upon by the partners, not at their previous book values.

## Rationale

The partnership is a new entity, and assets should be recorded at their fair value at the date of contribution to accurately reflect the partner's capital investment.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A partner contributes noncash assets to a new partnership
- **THEN** Record assets at agreed-upon current market values
- **OTHERWISE** Assets would be misstated relative to their true contribution value
