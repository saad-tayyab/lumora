# Break-Even Point Formula

## Statement

Break-Even Point (units) = Fixed Costs / Unit Contribution Margin. Break-Even Point (sales dollars) = Fixed Costs / Contribution Margin Ratio.

## Rationale

Determines the minimum sales volume needed to avoid losses.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Calculating the break-even point
- **THEN** Divide fixed costs by unit contribution margin (or CM ratio)
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot determine the sales level needed to cover all costs
