# Quick Assets Exclusion

## Statement

Quick assets include cash, temporary investments, and receivables but exclude inventories and prepaid assets.

## Rationale

Inventories and prepaid assets cannot be quickly converted to cash to pay liabilities.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing quick ratio
- **THEN** Exclude inventories and prepaid expenses from current assets
- **OTHERWISE** Quick ratio overstates instant debt-paying ability
