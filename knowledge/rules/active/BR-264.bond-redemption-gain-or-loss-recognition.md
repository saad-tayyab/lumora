# Bond Redemption Gain or Loss Recognition

## Statement

When bonds are redeemed before maturity, a gain or loss is recognized equal to the difference between the carrying amount of the bonds and the redemption price.

## Rationale

The gain or loss reflects the difference between what the company owes (carrying amount) and what it pays to retire the debt early.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Bonds are redeemed before maturity
- **THEN** Record gain if carrying amount > redemption price, or loss if carrying amount < redemption price
- **OTHERWISE** No gain or loss at maturity redemption
