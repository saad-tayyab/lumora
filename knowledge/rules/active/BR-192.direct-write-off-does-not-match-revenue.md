# Direct Write-Off Does Not Match Revenue

## Statement

The direct write-off method does not properly match bad debt expense with the revenue of the period in which the sale occurred, because expense is recognized in a different period than the sale.

## Rationale

This violates the matching principle, making the direct write-off method unacceptable under GAAP when bad debts are material.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating the direct write-off method for GAAP compliance
- **THEN** Expense recognition is delayed until the specific account is deemed uncollectible
- **OTHERWISE** Revenue and related expenses are not matched in the same period
