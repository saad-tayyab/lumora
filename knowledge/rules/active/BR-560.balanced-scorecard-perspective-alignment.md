# Balanced Scorecard Perspective Alignment

## Statement

Each strategic objective in a balanced scorecard must belong to one of four perspectives (financial, customer, internal process, learning and growth) and measures must be linked through cause-and-effect relationships.

## Rationale

The scorecard framework ensures strategy is translated into actionable measures across all dimensions of organizational performance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Designing or evaluating a balanced scorecard
- **THEN** Ensure all four perspectives are addressed and objectives are linked through measurable cause-and-effect chains
- **OTHERWISE** The scorecard may miss critical strategic dimensions or fail to drive desired behaviors
