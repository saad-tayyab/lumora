# Assign Costs to Completed Units

## Statement

Costs assigned to completed units include: (1) costs from beginning WIP carried forward, (2) costs to complete beginning WIP, and (3) costs of units started and completed in the current period.

## Rationale

Properly allocates costs between beginning inventory and current period production under FIFO.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Units are completed and transferred out
- **THEN** Assign beginning WIP costs + costs to complete + costs of started and completed units
- **OTHERWISE** Units remain in WIP
