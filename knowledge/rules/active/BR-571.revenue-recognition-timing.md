# Revenue Recognition Timing

## Statement

Revenues must be recorded when earned — when services are performed or products are delivered to customers.

## Rationale

Ensures financial statements reflect economic reality of when value is transferred.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A sale is made or service is performed
- **THEN** Record revenue at the amount of consideration received
- **OTHERWISE** Do not recognize revenue until performance obligation is satisfied
