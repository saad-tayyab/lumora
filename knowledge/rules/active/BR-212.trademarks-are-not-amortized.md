# Trademarks Are Not Amortized

## Statement

Trademarks have indefinite useful lives and are not amortized. They are reviewed periodically for impaired value and written down when impaired.

## Rationale

Trademarks can be renewed indefinitely and maintain their value as long as they are actively used.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A trademark is carried on the balance sheet
- **THEN** Do not amortize; test for impairment periodically
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
