# COGS Recorded at Each Sale

## Statement

Under the perpetual inventory system, the cost of goods sold and the reduction in inventory are recorded at the time of each sale.

## Rationale

Ensures the Inventory account always reflects the current amount of merchandise on hand.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A sale of merchandise occurs under the perpetual inventory system
- **THEN** Record Cost of Goods Sold (debit) and Inventory (credit)
- **OTHERWISE** Inventory will be overstated and COGS understated
