# Unearned Revenue Recognition

## Statement

Unearned revenue must be recognized as earned revenue when the related goods are delivered or services are performed.

## Rationale

Liabilities must be reduced and revenue recognized when the obligation is fulfilled.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Goods are delivered or services performed for advance payments
- **THEN** An adjusting entry debits unearned revenue and credits the revenue account
- **OTHERWISE** Liabilities are overstated and revenues understated
