# Discounted Note Proceeds Computation

## Statement

For a discounted note: Discount = Face Amount × Discount Rate × (Term / 365). Proceeds = Face Amount - Discount. The borrower receives the proceeds and repays the full face amount at maturity.

## Rationale

The discount represents prepaid interest deducted from the face amount before disbursement.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Issuing a discounted note
- **THEN** Record Cash for proceeds, Interest Expense for discount, and Notes Payable for face amount
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
