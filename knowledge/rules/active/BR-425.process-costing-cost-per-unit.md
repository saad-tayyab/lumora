# Process Costing Cost Per Unit

## Statement

In process costing, the cost per unit for each department is calculated by dividing the department's total cost by the number of units processed through that department.

## Rationale

Process costing assigns costs based on the average cost per unit of output from each production department.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing product costs using process costing
- **THEN** Divide each department's total cost by its units of production
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot determine the per-unit cost of the product
