# Adjusting Entries Are Made at Period End Only

## Statement

Adjusting entries are recorded only at the end of an accounting period, never during the period. They are never involving cash.

## Rationale

Adjusting entries ensure revenues and expenses are matched to the correct period under accrual accounting. They are internal entries, not triggered by external transactions.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** The accounting period ends
- **THEN** All necessary adjusting entries are journalized and posted before financial statements are prepared
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements will not reflect the true financial position and results of operations
