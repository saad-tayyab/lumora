# Depreciation Add-Back

## Statement

When using the indirect method, depreciation expense must be added back to net income because it is a noncash expense that reduced net income but did not involve a cash outflow.

## Rationale

Depreciation is an allocation of cost, not a cash payment, so it must be reversed to convert net income to cash basis.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Computing cash flows from operating activities using the indirect method
- **THEN** Add depreciation expense to net income
- **OTHERWISE** Cash flows from operating activities will be understated
