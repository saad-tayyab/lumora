# Sales Mix Constancy Assumption

## Statement

When computing break-even for multiple products, the sales mix percentages are assumed to remain constant across the relevant range.

## Rationale

The weighted average contribution margin approach requires a stable sales mix to produce a valid break-even point.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Performing multi-product break-even analysis
- **THEN** Assume the sales mix ratio remains constant as total volume changes
- **OTHERWISE** The break-even computation is invalid and must be recomputed with the new mix
