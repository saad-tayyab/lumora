# Supplier Finance Program Disclosure

## Statement

Companies participating in supplier finance programs must provide qualitative and quantitative disclosures about the nature, activity, changes, and magnitude of such programs.

## Rationale

ASU 2022-04 requires transparency about obligations that may be affected by third-party financing arrangements.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A company has supplier obligations confirmed under a supplier finance program
- **THEN** Disclose the outstanding amount and note it is included in accounts payable on the balance sheet
- **OTHERWISE** No disclosure is required if the company does not participate in such programs
