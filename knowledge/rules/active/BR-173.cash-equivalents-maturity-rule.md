# Cash Equivalents Maturity Rule

## Statement

To be classified as a cash equivalent, the investment must have an original maturity of three months or less when purchased.

## Rationale

Short-term, highly liquid investments that are readily convertible to known amounts of cash are equivalent to cash for reporting purposes.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Classifying short-term investments
- **THEN** Investments with original maturity of 3 months or less are cash equivalents
- **OTHERWISE** Investments are classified as short-term investments
