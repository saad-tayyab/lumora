# GAAP Period Expense Rule

## Statement

Generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) require that selling and administrative expenses be reported as period expenses on the income statement, not as product costs.

## Rationale

GAAP requires matching principle adherence; selling/admin costs are not manufacturing costs and must be expensed as incurred.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Reporting selling and administrative expenses for external financial statements
- **THEN** Report as period expenses, not inventoriable product costs
- **OTHERWISE** Financial statements will not comply with GAAP
