# Income-Sharing Ratio Not Used for Cash Distribution

## Statement

The income-sharing ratio determines how gains and losses are divided, but it is NOT used to determine how cash is distributed to partners upon liquidation.

## Rationale

Cash distribution follows capital balances, which reflect each partner's actual equity after all gains and losses have been allocated.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Distributing cash during liquidation
- **THEN** Use capital account balances, not income-sharing ratio
- **OTHERWISE** Partners may receive more or less than their equity
