# Internal Control Over Payroll

## Statement

Payroll processing should include segregation of duties: timekeeping, payroll preparation, check signing, and bank reconciliation should be performed by different individuals.

## Rationale

Segregation of duties prevents fraud and errors in payroll processing.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Payroll is being processed
- **THEN** No single person should control all steps from timekeeping to check distribution
- **OTHERWISE** Risk of payroll fraud or undetected errors
