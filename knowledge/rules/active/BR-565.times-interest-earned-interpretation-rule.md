# Times Interest Earned Interpretation Rule

## Statement

A higher times interest earned ratio indicates greater ability to meet interest obligations; a declining ratio signals increasing financial risk.

## Rationale

Measures the margin of safety for creditors by showing how many times operating income covers interest expense.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Assessing a company's debt-servicing capacity
- **THEN** Compare times interest earned across periods and against industry benchmarks
- **OTHERWISE** Credit risk may be underestimated
