# Straight-Line Amortization Equal Allocation

## Statement

Under the straight-line method, equal amounts of discount or premium are amortized to interest expense each period.

## Rationale

Simplifies amortization by spreading the total discount or premium evenly over the bond's life.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Amortizing bond discount or premium using the straight-line method
- **THEN** Periodic amortization = Total discount or premium ÷ Number of interest periods
- **OTHERWISE** Use the effective interest rate method
