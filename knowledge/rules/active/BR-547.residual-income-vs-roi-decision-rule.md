# Residual Income vs ROI Decision Rule

## Statement

Managers evaluated on residual income will accept all projects with positive residual income, while managers evaluated on ROI may reject profitable projects that reduce their overall ROI.

## Rationale

Residual income aligns manager incentives with company goals by encouraging acceptance of any project that exceeds the minimum required return, whereas ROI can lead to suboptimal decisions.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Deciding whether to accept a new investment project
- **THEN** If evaluated on residual income, accept if residual income > 0; if evaluated on ROI, accept only if project ROI > current division ROI
- **OTHERWISE** May lead to suboptimal investment decisions
