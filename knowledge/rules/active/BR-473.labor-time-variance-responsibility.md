# Labor Time Variance Responsibility

## Statement

Production supervisors are normally responsible for the direct labor time variance, as they manage workforce efficiency and production processes.

## Rationale

Time variances reflect labor efficiency, which is influenced by supervision, training, and working conditions.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Labor time variance is unfavorable
- **THEN** Investigate causes such as high employee turnover or equipment issues
- **OTHERWISE** Favorable time variance may indicate efficient operations
