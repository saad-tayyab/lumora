# Multiperiod Budget Dependency Rule

## Statement

When preparing multiperiod budgets, ensure that ending inventory balances of one period become beginning inventory balances of the next period.

## Rationale

Budgets across periods are interdependent; consistency in inventory flows ensures accurate projections.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing multiperiod budgets (e.g., quarterly budgets)
- **THEN** Link ending inventory of period N to beginning inventory of period N+1
- **OTHERWISE** Inventory balances will be inconsistent across periods
