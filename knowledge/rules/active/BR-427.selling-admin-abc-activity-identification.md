# Selling/Admin ABC Activity Identification

## Statement

When applying ABC to selling and administrative expenses, identify specific activities (order writing, technical support, promotional support, order entry, returns processing, shipping) and their respective activity bases.

## Rationale

Accurate identification of activities and their drivers is essential for meaningful cost allocation.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Implementing ABC for selling and administrative expenses
- **THEN** Identify discrete selling/admin activities and assign appropriate activity bases to each
- **OTHERWISE** Cost allocation will not reflect actual resource consumption
