# Service Job Completion and Billing

## Statement

When a service job is completed and the client is billed, transfer the accumulated costs from Work in Process to Cost of Services.

## Rationale

Matches service costs with the revenue earned from the service engagement.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A service job is completed and billed
- **THEN** Transfer costs from WIP to Cost of Services
- **OTHERWISE** Costs remain in WIP and income statement understates expenses
