# Cash Register Daily Count Rule

## Statement

At the end of each shift, the clerk and supervisor must count the cash in the clerk's cash drawer. The amount should equal the beginning cash plus cash sales for the day.

## Rationale

Ensures all cash is properly accounted for and detects shortages or overages promptly.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** End of a work shift with a cash register
- **THEN** Count cash and compare to register records
- **OTHERWISE** Cash shortages may go undetected
