# Break-Even Equality

## Statement

At the break-even point, total contribution margin exactly equals total fixed costs, resulting in zero operating income.

## Rationale

This is the fundamental equation of CVP analysis: revenues equal costs when contribution margin covers fixed costs.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Operating at the break-even point
- **THEN** Total Contribution Margin = Total Fixed Costs and Operating Income = 0
- **OTHERWISE** The company is either generating profit (above break-even) or incurring a loss (below break-even)
