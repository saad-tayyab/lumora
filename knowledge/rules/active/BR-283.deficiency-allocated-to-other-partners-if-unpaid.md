# Deficiency Allocated to Other Partners If Unpaid

## Statement

If a partner with a capital deficiency cannot pay, the deficiency is allocated to the remaining partners with positive capital balances in their income-sharing ratio.

## Rationale

The remaining partners bear the loss from the deficient partner's inability to pay, as they share in all partnership losses.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A partner declares bankruptcy or is unable to pay their capital deficiency
- **THEN** Allocate the deficiency to remaining partners in their income-sharing ratio
- **OTHERWISE** Cash distribution would be incomplete
