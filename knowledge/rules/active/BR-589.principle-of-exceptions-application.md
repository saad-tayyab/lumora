# Principle of Exceptions Application

## Statement

Managers should focus attention on areas that deviate significantly from expected results (budget or standard) rather than reviewing all areas equally.

## Rationale

Maximizes management efficiency by directing attention to areas requiring corrective action.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Reviewing operational performance and variances
- **THEN** Investigate and address significant variances that exceed predetermined thresholds
- **OTHERWISE** Defer review of areas performing within acceptable tolerances
