# Cash Payback Desirability Rule

## Statement

A shorter cash payback period is more desirable because cash is recovered sooner for reinvestment, there is less risk from changing conditions, and debt can be repaid faster.

## Rationale

Early cash recovery reduces risk and improves liquidity, allowing reinvestment in other profitable projects.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing capital investment proposals using the cash payback method
- **THEN** Shorter payback period is preferred; proposals with payback periods exceeding management's threshold are rejected
- **OTHERWISE** Use other evaluation methods
