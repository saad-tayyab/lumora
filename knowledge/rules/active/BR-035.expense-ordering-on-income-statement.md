# Expense Ordering on Income Statement

## Statement

Expenses on the income statement should be listed in order of magnitude (largest to smallest), with Miscellaneous Expense always listed last regardless of amount.

## Rationale

Highlights the most significant expenses for management and读者 decision-making.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing an income statement
- **THEN** List expenses from largest to smallest, with miscellaneous last
- **OTHERWISE** Statement does not follow standard presentation conventions
