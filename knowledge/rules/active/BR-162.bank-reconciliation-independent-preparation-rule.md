# Bank Reconciliation Independent Preparation Rule

## Statement

To enhance internal control, the bank reconciliation should be prepared by an employee who does not take part in or record cash transactions.

## Rationale

Prevents an employee who handles cash from also reconciling the bank statement, which could allow them to steal deposits and hide the theft by omitting items from both records and the reconciliation.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing a bank reconciliation
- **THEN** Use an employee independent of cash handling and recording
- **OTHERWISE** Employee could steal cash and manipulate both records and reconciliation
