# Equivalent Units for Materials at Beginning of Process

## Statement

When materials are added at the beginning of the process, beginning work in process inventory receives 0% equivalent units for materials in the current period because all materials were added in the prior period.

## Rationale

Avoids double-counting materials already added to beginning inventory in the prior period.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Materials are added at the beginning of the process and beginning WIP exists
- **THEN** Beginning WIP equivalent units for materials = 0
- **OTHERWISE** Compute equivalent units based on actual materials addition timing
