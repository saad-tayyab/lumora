# Working Capital Limitation

## Statement

Working capital is difficult to use for comparing companies of different sizes.

## Rationale

A dollar amount of working capital means different things for small vs large companies.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing companies of different sizes
- **THEN** Use current ratio instead of working capital
- **OTHERWISE** Working capital may be misleading
