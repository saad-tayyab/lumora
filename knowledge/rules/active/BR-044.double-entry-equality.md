# Double-Entry Equality

## Statement

For every journal entry, the total dollar amount of debits must equal the total dollar amount of credits.

## Rationale

This is the fundamental rule of double-entry bookkeeping. It ensures the accounting equation (Assets = Liabilities + Equity) remains in balance after every transaction.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A journal entry is recorded
- **THEN** Sum of all debit amounts equals sum of all credit amounts
- **OTHERWISE** The entry is rejected and cannot be posted to the ledger
