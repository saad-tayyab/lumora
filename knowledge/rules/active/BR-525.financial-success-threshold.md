# Financial Success Threshold

## Statement

An investment is expected to be financially successful if its NPV is positive at the desired rate of return. A negative NPV indicates the investment does not meet the required return threshold.

## Rationale

The NPV decision rule provides a clear threshold for determining whether an investment creates or destroys value.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** NPV > 0 at the desired rate of return
- **THEN** Investment is expected to be financially successful
- **OTHERWISE** Investment is expected to destroy value and should be rejected
