# Accounting Equation Equality

## Statement

Total Assets must always equal Total Liabilities plus Total Shareholders' Equity at any point in time.

## Rationale

Fundamental principle of double-entry bookkeeping ensuring the balance sheet is always in balance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A balance sheet is prepared or the accounting equation is tested
- **THEN** Assets = Liabilities + Shareholders' Equity must hold true
- **OTHERWISE** There is an error in recording, classification, or posting of transactions
