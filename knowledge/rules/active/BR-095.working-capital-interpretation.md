# Working Capital Interpretation

## Statement

A positive working capital indicates the company has sufficient current assets to cover current liabilities. A negative working capital may signal liquidity problems.

## Rationale

Working capital is a key measure of short-term financial health.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating short-term liquidity
- **THEN** Compute working capital as current assets minus current liabilities
- **OTHERWISE** Inability to assess short-term debt-paying ability
