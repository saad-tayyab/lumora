# Capital Rationing Screening Rule

## Statement

In capital rationing, proposals are initially screened by minimum cash payback and average rate of return standards; those that pass are further analyzed with NPV and IRR methods.

## Rationale

Screening with simple methods quickly eliminates clearly unacceptable proposals before more complex (and costly) present value analysis is applied.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Allocating limited capital funds among competing proposals
- **THEN** Step 1: Screen by minimum payback and ARR standards → Step 2: Analyze survivors with NPV and IRR → Step 3: Consider qualitative factors → Step 4: Rank and fund
- **OTHERWISE** Evaluate all proposals directly with NPV/IRR
