# Credit Card Sale Recording

## Statement

For credit card sales, record the sale at the full sales price, then record the service fee as an expense and the net amount as cash received or accounts receivable from the clearing house.

## Rationale

Credit card companies charge a service fee for processing transactions. The seller receives the sales amount minus the fee.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Customer pays using a credit card
- **THEN** Record full sale, service fee expense, and net cash received
- **OTHERWISE** For cash sales, record the full amount as cash
