# Employee Withholding Requirements

## Statement

Employers must withhold federal income tax, Social Security tax (6.2%), and Medicare tax (1.45%) from employee earnings each pay period.

## Rationale

IRS requires employers to collect taxes at source to ensure compliance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** An employee is paid wages or salary
- **THEN** Withhold applicable taxes before issuing net pay
- **OTHERWISE** The employer faces penalties and interest
