# Strategy Map Causality

## Statement

Strategic objectives in different perspectives should be linked in a cause-and-effect relationship, typically flowing from learning and growth through internal processes and customer perspectives to financial outcomes.

## Rationale

Strategy maps help management understand how investments in employee training and process improvements ultimately drive financial results.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Creating a strategy map for the balanced scorecard
- **THEN** Define causal links between objectives across perspectives
- **OTHERWISE** The strategy map cannot illustrate how objectives influence each other
