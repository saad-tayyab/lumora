# Volume Variance Interpretation

## Statement

An unfavorable volume variance indicates production below normal capacity, while a favorable volume variance indicates production above normal capacity.

## Rationale

Volume variance measures utilization of fixed overhead resources like plant and equipment.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Actual production is less than normal capacity
- **THEN** Volume variance is unfavorable (unused capacity cost)
- **OTHERWISE** Volume variance is favorable (excess capacity utilization)
