# Errors Do Not Always Cause Trial Balance Imbalance

## Statement

A trial balance may still balance even if errors exist. Errors that do not affect the equality of debits and credits include: omitted transactions, duplicate posting, incorrect account used, or offsetting errors.

## Rationale

The trial balance only checks that debits equal credits, not that transactions are correctly recorded.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Trial balance balances but financial statements seem incorrect
- **THEN** Investigate for errors that don't affect debit/credit equality (omissions, duplications, wrong accounts, offsetting errors)
- **OTHERWISE** Errors may go undetected
