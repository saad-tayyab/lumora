# Materials Price Variance Responsibility

## Statement

The Purchasing Department is typically responsible for the direct materials price variance, as they negotiate prices and select suppliers.

## Rationale

Purchasing agents control supplier selection and price negotiations, making them accountable for price deviations from standard.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Materials price variance is identified
- **THEN** Report to Purchasing Department for investigation
- **OTHERWISE** If market-wide price increases, variance may be uncontrollable
