# AR Turnover Favorable Trend

## Statement

An increasing accounts receivable turnover ratio or decreasing days' sales in receivables indicates a favorable trend in collecting receivables.

## Rationale

Faster collection improves cash flow and reduces the risk of bad debts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing AR turnover or days' sales across periods
- **THEN** Higher turnover or fewer days' sales in receivables is favorable
- **OTHERWISE** Collection efficiency is deteriorating
