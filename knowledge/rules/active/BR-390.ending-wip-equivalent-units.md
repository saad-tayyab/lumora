# Ending WIP Equivalent Units

## Statement

Ending work in process equivalent units are computed by multiplying the whole units in ending inventory by the percentage of completion for materials and conversion separately.

## Rationale

Captures the work done in the current period on units not yet completed.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Ending WIP exists with given completion percentages
- **THEN** Equivalent units = Ending WIP whole units x Percent complete
- **OTHERWISE** No ending inventory
