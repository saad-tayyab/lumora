# Static Budget Limitation

## Statement

A static budget should not be used to evaluate cost control when actual activity differs from the budgeted activity level, as it does not adjust for volume changes.

## Rationale

Comparing actual costs at one activity level to budgeted costs at a different activity level produces misleading variance analysis.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Actual activity level differs from the static budget's planned activity level
- **THEN** Use a flexible budget for meaningful cost variance analysis
- **OTHERWISE** Cost comparisons are invalid and may lead to incorrect management decisions
