# Investing and Financing Sections Identical

## Statement

The 'Cash flows from (used for) investing activities' and 'Cash flows from (used for) financing activities' sections of the statement of cash flows are exactly the same under both the direct and indirect methods.

## Rationale

Only the presentation of operating activities differs between the two methods; investing and financing activities are reported identically.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing direct and indirect method statements
- **THEN** Investing and financing sections are identical in both methods
- **OTHERWISE** An error exists in one of the statements
