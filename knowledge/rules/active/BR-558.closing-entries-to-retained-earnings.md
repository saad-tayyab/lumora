# Closing Entries to Retained Earnings

## Statement

At period end, revenue and expense accounts are closed to Income Summary, which is then closed to Retained Earnings. Net income credits Retained Earnings; net loss debits Retained Earnings.

## Rationale

Closing entries transfer temporary account balances to permanent equity accounts to reset for the next period.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** At the end of an accounting period
- **THEN** Close all revenue and expense accounts; credit Retained Earnings for net income
- **OTHERWISE** Temporary accounts would accumulate balances across periods, violating the periodicity assumption
