# Cash Flows Classification

## Statement

Cash transactions must be classified into operating, investing, or financing activities for the statement of cash flows.

## Rationale

Provides insight into the sources and uses of cash, enabling stakeholders to assess the company's ability to generate future cash flows.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing the statement of cash flows
- **THEN** Classify each cash transaction as operating, investing, or financing
- **OTHERWISE** Statement of cash flows is incomplete and not useful for analysis
