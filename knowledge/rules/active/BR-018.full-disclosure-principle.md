# Full Disclosure Principle

## Statement

Financial statements and accompanying notes must disclose all information that would make a difference to financial statement users.

## Rationale

Users need complete information to make informed decisions about the company.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preparing financial statements
- **THEN** Disclose all material information in notes and reports
- **OTHERWISE** Users may make decisions based on incomplete information
