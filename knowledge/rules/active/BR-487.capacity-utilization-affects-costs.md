# Capacity Utilization Affects Costs

## Statement

When operating below full capacity, additional production may not increase fixed costs, making variable costs the relevant differential costs.

## Rationale

Fixed costs are already incurred; only variable costs change with additional production when excess capacity exists.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Company is operating below full capacity
- **THEN** Only variable manufacturing costs are differential for additional production decisions
- **OTHERWISE** Full manufacturing costs including fixed overhead are differential
