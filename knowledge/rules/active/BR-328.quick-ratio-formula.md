# Quick Ratio Formula

## Statement

The quick ratio is computed as quick assets (cash, temporary investments, and receivables) divided by current liabilities. It excludes inventories and prepaid expenses.

## Rationale

The quick ratio is a more stringent measure of liquidity because it excludes assets that may not be readily convertible to cash.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating a company's immediate liquidity position
- **THEN** Quick ratio = (Cash + Temporary investments + Receivables) / Current liabilities
- **OTHERWISE** The most liquid measure of short-term solvency is unknown
