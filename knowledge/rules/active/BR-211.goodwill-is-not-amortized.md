# Goodwill Is Not Amortized

## Statement

Goodwill is not amortized. Instead, a loss should be recorded if the future prospects of the purchased firm become impaired (fair value less than carrying value).

## Rationale

Goodwill has an indefinite useful life and is subject to impairment testing rather than systematic amortization.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Goodwill is carried on the balance sheet
- **THEN** Test for impairment periodically; do not amortize
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
