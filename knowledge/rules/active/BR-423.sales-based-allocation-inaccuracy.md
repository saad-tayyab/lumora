# Sales-Based Allocation Inaccuracy

## Statement

Allocating selling and administrative expenses based solely on sales dollars is inaccurate when products consume selling/admin activities in proportions different from their sales volumes.

## Rationale

Products with complex orders, returns, or special shipping needs consume more resources than sales-based allocation would suggest.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Products have significantly different selling/admin activity consumption patterns
- **THEN** Use ABC instead of sales-based allocation for more accurate product profitability
- **OTHERWISE** High-volume/simple products will be overcosted and low-volume/complex products undercosted
