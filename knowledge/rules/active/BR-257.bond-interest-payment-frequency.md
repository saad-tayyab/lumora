# Bond Interest Payment Frequency

## Statement

Bond interest is typically paid semiannually (every six months), with the payment equal to Face Amount × Contract Rate × ½ year.

## Rationale

Semiannual interest payments are the standard convention in the bond market.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A bond with semiannual interest is outstanding
- **THEN** Interest payments are made every six months
- **OTHERWISE** Other payment frequencies (annual, quarterly) may apply
