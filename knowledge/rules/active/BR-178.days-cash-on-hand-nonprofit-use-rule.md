# Days' Cash on Hand Nonprofit Use Rule

## Statement

Days' cash on hand is a popular measure used by nonprofits whose revenues are based on contributions. It reveals how long the nonprofit could survive if contributions dropped significantly.

## Rationale

Nonprofits with contribution-based revenue need to assess how long they can operate during funding downturns.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating a nonprofit's financial sustainability
- **THEN** Use days' cash on hand to assess survival capacity during revenue declines
- **OTHERWISE** May underestimate financial vulnerability
