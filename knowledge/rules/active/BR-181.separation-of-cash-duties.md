# Separation of Cash Duties

## Statement

The responsibilities for cash handling, record-keeping, and reconciliation must be separated among different individuals.

## Rationale

Prevents and detects fraud through the principle of checks and balances; no single person controls the entire cash cycle.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Cash is received or disbursed
- **THEN** Different individuals must handle, record, and reconcile
- **OTHERWISE** Increased vulnerability to fraud and undetected errors
