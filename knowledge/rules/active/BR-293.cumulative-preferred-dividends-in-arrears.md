# Cumulative Preferred Dividends in Arrears

## Statement

If cumulative preferred dividends are not paid in any year, they accumulate as dividends in arrears and must be paid before any common dividends.

## Rationale

Cumulative feature protects preferred stockholders from missed dividend payments.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Preferred stock is cumulative and dividends are not declared in a period
- **THEN** Unpaid dividends accumulate as dividends in arrears
- **OTHERWISE** Non-cumulative preferred stock does not accumulate unpaid dividends
