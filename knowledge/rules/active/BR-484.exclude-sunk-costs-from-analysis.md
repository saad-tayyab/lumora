# Exclude Sunk Costs from Analysis

## Statement

Sunk costs that have already been incurred and cannot be recovered must be excluded from differential analysis.

## Rationale

Sunk costs are the same regardless of which alternative is chosen, so they do not affect the differential decision.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Performing differential analysis
- **THEN** Exclude all sunk costs from the analysis
- **OTHERWISE** Including sunk costs would distort the decision
