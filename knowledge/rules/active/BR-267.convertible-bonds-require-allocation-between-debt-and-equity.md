# Convertible Bonds Require Allocation Between Debt and Equity

## Statement

Convertible bonds must be separated into a debt component (bonds payable) and an equity component (conversion option) at issuance, with proceeds allocated between them.

## Rationale

GAAP requires that compound financial instruments be separated into their constituent parts for proper financial reporting.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A bond includes a conversion feature
- **THEN** Allocate proceeds between debt and equity components
- **OTHERWISE** Treat as standard bonds payable
