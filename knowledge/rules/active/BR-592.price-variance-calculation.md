# Price Variance Calculation

## Statement

Price variance equals (actual price minus standard price) multiplied by actual quantity purchased.

## Rationale

Isolates the impact of price changes from quantity changes for variance analysis and cost control.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Calculating price variance for purchased inputs
- **THEN** Compute (AP - SP) x AQ
- **OTHERWISE** Price variance cannot be determined without actual and standard prices
