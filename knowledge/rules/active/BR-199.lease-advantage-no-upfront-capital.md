# Lease Advantage — No Upfront Capital

## Statement

Leasing allows a lessee to use an asset without spending funds or obtaining financing to purchase it, and may shift maintenance and obsolescence risk to the lessor.

## Rationale

Leasing provides operational flexibility and can be more cost-effective than purchasing for certain assets.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating acquisition alternatives for a long-lived asset
- **THEN** Consider leasing as an alternative to purchasing
- **OTHERWISE** May miss cost-saving or risk-mitigating opportunities
