# Bond Premium Amortization

## Statement

When bonds are issued at a premium, the premium must be amortized over the life of the bonds to reduce interest expense.

## Rationale

Matches the cost of borrowing with the periods that benefit from the borrowed funds.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Bonds are issued at a price above face value
- **THEN** Amortize the premium periodically using the effective interest method
- **OTHERWISE** Interest expense will be overstated
