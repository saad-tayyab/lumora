# LCM Valuation Rule

## Statement

Inventory must be reported at the lower of its historical cost or its market value (net realizable value). Any decline in market value below cost is included in cost of goods sold.

## Rationale

The conservatism principle requires that inventory not be overstated on the balance sheet, and losses from market declines be recognized in the period they occur.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Reporting inventory in the financial statements
- **THEN** Apply LCM and include any market decline in COGS
- **OTHERWISE** Inventory would be overstated and income would be overstated
