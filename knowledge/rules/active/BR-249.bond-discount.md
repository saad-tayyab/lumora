# Bond Discount

## Statement

When the contract rate is less than the market rate, the bond sells for less than face amount; the difference is recorded as Discount on Bonds Payable.

## Rationale

Investors pay less for bonds offering lower-than-market interest rates.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Contract rate < market rate at issuance
- **THEN** Debit Discount on Bonds Payable for the difference between face amount and proceeds
- **OTHERWISE** No discount is recorded
