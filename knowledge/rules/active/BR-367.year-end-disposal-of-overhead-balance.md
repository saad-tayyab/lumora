# Year-End Disposal of Overhead Balance

## Statement

The balance in Manufacturing Overhead at year-end should not be carried over to the next year. It is disposed of by transferring to Cost of Goods Sold.

## Rationale

Overhead estimates apply only to the current period's operations.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Year-end arrives with a balance in Manufacturing Overhead
- **THEN** Transfer the balance to Cost of Goods Sold
- **OTHERWISE** Costs from one period distort the next period's results
