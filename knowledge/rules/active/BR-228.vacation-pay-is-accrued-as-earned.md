# Vacation Pay Is Accrued as Earned

## Statement

The estimated cost of employee vacation pay should be accrued as an expense and liability during the period in which employees earn the vacation benefits, matching revenues and expenses.

## Rationale

Vacation pay represents compensation earned but not yet paid; it must be recorded in the period earned.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Employees earn vacation time during the period
- **THEN** Debit Vacation Pay Expense, credit Vacation Pay Payable
- **OTHERWISE** When employees take vacation, debit Vacation Pay Payable
