# Invoice Total Must Equal Sum of Line Items

## Statement

The invoice total amount must equal the sum of all line item amounts plus tax.

## Rationale

Ensures mathematical accuracy of the invoice and prevents billing errors.

## Conditions

- **WHEN an invoice is created or updated
- THEN totalAmount MUST equal sum(lineItems.amount) + sum(lineItems.taxAmount)
- OTHERWISE the invoice is rejected with error code `ERR_INVOICE_TOTAL_MISMATCH`**

## Pseudocode

```
function validateInvoiceTotal(invoice):
    lineTotal = sum(item.quantity * item.unitPrice for item in invoice.lineItems)
    taxTotal = sum(item.taxAmount for item in invoice.lineItems)
    expectedTotal = lineTotal + taxTotal
    if invoice.totalAmount != expectedTotal:
        throw InvoiceTotalMismatchError(invoice.id, invoice.totalAmount, expectedTotal)
```

## Exceptions

Rounding differences up to 0.01 are acceptable.
