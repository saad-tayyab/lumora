# Debit Increases Drawing

## Statement

The drawing account increases on the debit side because drawing decreases owner's equity.

## Rationale

Drawing reduces equity, so it follows the same pattern as expense accounts.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Owner withdraws assets
- **THEN** Record the increase on the debit side of the drawing account
- **OTHERWISE** Owner's equity will be overstated
