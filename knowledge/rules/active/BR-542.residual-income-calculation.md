# Residual Income Calculation

## Statement

Residual Income equals Operating Income minus the product of Minimum Rate of Return and Invested Assets.

## Rationale

Residual income measures the absolute dollar amount of income above the minimum required return, encouraging managers to accept any project with positive residual income.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating division performance with a focus on absolute profit contribution
- **THEN** Calculate Residual Income = Operating Income - (Minimum Rate of Return × Invested Assets)
- **OTHERWISE** Use ROI for relative performance comparison
