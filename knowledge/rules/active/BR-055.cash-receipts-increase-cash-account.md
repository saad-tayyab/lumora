# Cash Receipts Increase Cash Account

## Statement

All cash received by the business must be recorded as a debit to the Cash account.

## Rationale

Cash is an asset with a normal debit balance. Increases to assets are recorded as debits.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Cash is received from any source (customer payment, owner investment, loan proceeds)
- **THEN** Debit Cash for the amount received
- **OTHERWISE** Cash balance will be understated and the transaction incomplete
