# Periodic Trial Balance Preparation

## Statement

A trial balance should be prepared at the end of each accounting period to verify the equality of debits and credits.

## Rationale

Catches errors before financial statements are prepared and ensures the ledger is in balance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** End of an accounting period
- **THEN** Prepare an unadjusted trial balance before adjustments
- **OTHERWISE** Errors may go undetected into financial statements
