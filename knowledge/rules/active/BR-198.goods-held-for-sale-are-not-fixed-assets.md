# Goods Held for Sale Are Not Fixed Assets

## Statement

Items offered for sale as part of normal operations (e.g., cars at a dealership) are not classified as fixed assets, even if they are long-lived tangible assets.

## Rationale

The classification depends on whether the asset is used in operations or held for sale to customers.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating long-lived tangible items
- **THEN** Classify as inventory if held for sale in normal operations
- **OTHERWISE** Fixed assets and inventory are both misstated
