# Fixed Asset Turnover Measures Efficiency

## Statement

The fixed asset turnover ratio (Sales / Average Net Fixed Assets) measures how efficiently a company uses its fixed assets to generate sales.

## Rationale

Higher ratios indicate more efficient use of fixed assets in generating revenue.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating asset utilization efficiency
- **THEN** Compute fixed asset turnover and compare to industry peers
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot assess fixed asset efficiency
