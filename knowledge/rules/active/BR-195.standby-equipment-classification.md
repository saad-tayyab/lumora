# Standby Equipment Classification

## Statement

Equipment kept on standby for use during peak periods or when other equipment breaks down is classified as a fixed asset even if not used frequently, because it is held for use in normal operations.

## Rationale

The classification depends on intent to use in operations, not frequency of use.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating infrequently used equipment
- **THEN** Classify as fixed asset if held for operational use
- **OTHERWISE** Equipment may be incorrectly reclassified as an investment
