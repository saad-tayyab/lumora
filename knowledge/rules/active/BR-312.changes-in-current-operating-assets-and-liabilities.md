# Changes in Current Operating Assets and Liabilities

## Statement

Increases in current operating assets (e.g., accounts receivable, inventories) are subtracted from net income; decreases are added. Increases in current operating liabilities (e.g., accounts payable, accrued expenses) are added; decreases are subtracted.

## Rationale

Changes in working capital accounts reflect timing differences between accrual-based net income and actual cash flows.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Adjusting net income for changes in working capital under the indirect method
- **THEN** Asset increases are subtracted, asset decreases are added; liability increases are added, liability decreases are subtracted
- **OTHERWISE** Cash flows from operating activities will be incorrect
