# Current Portion of Long-Term Debt Is Current Liability

## Statement

The portion of long-term debt (such as installment notes) due within one year must be reported as a current liability on the balance sheet.

## Rationale

Current liabilities represent obligations due within one year; the near-term portion of long-term debt meets this criterion.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Long-term debt has payments due within the next 12 months
- **THEN** Classify the current-year portion as a current liability
- **OTHERWISE** Remaining balance is reported as a long-term liability
