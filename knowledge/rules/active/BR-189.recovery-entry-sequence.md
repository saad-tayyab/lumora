# Recovery Entry Sequence

## Statement

When recovering a previously written-off account under the allowance method: (1) reinstate the receivable by debiting Accounts Receivable and crediting Allowance for Doubtful Accounts, then (2) record the collection by debiting Cash and crediting Accounts Receivable.

## Rationale

Maintains a complete audit trail showing both the reversal of the write-off and the cash receipt.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Cash is received for an account previously written off
- **THEN** Two entries: (1) reinstate receivable, (2) record cash collection
- **OTHERWISE** Customer credit history is not properly restored and the audit trail is incomplete
