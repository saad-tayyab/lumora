# Service Business Overhead Allocation

## Statement

Service businesses allocate overhead to individual jobs based on an activity base such as direct labor hours, using a predetermined overhead rate.

## Rationale

Ensures each service job carries its fair share of indirect costs for accurate profitability analysis.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Service jobs incur overhead costs
- **THEN** Apply overhead using the predetermined rate and actual activity
- **OTHERWISE** Service job costs are understated and profitability is overstated
