# Petty Cash Replenishment

## Statement

When replenishing a petty cash fund, the check amount plus remaining cash must equal the established fund amount. Any discrepancy is recorded in the Cash Short and Over account.

## Rationale

Ensures the petty cash fund is maintained at its established level and all disbursements are properly recorded.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Replenishing a petty cash fund
- **THEN** Check amount + cash on hand = fund amount; record difference in Cash Short and Over
- **OTHERWISE** Fund balance will not match its established amount
