# Target Costing Market-Driven Rule

## Statement

Under target costing, the selling price is determined by the competitive market, and cost must be adjusted to achieve the desired profit margin within that price.

## Rationale

In competitive markets, companies cannot freely set prices; instead, they must manage costs to earn acceptable profits at market-determined prices.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A product operates in a competitive market with a well-defined price point
- **THEN** Target Cost = Market Selling Price - Desired Profit Margin; engineer costs down to meet the target
- **OTHERWISE** Use cost-plus pricing or other methods
