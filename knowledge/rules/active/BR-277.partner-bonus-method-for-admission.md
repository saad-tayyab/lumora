# Partner Bonus Method for Admission

## Statement

When a new partner invests assets in a partnership, a bonus may be calculated to ensure the new partner receives the agreed ownership percentage, with the bonus allocated to or from existing partners.

## Rationale

The bonus method adjusts capital accounts when the investment amount differs from the book value of the interest acquired.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** New partner invests and receives a different ownership percentage than the proportional investment suggests
- **THEN** Calculate bonus as difference between investment and capital credit; allocate per agreement
- **OTHERWISE** No bonus adjustment needed
