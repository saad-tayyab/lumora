# Sales Returns and Discounts Estimation

## Statement

At the time of revenue recognition, companies must estimate the most likely amount of variable consideration (returns, discounts, claims) and record an offsetting reserve against revenue.

## Rationale

Conservative accounting requires anticipating known risks to avoid overstating revenue.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** A revenue transaction includes variable consideration (return rights, discount programs, claims)
- **THEN** Estimate the most likely amount and reduce revenue with an offsetting accrued liability
- **OTHERWISE** If actual returns differ from estimates, adjust net revenue in the period the difference is determined
