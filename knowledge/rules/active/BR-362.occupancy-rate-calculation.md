# Occupancy Rate Calculation

## Statement

Occupancy Rate = Guest Nights / Available Room Nights. Guest Nights = sum of (Guests × Nights per Visit). Available Room Nights = Number of Rooms × Days in Period.

## Rationale

Measures capacity utilization in the hospitality industry.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Evaluating hotel capacity utilization
- **THEN** Calculate occupancy rate using guest nights and available room nights
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot assess performance relative to capacity
