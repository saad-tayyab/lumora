# Department-Specific Scorecards

## Statement

Organizations with firm-wide balanced scorecards may need to create unique, department-specific scorecards to ensure metrics are relevant to each department's specific responsibilities.

## Rationale

Different departments have different strategic priorities and operational focuses, requiring tailored metrics to effectively manage performance.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Managing performance across multiple departments
- **THEN** Create department-specific scorecards with metrics relevant to each department's objectives
- **OTHERWISE** Firm-wide metrics may not accurately reflect department-level performance
