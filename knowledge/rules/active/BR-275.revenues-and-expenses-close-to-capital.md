# Revenues and Expenses Close to Capital

## Statement

At the end of each accounting period, revenues and expenses are closed to the partners' capital accounts, with each partner's share of net income credited to their capital account.

## Rationale

This is part of the closing process that transfers net income to equity, updating capital accounts to reflect the period's results.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** End of the accounting period
- **THEN** Close revenues and expenses; credit each partner's capital for their share of net income
- **OTHERWISE** Income would not be reflected in equity
