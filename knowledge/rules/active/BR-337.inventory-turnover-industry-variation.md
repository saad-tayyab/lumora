# Inventory Turnover Industry Variation

## Statement

What is considered a good inventory turnover varies by type of inventory, company, and industry.

## Rationale

Grocery stores have higher turnover than jewelers; perishable foods have higher turnover than cleansers.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Interpreting inventory turnover ratio
- **THEN** Compare to industry averages and company-specific benchmarks
- **OTHERWISE** Misleading conclusions about inventory management
