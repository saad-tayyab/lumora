# Markup Percentage Calculation

## Statement

Markup percentage equals (Desired Profit + Total Selling and Administrative Expenses) divided by Total Product (Manufacturing) Costs.

## Rationale

The markup must cover non-manufacturing costs and provide the desired return on investment.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Using product cost method for pricing
- **THEN** Calculate markup as (Desired Profit + S&A Expenses) / Total Product Costs
- **OTHERWISE** Selling price will not cover all costs
