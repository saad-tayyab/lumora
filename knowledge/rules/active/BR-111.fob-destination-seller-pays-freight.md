# FOB Destination - Seller Pays Freight

## Statement

When merchandise is sold FOB destination, the seller pays freight costs and debits Delivery Expense (a selling expense).

## Rationale

Ownership passes at the destination, so the seller bears the freight cost as a selling expense.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Merchandise is sold with FOB destination terms
- **THEN** Seller records freight costs in Delivery Expense
- **OTHERWISE** Selling expenses are understated
