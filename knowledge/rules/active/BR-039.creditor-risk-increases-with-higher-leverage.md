# Creditor Risk Increases with Higher Leverage

## Statement

When the ratio of liabilities to stockholders' equity increases from one period to the next, creditor risk has increased.

## Rationale

A higher ratio means the business relies more heavily on debt financing, increasing the risk to creditors.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Comparing liability-to-equity ratios across periods
- **THEN** An increase in the ratio indicates increased creditor risk; a decrease indicates decreased risk
- **OTHERWISE** Risk assessment would be inaccurate
