# Manufacturing Overhead Cost per Unit

## Statement

Manufacturing Overhead Cost per Unit = Total Manufacturing Overhead Cost / Total Units of Estimated Production

## Rationale

Converts total allocated overhead to a per-unit cost for product costing and pricing.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Determining per-unit manufacturing overhead cost under ABC
- **THEN** Divide the product's total allocated overhead by the estimated number of units
- **OTHERWISE** Cannot determine accurate per-unit product cost
