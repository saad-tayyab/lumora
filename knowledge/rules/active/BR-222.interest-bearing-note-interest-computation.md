# Interest-Bearing Note Interest Computation

## Statement

Interest on an interest-bearing note is computed as Face Amount × Interest Rate × (Term in Days / 365). The interest expense is reported in the 'Other expense' section of the income statement.

## Rationale

Interest represents the cost of borrowing and must be properly allocated to the period in which the funds are used.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Paying an interest-bearing note at maturity
- **THEN** Record Interest Expense for the computed interest amount
- **OTHERWISE** N/A
