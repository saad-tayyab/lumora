# Unequal Cash Flows Payback Calculation

## Statement

When annual net cash inflows are not equal, the cash payback period is determined by cumulating annual net cash inflows until the cumulative total equals the initial cost.

## Rationale

Unequal cash flows require year-by-year accumulation to determine when the investment is fully recovered.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Annual net cash inflows vary across years
- **THEN** Add annual cash flows sequentially; payback period is the year when cumulative total first equals or exceeds initial cost
- **OTHERWISE** Use the simple formula: Payback Period = Initial Cost / Annual Net Cash Inflow
