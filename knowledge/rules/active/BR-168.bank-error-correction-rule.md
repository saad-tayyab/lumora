# Bank Error Correction Rule

## Statement

Bank errors should be added to or deducted from the bank balance in the reconciliation, and the bank should be notified so it can correct the error.

## Rationale

The bank needs to correct its records, and the reconciliation adjusts for the error in the interim.

## Conditions

- **WHEN** Bank makes an error on the bank statement
- **THEN** Adjust bank section of reconciliation and notify bank
- **OTHERWISE** Bank statement balance will not reconcile with company records
