# Straight-Line Method (Depreciation)

## Definition

A method of depreciation that provides for equal periodic depreciation expense over the estimated life of a fixed asset.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| initial_cost | Money | Cost of the asset |
| residual_value | Money | Estimated value at end of life |
| useful_life | number | Estimated useful life in periods |
