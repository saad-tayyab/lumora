# Commercial Substance

## Definition

A characteristic of an asset exchange indicating that future cash flows change as a result of the exchange. If an exchange has commercial substance, gains and losses are recognized.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| has_commercial_substance | boolean | Whether the exchange changes future cash flows |
