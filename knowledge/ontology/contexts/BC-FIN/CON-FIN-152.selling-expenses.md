# Selling Expenses

## Definition

Operating expenses incurred directly in the selling of merchandise, such as sales salaries, store supplies used, depreciation of store equipment, delivery expense, and advertising.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| examples | list | Sales salaries, advertising, depreciation of store equipment, delivery expense, miscellaneous selling expense |
| classification | string | Subcategory of operating expenses on multiple-step income statement |
