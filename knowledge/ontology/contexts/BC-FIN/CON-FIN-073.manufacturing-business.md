# Manufacturing Business

## Definition

A business that uses labor, plant, and equipment to convert raw materials into new finished products.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| revenue_source | string | Revenue from manufactured product sales |
| raw_materials | boolean | Converts raw materials to finished goods |
