# Cost of Finished Goods Available for Sale

## Definition

The sum of beginning finished goods inventory and cost of goods manufactured, representing the total cost of goods that could have been sold during the period.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| formula | string | Available for Sale = Beginning Finished Goods + Cost of Goods Manufactured |
