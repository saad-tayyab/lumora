# Investing Activities

## Definition

The section of the statement of cash flows that reports cash inflows and outflows related to changes in a company's long-term assets, such as purchasing or selling property, plant, and equipment.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| cash_received_from_sale_of_assets | decimal | Cash inflows from selling long-term assets |
| cash_paid_for_purchase_of_assets | decimal | Cash outflows for acquiring long-term assets |
