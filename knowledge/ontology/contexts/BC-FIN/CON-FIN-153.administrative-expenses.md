# Administrative Expenses

## Definition

Operating expenses incurred in the administration or general operations of the business, such as office salaries, depreciation of office equipment, and office supplies used.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| examples | list | Office salaries, depreciation of office equipment, office supplies expense, insurance expense, rent expense, miscellaneous administrative expense |
| classification | string | Subcategory of operating expenses on multiple-step income statement |
