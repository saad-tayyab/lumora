# Noncash Investing and Financing Activities

## Definition

Transactions involving investing and financing activities that do not directly affect cash but indirectly affect future cash flows, such as issuing common stock to retire long-term debt. These are reported in a separate schedule at the bottom of the statement of cash flows.

| description | string | Description of the noncash transaction |
| amount | decimal | Dollar amount of the noncash transaction |
