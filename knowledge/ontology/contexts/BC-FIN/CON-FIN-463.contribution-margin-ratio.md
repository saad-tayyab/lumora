# Contribution Margin Ratio

## Definition

The ratio of contribution margin to sales revenue, expressed as a percentage. Indicates what percentage of each sales dollar is available to cover fixed costs and profit.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| formula | string | Contribution Margin / Sales Revenue |
| percentage | decimal | Contribution margin as a percentage of sales |
