# Internal Control

## Definition

The procedures and processes used by a company to safeguard its assets, process information accurately, and ensure compliance with laws and regulations.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| safeguard_assets | boolean | Prevent theft, fraud, misuse, or misplacement of assets |
| accurate_information | boolean | Ensure business information is processed accurately |
| compliance | boolean | Ensure compliance with laws, regulations, and financial reporting standards |
