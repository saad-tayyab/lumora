# Current Portion of Long-Term Debt

## Definition

The portion of long-term debt (such as installment notes) that is due within the next 12 months. Reported as a current liability on the balance sheet.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| amount_due_within_one_year | decimal | Principal portion of long-term debt due in the next 12 months |
