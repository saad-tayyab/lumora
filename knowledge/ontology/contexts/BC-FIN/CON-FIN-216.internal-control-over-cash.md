# Internal Control over Cash

## Definition

A system of procedures designed to safeguard cash, ensure accurate records, and promote efficient operations. Includes separation of duties, daily deposits, reconciliation, and proper authorization.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| separation_of_duties | Boolean | Cash handling and record-keeping performed by different individuals |
| daily_deposit | Boolean | All cash receipts deposited daily |
| reconciliation | Boolean | Daily comparison of deposit slips with bank receipts |
