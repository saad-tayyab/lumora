# Trade-In Allowance

## Definition

The amount a seller allows a buyer for old equipment traded in when acquiring new equipment of similar use. This amount may be greater or less than the book value of the old equipment.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| amount | decimal | The fair market value credit given for the old asset |
