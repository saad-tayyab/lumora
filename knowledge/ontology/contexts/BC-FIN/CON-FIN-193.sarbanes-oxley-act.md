# Sarbanes-Oxley Act

## Definition

A U.S. federal law enacted to maintain public confidence and trust in financial reporting. It requires publicly held companies to maintain effective internal controls over financial reporting and to report on the effectiveness of those controls.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| scope | string | Applies to publicly held companies (stock traded on public exchanges) |
| section_404 | string | Requires management assessment and external auditor attestation of internal control effectiveness |
| purpose | string | Maintain public confidence in financial reporting |
