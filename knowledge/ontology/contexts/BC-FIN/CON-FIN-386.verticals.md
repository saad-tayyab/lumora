# Verticals

## Definition

Organizational divisions within a company structured as separate businesses that develop products sold directly to customers and prepare their own income statements (P&L statements).

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| purpose | string | Develop and sell products to customers |
| reporting | string | Prepare their own income statements (P&L statements) |
