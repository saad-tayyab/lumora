# Merchandising Business

## Definition

A business that purchases merchandise from other companies and resells it to customers, rather than producing or manufacturing products itself.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| business_type | enum | Wholesaler or Retailer |
| operating_cycle | description | Includes purchasing merchandise before selling to customers |
