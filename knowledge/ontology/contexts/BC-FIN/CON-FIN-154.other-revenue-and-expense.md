# Other Revenue and Expense

## Definition

Revenue and expense items not related to the primary operations of the business, such as interest revenue, rent revenue, and interest expense.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| examples | list | Interest revenue, rent revenue, interest expense, gains/losses on asset sales |
| reporting | string | Offset against each other below operating income on multiple-step income statement |
