# Equity Method

## Definition

An accounting method for equity investments of 20%-50% ownership where the investor records its share of the investee's net income as an increase to the investment account and its share of dividends as a decrease. Investments are reported as noncurrent assets and not adjusted to fair value.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| initial_recording | string | Recorded at initial cost |
| income_recognition | string | Investor records share of investee's net income |
| dividend_treatment | string | Dividends reduce the investment account |
| fair_value_adjustment | string | Not adjusted to fair value |
