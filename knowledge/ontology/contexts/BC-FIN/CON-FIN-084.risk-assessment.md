# Risk Assessment

## Definition

The process of identifying risks, analyzing their significance, assessing their likelihood of occurring, and taking necessary actions to minimize them. One of the five elements of internal control.

## Attributes

| Attribute | Type | Description |
|-----------|------|-------------|
| id | UUID v7 | Unique identifier |
| risk_identification | string | Identify changes in customer requirements, competitive threats, regulatory changes, economic factors |
| risk_analysis | string | Analyze significance and likelihood of risks occurring |
| risk_mitigation | string | Take actions to minimize identified risks |
