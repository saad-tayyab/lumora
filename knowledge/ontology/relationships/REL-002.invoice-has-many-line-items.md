# Invoice has many Line Items

## Definition

An invoice contains one or more line items, each representing a product or service being billed. Line items are children of the Invoice aggregate and cannot exist independently.

## Source

- **CON-AR-002** (Invoice) — Aggregate root
- **CON-AR-003** (InvoiceLineItem) — Child entity

## Constraints

- An invoice must have at least one line item.
- Line items are deleted when the parent invoice is deleted (cascade).
