# VendorPayment pays Bills

## Description

A vendor payment can pay one or multiple bills. Each bill can be partially or fully paid by one or multiple payments.

## Source

- CON-AP-002 (VendorPayment) — aggregate

## Target

- CON-AP-003 (Bill) — aggregate

## Notes

- Payment-to-bill mapping is tracked via line items on the payment.
- A bill is fully paid when total payments equal the bill amount.
