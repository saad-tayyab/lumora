# Invoice has many Payment Applications

## Definition

An invoice can receive multiple payment applications over time as partial or full payments are made. Each payment application records how much of a payment was applied to this specific invoice.

## Source

- **CON-AR-002** (Invoice) — Parent aggregate
- **CON-AR-005** (PaymentApplication) — Linking entity

## Constraints

- The sum of all payment applications for an invoice must not exceed the invoice total.
- An invoice with zero balance due should have no further applications.
