# Payment has many Payment Applications

## Definition

A single payment can be applied across multiple invoices. Each payment application records how much of the payment was allocated to a specific invoice.

## Source

- **CON-AR-004** (Payment) — Parent aggregate
- **CON-AR-005** (PaymentApplication) — Linking entity

## Constraints

- The sum of all payment applications for a payment must equal the payment amount.
- A payment with no applications is considered unapplied (on-account).
