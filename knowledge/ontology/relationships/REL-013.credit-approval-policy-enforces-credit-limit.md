# CreditApprovalPolicy enforces CreditLimit

## Definition

The CreditApprovalPolicy evaluates the customer's current outstanding balance against their CreditLimit before allowing new invoices. If the limit would be exceeded, the invoice requires manual approval.

## Source

- **CON-AR-016** (CreditApprovalPolicy) — Policy
- **CON-AR-008** (CreditLimit) — Value object

## Constraints

- The policy must be evaluated before any invoice is finalized.
- BR-003: Payment terms (including credit limits) are defined per customer.
