# Customer has one Credit Limit

## Definition

Each customer may have a credit limit value object that defines the maximum outstanding balance they are allowed. The credit limit is optional — customers without a credit limit may have unlimited credit (subject to policy).

## Source

- **CON-AR-001** (Customer) — Parent entity
- **CON-AR-008** (CreditLimit) — Value object

## Constraints

- A customer can have at most one active credit limit at a time.
- The credit limit can be updated through a credit review process.
