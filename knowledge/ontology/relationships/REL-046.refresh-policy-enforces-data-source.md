# REL-046: DataRefreshPolicy enforces DataSource

## Definition

The DataRefreshPolicy enforces how and when DataSources are refreshed, ensuring data freshness while respecting resource constraints.

## Constraints

- Policy must belong to the same tenant as the DataSource
- Policy must be active to apply
