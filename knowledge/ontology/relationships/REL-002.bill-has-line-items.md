# Bill has many BillLineItems

## Description

Each vendor bill contains one or more line items representing the individual goods or services being billed.

## Source

- CON-AP-003 (Bill) — one side

## Target

- CON-AP-004 (BillLineItem) — many side

## Notes

- A bill must have at least one line item.
- Line item amounts roll up to the bill total.
