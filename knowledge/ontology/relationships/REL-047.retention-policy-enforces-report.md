# REL-047: ReportRetentionPolicy enforces Report

## Definition

The ReportRetentionPolicy enforces how long generated Reports and their exports are retained before automatic cleanup.

## Constraints

- Policy must belong to the same tenant as the Report
- Policy must be active to apply
