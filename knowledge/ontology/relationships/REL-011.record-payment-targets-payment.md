# RecordPayment targets Payment

## Definition

The RecordPayment command is the intent to create a new Payment aggregate. When executed, it validates the payment details, creates PaymentApplication records, and updates invoice balances.

## Source

- **CON-AR-014** (RecordPayment) — Command
- **CON-AR-004** (Payment) — Target aggregate

## Constraints

- The sum of applied amounts must not exceed the payment amount.
- CTR-AR-004: Applied amounts cannot exceed invoice balances.
