# Customer has many Credit Notes

## Definition

A customer can have multiple credit notes issued over time for returns, allowances, or corrections. Each credit note belongs to exactly one customer.

## Source

- **CON-AR-001** (Customer) — Parent entity
- **CON-AR-006** (CreditNote) — Child entity

## Constraints

- A credit note must reference a valid, active customer.
- Credit notes can be in any status (Draft, Issued, Applied, Voided).
