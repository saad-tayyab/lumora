# Bill has one PaymentSchedule

## Description

Each vendor bill has exactly one payment schedule defining the terms and due date for payment.

## Source

- CON-AP-003 (Bill) — one side

## Target

- CON-AP-005 (PaymentSchedule) — one side

## Notes

- Payment schedule is derived from vendor terms or explicitly set.
- Schedule can be recalculated if terms change before approval.
