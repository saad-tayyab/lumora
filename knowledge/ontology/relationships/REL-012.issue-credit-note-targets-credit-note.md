# IssueCreditNote targets CreditNote

## Definition

The IssueCreditNote command is the intent to create and issue a new CreditNote. When executed, it validates the reason and amount, creates the credit note, and emits a CreditNoteIssued event.

## Source

- **CON-AR-015** (IssueCreditNote) — Command
- **CON-AR-006** (CreditNote) — Target entity

## Constraints

- The credit note amount must be positive (CTR-AR-003).
