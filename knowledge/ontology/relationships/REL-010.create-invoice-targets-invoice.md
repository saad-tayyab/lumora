# CreateInvoice targets Invoice

## Definition

The CreateInvoice command is the intent to create a new Invoice aggregate. When executed, it validates business rules and creates the Invoice with its line items.

## Source

- **CON-AR-013** (CreateInvoice) — Command
- **CON-AR-002** (Invoice) — Target aggregate

## Constraints

- The command must pass CreditApprovalPolicy (CON-AR-016) before the invoice can be created.
- BR-003: Payment terms are defined per customer and determine the due_date.
